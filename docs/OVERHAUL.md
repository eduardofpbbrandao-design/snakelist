# SnakeList overhaul (brief of 11 Nov 2026)

One checklist for the whole brief. Every item says what kind of issue it is:
**D** = reproduced defect · **I** = data or wording inconsistency · **R** = requested design change ·
**P** = proposed improvement · **U** = unverified concern (needs more testing).
Status: ⏳ not started · 🔧 in progress · ✅ done and tested · ⚠️ done in part (see note) · ⛔ blocked.

Workstreams: **A** 3D viewer · **B** species pages, taxonomy, species data · **C** places (country/region/city pages, counts, habitats) · **D** app map and sightings · **E** whole-app visual system and supporting flows.

Environment limits: iNaturalist, Wikipedia, Wikidata, GBIF and the Reptile Database cannot be reached from the build machine (web search works). Features that fetch from them at run time are tested with recorded/mock responses and say so here.

- Lead: map test tapped a dot below the window after the merge (test fixed to scroll first); tests/sightings-empty-search.js now takes $PORT. Results on the merged build: map-sightings 61/61, empty-search 10/10.
- D: a dot under the Sightings status bar cannot be tapped (bar on top) - fixed: the bar and Options sit just above the map (same look); in full screen they stay on the map but only their buttons take taps. tests/sightings-bar.js with tests/fixtures/inat-sightings.json (desktop + phone, two zooms, full screen, no-photo record, quick switching).
- B: Malpolon monspessulanus was also missing its North African range - fixed. Natrix astreptophora and Hemorrhois hippocrepis now have Morocco, Algeria and Tunisia; H. hippocrepis's Italian populations (Sardinia, Pantelleria) are introduced (Faraone et al. 2020), not native. Summary, map, country pages and place-counts agree (checked for these two, Natrix maura and Malpolon).
- B: Bitis gabonica Red List category conflicting in sources (VU vs NT) - shown as not confirmed.
- Lead: region/city pages said introduced species were "not counted" while counting them - now tagged "introduced" in the list and the note says so.
- C: fossil ages under 1 million years were shown 1,000x too young (Crotalus potterensis "about 20 years ago") - fixed.
- C: some big cities fell in the wrong region (Addis Ababa -> Oromia) - fixed at the root: cities were placed only among regions with species records, so a city whose own unit had none (Brussels, Bogota, Islamabad, Tunis, Busan...) went to a neighbour; a same-name shape also lost to the region around it. Now every map shape is looked at, a nearby shape with the city's own name wins, and a city whose unit has no records gets no region (about 60 city pages that were in the wrong region are gone). Same rule in the app's cityArea(). Chartered/metropolitan/municipal cities typed as such.
- C: the map data's "Addis Ababa" shape is wrong (its outline lies in southern Ethiopia) and Buenos Aires city/province and Lima city/region are merged into one region page - not fixed (geo data).
- Firestore rules in the repo (firestore.rules) only allow users/{uid}; the Cameras layer writes to cams/ - either the deployed rules differ or adding a camera fails in production. Not changed here (needs the Firebase console).
- E: "Harmless" filter renamed "Low risk" (non-venomous is not a promise a snake cannot bite).
- E: About page photo total now computed from the photo index (was "about 3,100 of the 4,254").

## Simplification round (Eduardo's review)
- Sightings: one button (muted teal when on), one set of same-size dots, numbered groups that zoom in or list. No bar, Options, Load more, dot key or Cameras button; the newest 600 in view load by themselves. One compact card: the sighting's own photo (opened only once it has loaded), name, date, observer, "View original observation", and More details (photo credit, licence, place, identification). "No photo available for this sighting." Heatmap's own switch now says "Where seen".
- 3D: a small "3D" button in the map's bottom-right corner only turns 3D mode on/off (no panel). With it on, a species row opens that snake's box over the map, with a 3D action when a model exists; the model opens in the map area with "Back to map" (zoom, place and box kept).
- Also: no "+ N fossil or extinct" line under the map; the countries/territories counts are plain text again; Antarctica's hover label said "World Map" (now "Antarctica"); "Clear" sits next to "Close"; the Heatmap map style has no subtitle.
