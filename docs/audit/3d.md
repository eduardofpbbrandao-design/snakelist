# 3D snake realism audit (v6.35, preview "3d")

Request (Eduardo): the 3D snakes "still look like worms with eyes". Goal: real shape and anatomy improvements through the
shared modelling system, plus corrections by body-plan group, without breaking performance. Everything that changes how
snakes look is behind the **"3d" preview** (`tools/previews.js`; Settings > Previews, or `?preview=3d`). With the preview
off the models are exactly as before.

## Inventory

`data/3d-inventory.json` (made by `python3 tools/3d_inventory.py`, which reads `species/3d/models.json` and the tables in
`species/3d/viewer.js`, so the two cannot drift apart) lists every one of the 4,243 species: body plan, group, whether it
was checked by hand, whether it takes the shared improvements, and what it still needs.

Honest status:

- **331 individually checked**: a hand-made profile with cited references, or a head/look set by hand against photos
  (`CURATED`, `HEAD_SET`, `SPECIES_LOOK`, and now `REAL_LOOK`). Even these were checked mainly for colour, pattern and head
  shape; few were compared from more than one angle.
- **2,664 photo-checked on contact sheets only**: colours and pattern were read from their photo and looked at in bulk
  (v6.13 audit). Their *shape* has never been looked at species by species: it comes from their group's shared shape.
- **1,248 not checked against any photo** (no usable photo: mostly blind snakes, little-known colubrids, fossils).
- 988 have guessed colours; about 3,850 use a typical length for their group rather than a measured one; 169 are fossils
  (those without a real photo show "3D Reconstruction Unavailable").

## What the preview changes (species/3d/viewer.js `realism`, `REAL`, `REAL_LOOK`)

Found by rendering the validation species from the same cameras before and after:

1. **Body as a cone.** The width rose slowly from a thin neck to its maximum at 42-50% of the length, so the forebody of
   every snake was a long thin cone (a ball python was at less than half its girth 20% along). Real snakes are close to
   full girth a fifth to a quarter of the way along and keep it to the vent. Each group now has a forebody control point
   (`body.fore`): vipers, rattlesnakes and pythons fill out by ~20%, racers by ~26%, vine snakes by ~30%, blind snakes
   almost at once. The neck now reads as a neck between head and body.
2. **Tails.** A group taper when the profile names none: abrupt in vipers and pit vipers, a thick prehensile tail in
   pythons and boas, a long whip in racers, mambas and vine snakes, blunt in burrowers and sand boas. The very tip is
   rounded off (`body.tipRound`) instead of ending in a needle.
3. **Stouter or slimmer by group** (`ld` x0.95-1.04, head and neck kept at their real size), sea snakes 10% flatter from
   side to side.
4. **Head detail.** The jawline crease 1.4-1.6x deeper (both the plain head and the sculpted High-detail head), the brow
   ridge over the eye 1.2-1.4x, eye sockets 10-25% deeper so the eye sits in the head. Vine snakes (Ahaetulla, Oxybelis,
   Thelotornis...) get a longer head with a drawn-out pointed snout; generated hognoses an upturned shovel snout; burrowers
   and blind snakes smaller eyes.
5. **Eyes and skin.** The spectacle's reflections 28% weaker (they flashed white); the gloss value 16% lower and the gloss
   coat 20% weaker (less plastic), except in blind snakes, which really are glassy. Pupil shapes were already per genus
   (`PUPIL_GENUS`: vertical slits in vipers, pythons, cat snakes...; horizontal keyholes in vine snakes; round elsewhere).
6. **Posture.** A grounded snake in the resting S-pose holds its head and neck a little off the ground (about one head
   height over four head lengths) instead of laying its head flat in line with the body; not in burrowers, sea snakes,
   sand boas, coiled, rearing or tree poses.
7. **Inland Taipan** (`REAL_LOOK`): narrower, less bulbous head (width 0.8 of the body, depth 0.64) on a slimmer neck
   (head about 1.2x the neck), medium-slender body, dark-brown iris so the round pupil shows, slightly less gloss. Its
   dark glossy head cap, brown-olive body and dark-edged scales were already there.
8. **Smooth Green Snake** (`REAL_LOOK`): a longer, narrower head, a paler yellow-white belly, satin rather than wet
   gloss, dark iris with the round black pupil showing; it takes the racer group's slender forebody and whip tail.

## Live fixes (not behind the preview)

- **Touch instructions**: the viewer chose its instructions by screen width only, so iPads and other tablets were told
  "W A S D or right-drag" and "scroll". Now a touch-first device (coarse pointer and no fine one, or touch with no hover)
  gets "drag / two fingers / pinch / double-tap", and the Anatomy and Compare-photo hints say "Tap" and "Pinch".

## Checked and found already correct (no change made)

- **Contact.** Measured along the body (belly height over the ground or over the turn of the body under it, in body
  radii) for taipan, smooth green snake, rattlesnake, ball python and blind snake, preview on and off: no floating and no
  sinking anywhere past the neck (worst gap 0.01 radii). The rattlesnake's neck rises over its coil on purpose.
- **Size vs person**: already a sculpted 1.70 m person, labelled "Person 1.70 m", and the caption says "Typical adult
  length ... (source ...)".
- **Anatomy**: already says "A simplified model: organs at their usual places ..., not to exact scale".
- **Disposal**: closing the viewer leaves no geometries or textures in the renderer (checked in the new test).

## Tests

- `tests/viewer3d-realism.js` (new): preview off leaves the profile untouched; preview on gives each validation species its
  group, an early full forebody, a head wider than the neck, the right tail; contact with the ground or coils; touch
  instructions and toolbar fit on an iPad-sized and a phone-sized touch screen; close frees GPU memory.
- Results (SwiftShader, own server): viewer3d-realism 46/46; data-safety 68/68 before, 69/69 after; viewer3d-scene
  17/18 before and after (the same timing check, "plays again", fails on this slow software renderer both times).
- `node` check: all 4,243 profiles build their body functions with the preview on (no NaN), and the JS and Python group
  assignments agree for every species.

## Still to do (honest)

- The shape of ~3,900 species comes only from their group; none of them has been looked at one by one.
- Scale texture repetition along the body is still visible up close on long plain snakes.
- Arboreal grip: tree snakes lie on their branch but do not wrap or grip it; aquatic snakes were not re-measured on water.
- Open mouth, See it eat prey sizing and the anatomy organ proportions were not changed in this pass.
- Weak devices (Light quality) still get the plain head without the sculpted scales; the preview's shape changes apply
  there too, but the brow and lip relief of the sculpted head does not.
