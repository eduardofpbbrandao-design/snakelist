Striped House Snake

Boaedon lineatus

Striped House Snake (Boaedon lineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 15 more.

Where the name comes from

lineatus: ‘lined’

Striped House Snake: ‘Striped’ – its stripes; ‘House Snake’ – it is often found near houses.
