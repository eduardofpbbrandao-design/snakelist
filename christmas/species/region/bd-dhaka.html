Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Dhaka Division in particular. The other 6 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Buff Striped Keelback Amphiesma stolatum: Well-watered lowland plains and hills. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Chequered Keelback Fowlea piscator: In or near fresh water and rice paddies; active by day and by night. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Daudin's Bronzeback Dendrelaphis tristis: Many kinds of forest, and even gardens and parks in towns. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia: Prefers places near water - rice paddies, swamps and mangroves - but also grassland, scrub, forest, farmland and even cities, up to about 1,000 m. Source: Wikipedia.
