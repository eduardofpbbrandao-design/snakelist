Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Western Australia in particular. The other 73 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Carpet Python Morelia spilota: A wide range of habitats, from rainforest in north-eastern Queensland to river woodlands and forests elsewhere. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Children's Python Antaresia childreni: Forest, savanna, shrubland, desert, grassland and wetlands; often in caves. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Eastern Brown Snake Pseudonaja textilis: Most habitats except dense forest, including grassland, woodland, scrub and especially farmland and the outskirts of cities. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Green Tree Snake Dendrelaphis punctulatus: Bushland, well-vegetated banks of rivers and creeks, rainforest edges and gardens with ponds. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Mainland Tiger Snake Notechis scutatus: Often near water - wetlands, creeks, swamps and coastal areas - but also grassland and forest. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Mair's Keelback Tropidonophis mairii: Forest, grassland and freshwater wetlands. Source: Wikipedia.
