Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Beja in particular. The other 2 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Horseshoe Whip Snake Hemorrhois hippocrepis: Mediterranean scrub, rocky places and shores, farmland, pasture, plantations, gardens and towns. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Iberian Grass Snake Natrix astreptophora: Slow-flowing and still fresh water - streams, rivers, ponds and their banks - usually in the shallows. In Iberian rivers it is more numerous where there are no invasive fish. Source: Fishipedia, Wikipedia.
  • Southern Smooth Snake Coronella girondica: Forests, Mediterranean scrub, rocky places, pasture and plantations. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Viperine Snake Natrix maura: Closely tied to fresh water: rivers, streams, ponds, lakes, irrigation channels and other wetlands, where it hunts in the water and basks on the banks. Source: U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Wikipedia.
