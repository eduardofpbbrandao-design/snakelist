Where the name comes from

cantoris: named after Theodore Cantor, a Danish doctor and zoologist in India

Günther's Sea Snake: ‘Günther's’ – named after Albert Günther, a German-born British zoologist at the British Museum; ‘Sea Snake’ – it lives in the sea.
