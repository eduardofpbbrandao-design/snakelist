Mexican Bull Snake

Pituophis deppei (Duméril, 1853)

The Mexican Bullsnake (Mexican Pine Snake) is a large, harmless snake found only in Mexico.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Big square dark blotches on a yellowish-tan body.[1]

Appearance

Yellowish tan with large, squarish dark brown to black blotches and smaller dark side spots; yellowish belly with square brown spots.[1]

Size

Adults: Up to 1.69 m long, including an 18 cm tail.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: Central and northern Mexico, from Sonora and Coahuila south to Oaxaca and Puebla.[1,2]

Who described it

First described by Duméril in 1853. The brackets around the authority, (Duméril, 1853), mean it was first described in a different genus.

Where the name comes from

deppei: named after Ferdinand Deppe, a German naturalist in Mexico

Mexican Bull Snake: ‘Mexican’ – it lives in Mexico.
