Spotted Bush Snake

Philothamnus semivariegatus (Smith, 1840)

The Spotted Bush Snake is a slender, bright green, harmless snake found across much of Africa.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Bright green body with black speckles.[1]

Appearance

Bright green with black speckles.[1]

Size

Adults: The body (without the tail) is usually 60 to 90 cm long.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: From South Africa north to Sudan and Ethiopia, and west to Guinea and Senegal.[1,2]

Reproduction

Lays 3 to 12 long eggs each summer; hatchlings are about 25 cm long.[1]

Where the name comes from

semivariegatus: ‘partly variegated’

Spotted Bush Snake: ‘Spotted’ – its spots.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
