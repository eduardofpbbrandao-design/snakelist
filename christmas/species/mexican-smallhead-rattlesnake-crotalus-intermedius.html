Where the name comes from

Crotalus: from the Greek krotalon, a rattle or castanet - after the rattle at the tip of the tail[1]

intermedius: ‘intermediate’

Mexican Smallhead Rattlesnake: ‘Mexican’ – it lives in Mexico; ‘Rattlesnake’ – it shakes the rattle at the tip of its tail.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
