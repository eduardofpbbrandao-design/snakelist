How precisely they are recorded

Of the 77 living species recorded in Pakistan, 25 are recorded by province or territory in SnakeList’s data; 52 are recorded only for Pakistan as a whole, so the province or territory pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by province or territory, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a province may live in only part of it.
