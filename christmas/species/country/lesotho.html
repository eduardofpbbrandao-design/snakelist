How precisely they are recorded

Of the 21 living species recorded in Lesotho, 2 are recorded by district in SnakeList’s data; 19 are recorded only for Lesotho as a whole, so the district pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by district, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a district may live in only part of it.
