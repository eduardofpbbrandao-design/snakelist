How precisely they are recorded

Of the 9 living species recorded in Mongolia, 5 are recorded by province in SnakeList’s data; 4 are recorded only for Mongolia as a whole, so the province pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by province, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a province may live in only part of it.
