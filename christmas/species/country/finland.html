How precisely they are recorded

Of the 3 living species recorded in Finland, 2 are recorded by region in SnakeList’s data; 1 is recorded only for Finland as a whole, so the region pages list it as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by region, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a region may live in only part of it.
