How precisely they are recorded

Of the 13 living species recorded in Kazakhstan, 11 are recorded by region in SnakeList’s data; 2 are recorded only for Kazakhstan as a whole, so the region pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by region, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a region may live in only part of it.
