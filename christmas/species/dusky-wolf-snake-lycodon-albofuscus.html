Where the name comes from

Lycodon: from the Greek lykos (wolf) and odon (tooth) - for the long, fang-like front teeth[1]

Dusky Wolf Snake: ‘Dusky’ – its dark colour; ‘Wolf Snake’ – it has long fang-like front teeth, like a wolf.
