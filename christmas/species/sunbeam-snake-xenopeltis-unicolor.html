Sunbeam Snake

Xenopeltis unicolor Reinwardt, 1827

The Sunbeam Snake is a harmless burrowing snake of South-east Asia whose scales shine with rainbow colours in the sun.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Rainbow shine on dark, smooth scales.[1]

Appearance

Reddish-brown, brown or blackish above, whitish-grey below, with highly iridescent scales; a narrow, wedge-shaped head for digging.[1]

Size

Adults: About 1 m long on average.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: Southern China, Myanmar, the Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Thailand, Indochina, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and the Philippines.[1,2]
