Where the name comes from

Bothrops: from the Greek bothros (pit) and ops (eye or face) - for the heat-sensing pits on the head[1]

ayerbei: named after a person called Ayerbe

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
