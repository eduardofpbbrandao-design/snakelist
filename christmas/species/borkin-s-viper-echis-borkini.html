    Where the name comes from

    Echis: the Latin spelling of the Greek word echis, meaning viper[1]

    borkini: named after a person called Borkin

    Borkin's Viper: ‘Borkin's’ – named after a person called Borkin; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.
