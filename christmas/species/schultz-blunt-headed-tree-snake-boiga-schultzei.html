Where the name comes from

schultzei: named after a person called Schultze

Schultz' Blunt-headed Tree Snake: ‘Schultz'’ – named after a person called Schultze; ‘Blunt-headed’ – for its blunt head; ‘Tree Snake’ – it lives in trees.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
