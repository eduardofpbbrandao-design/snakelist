Mexican Parrot Snake

Leptophis mexicanus Duméril, Bibron & Duméril, 1854

The Mexican Parrot Snake is a medium-sized, slender snake of southern Mexico and Central America.[1]

Who described it

First described by Duméril, Bibron & Duméril in 1854.

Where the name comes from

Leptophis: ‘slender snake’, from the Greek roots lept- ‘slender’ + -ophis ‘snake’

mexicanus: ‘of Mexico’ – named after where it lives

Mexican Parrot Snake: ‘Mexican’ – it lives in Mexico; ‘Parrot Snake’ – it is bright green, like a parrot.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
