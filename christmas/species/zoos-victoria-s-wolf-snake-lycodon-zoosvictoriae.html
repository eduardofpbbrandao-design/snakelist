    Where the name comes from

    Lycodon: from the Greek lykos (wolf) and odon (tooth) - for the long, fang-like front teeth[1]

    zoosvictoriae: named after Zoos Victoria, the zoo group in Victoria, Australia

    Zoos Victoria's Wolf Snake: ‘Victoria's’ – named after Zoos Victoria, the zoo group in Victoria, Australia; ‘Wolf Snake’ – it has long fang-like front teeth, like a wolf.
