Balsas Coral Snake

Micrurus laticollaris

Balsas Coral Snake (Micrurus laticollaris) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

Micrurus: ‘small tail’, from the Greek roots micr- ‘small’ + -urus ‘tail’

laticollaris: ‘broad collar’, from Latin roots: lati- ‘broad’ + -collaris ‘collar’

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
