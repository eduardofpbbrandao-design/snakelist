African Bush Viper

Atheris squamigera

African Bush Viper (Atheris squamigera) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 8 more.

Where the name comes from

squamigera: ‘scale-bearing’

African Bush Viper: ‘African’ – it lives in Africa; ‘Bush Viper’ – a viper that climbs in bushes and trees.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
