Where the name comes from

Vipera: probably from the Latin vivus (alive) and pario (to give birth) - because most vipers bear live young[1]

graeca: ‘of Greece’ – named after where it lives

Greek Meadow Viper: ‘Greek’ – it lives in Greece; ‘Meadow’ – it lives in meadows; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
