SnakeList - Christmas preview: credits

All Christmas artwork in this folder is original and drawn by code for SnakeList
(tools/christmas_art.py and tools/christmas/xmas.js): the pine branches, fairy lights,
baubles, pine sprigs, snowflakes, snowfall and the Santa hat. No outside images,
fonts or libraries were added, so there is nothing to license or credit.

The snake S in the Christmas icon is SnakeList's own logo, taken from the live site
each time the preview is built.

Species photographs, maps and 3D models shown in the preview are the live site's own
files (credited there as usual); the preview links to them and does not copy them.
