SnakeList - Christmas preview: credits

All Christmas artwork in this folder is original and drawn by code for SnakeList
(tools/christmas_art.py, tools/christmas/xmas.js and tools/christmas/cabin.js): the pine
branches, fairy lights, baubles, snowflakes, snowfall, the Santa hats, the stocking, the
snow on the logo, the little Santa-hat snakes, the ornaments and the Christmas Cabin room
(painted by code and rendered to xmas-cabin.webp when the preview is built). No outside
images, fonts or libraries were added, so there is nothing to license or credit.

Music: "Jingle Bells" by James Lord Pierpont (1857), public domain. It is not a
recording - the tune is played in the browser by code (Web Audio: bells, a soft pad,
a bass line and sleigh bells, arranged for SnakeList). The fireplace sound is made the
same way. Nothing is downloaded.

The snake S in the Christmas icon and logo is SnakeList's own logo, taken from the live
site each time the preview is built. The little snakes' colours come from SnakeList's own
3D snake models (species/3d/models.json).

Species photographs, maps and 3D models shown in the preview are the live site's own
files (credited there as usual); the preview links to them and does not copy them.
