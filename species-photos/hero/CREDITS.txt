Randomized opening photographs (v6.11)

Each was cropped to the 2560:1658 shape of the opening (so the logo sits under the snake's head) and saved as WebP at the widths
listed. They keep the licence of the original; CC BY-SA photos remain under CC BY-SA. The credit is shown on the opening itself.
