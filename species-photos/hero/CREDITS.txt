SnakeList homepage hero photograph
==================================

Files:    wagler-wide-3000.webp, wagler-wide-2400.webp, wagler-wide-1800.webp, wagler-wide-1200.webp
Subject:  Wagler's pit viper (Tropidolaemus wagleri), a living snake among green leaves in its habitat
          (the photo SnakeList already uses for this species - see species/photo-credits.html)
Author:   Nico (iNaturalist)
Source:   https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/106537980
          (original file: https://inaturalist-open-data.s3.amazonaws.com/photos/106537980/original.jpg,
          1536 x 2048 px, from the iNaturalist Open Data bucket, which only holds openly licensed photos)
Licence:  Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
Changes:  top 18% cropped, slight contrast boost, placed on a wide canvas (3000 x 1680) whose background is
          a heavily blurred, darkened copy of the same photo (its own green leaves), resized to
          3000/2400/1800/1200 px wide copies, saved as WebP. On the page the lower part is progressively
          blurred and faded into the background. The photographer does not endorse SnakeList.

The hero shows a credit line ("Photo: Nico - CC BY-NC") linking to the source.
