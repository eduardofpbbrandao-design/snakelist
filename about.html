About me

Hi, I'm Eduardo - a snake lover who built SnakeList so anyone can explore every snake on Earth.

Why I made SnakeList

I wanted one place to see every snake species in the world - what it looks like, where it lives, whether it's venomous - and to keep track of the ones I've seen myself. SnakeList now covers more than 4,000 species, with photos, range maps down to states and provinces, a quiz and a camera that guesses which snake you're looking at. It's free, and it keeps growing with every update.

What you can do on SnakeList

  • Browse all 4,254 snake species, each with its own page, photo and map
  • Mark the snakes you've seen, add your photos, dates and places
  • See where every snake lives on the map and test yourself in the quiz
  • Take a photo with the Camera and find out which snake it is

Get in touch

Got an idea, or found something wrong? Use the Report button at the top of SnakeList - I read every one.
