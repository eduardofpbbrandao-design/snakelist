About

Malayan Whipsnake (Ahaetulla mycterizans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 122 places on iNaturalist.
