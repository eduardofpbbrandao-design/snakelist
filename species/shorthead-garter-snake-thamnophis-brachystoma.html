About

Shorthead Garter Snake (Thamnophis brachystoma) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, forest edges and 15 more. Common: recorded in 85 places on iNaturalist.
