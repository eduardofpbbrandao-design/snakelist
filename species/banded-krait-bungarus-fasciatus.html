About

Banded Krait (Bungarus fasciatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Brunei, Cambodia, China, India and 9 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 350 places on iNaturalist.
