Gloyd's Moccasin

Agkistrodon howardgloydi

Gloyd's Moccasin (Agkistrodon howardgloydi) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras and Nicaragua.

Where the name comes from

Agkistrodon: from the Greek ankistron (fishhook) and odon (tooth), probably for the curved fangs[1]

howardgloydi: named after Howard Gloyd, an American herpetologist

Gloyd's Moccasin: ‘Gloyd's’ – named after Howard Gloyd, an American herpetologist.
