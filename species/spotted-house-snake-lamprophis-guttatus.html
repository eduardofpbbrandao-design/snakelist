About

Spotted House Snake (Lamprophis guttatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eswatini, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as rocky hillsides, rocky outcrops, rock crevices. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
