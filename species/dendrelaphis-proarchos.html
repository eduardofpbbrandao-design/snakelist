About

Eastern bronzeback tree snake (Dendrelaphis proarchos) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Laos and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 30 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
