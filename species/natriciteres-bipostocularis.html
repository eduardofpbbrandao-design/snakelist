About

Southwestern Forest Marsh Snake (Natriciteres bipostocularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 25 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
