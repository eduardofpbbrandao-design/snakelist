About

Peri-Forest House Snake (Boaedon perisilvestris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
