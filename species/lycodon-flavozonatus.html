About

Big-tooth Snake (Lycodon flavozonatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Myanmar, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 207 places on iNaturalist.
