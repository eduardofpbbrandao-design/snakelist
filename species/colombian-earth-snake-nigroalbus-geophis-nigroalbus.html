About

Colombian Earth Snake (nigroalbus) (Geophis nigroalbus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
