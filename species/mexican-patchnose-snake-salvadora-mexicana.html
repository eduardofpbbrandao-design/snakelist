About

Mexican Patchnose Snake (Salvadora mexicana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 104 places on iNaturalist.
