About

Green Water Snake (Philothamnus hoplogaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini and 10 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 52 more. Common: recorded in 212 places on iNaturalist.
