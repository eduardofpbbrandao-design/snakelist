About

Papuan Whip Snake (Demansia papuensis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 112 places on iNaturalist.
