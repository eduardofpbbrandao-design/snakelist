About

Brown-spotted Pit Viper (Trimeresurus venustus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
