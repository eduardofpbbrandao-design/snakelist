About

Moila Snake (Malpolon moilensis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Iran, Iraq and 15 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 106 places on iNaturalist.
