About

Blackbelly Centipede Snake (Tantilla moesta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 7 more. Recorded in 23 places on iNaturalist.
