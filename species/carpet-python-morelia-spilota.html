About

Carpet Python (Morelia spilota) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 586 places on iNaturalist.
