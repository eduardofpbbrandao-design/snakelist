About

Chiapan Boa (Ungaliophis continentalis) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
