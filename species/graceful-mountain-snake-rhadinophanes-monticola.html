About

Graceful Mountain Snake (Rhadinophanes monticola) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
