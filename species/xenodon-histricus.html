About

Jan's Hognose Snake (Xenodon histricus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
