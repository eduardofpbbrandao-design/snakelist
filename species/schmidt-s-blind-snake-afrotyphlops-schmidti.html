Schmidt's blind-snake

Afrotyphlops schmidti

Schmidt's blind-snake (Afrotyphlops schmidti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, South Africa, Zambia.
