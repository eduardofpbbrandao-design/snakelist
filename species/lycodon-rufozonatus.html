About

Red-banded Snake (Lycodon rufozonatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Laos, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, mountain forest, forest edges and 12 more. Common: recorded in 863 places on iNaturalist.
