Where the name comes from

Daboia: thought to come from a Hindi word, dabohiya, meaning "that lies hid" or "the lurker"[1]

palaestinae: ‘of Palestine’ – named after where it lives

Palestine Viper: ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.
