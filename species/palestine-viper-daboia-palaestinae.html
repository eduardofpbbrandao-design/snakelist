Palestine Viper

Daboia palaestinae

Palestine Viper (Daboia palaestinae) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Israel, Jordan, Lebanon and Syria.

Daboia: thought to come from a Hindi word, dabohiya, meaning "that lies hid" or "the lurker"[1]
