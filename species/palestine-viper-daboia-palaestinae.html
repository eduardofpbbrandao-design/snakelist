About

Palestine Viper (Daboia palaestinae) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 14 more. Recorded in 66 places on iNaturalist.
