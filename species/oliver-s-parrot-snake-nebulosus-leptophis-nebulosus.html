About

Oliver's Parrot Snake (nebulosus) (Leptophis nebulosus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Recorded in 36 places on iNaturalist.
