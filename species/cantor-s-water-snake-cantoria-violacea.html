About

Cantor's water snake (Cantoria violacea) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
