About

Mulanje Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus leleupi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 25 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
