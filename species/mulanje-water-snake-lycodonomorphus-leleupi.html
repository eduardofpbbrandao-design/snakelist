Mulanje Water Snake

Lycodonomorphus leleupi

Mulanje Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus leleupi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Zambia and Zimbabwe.
