About

Cape Wolf Snake (multimaculatum) (Lycophidion multimaculatum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Namibia and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 15 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
