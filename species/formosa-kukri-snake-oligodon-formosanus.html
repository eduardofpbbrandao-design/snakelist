About

Formosa Kukri Snake (Oligodon formosanus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 307 places on iNaturalist.
