About

Jack's Water Snake (Homalopsis mereljcoxi) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, China, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
