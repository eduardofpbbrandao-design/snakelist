About

Sargi's Earth Snake (Adelphicos sargii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
