About

Many-scaled Keelback (Tropidonophis multiscutellatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
