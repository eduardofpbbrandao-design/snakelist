About

Burmese Python (Python bivittatus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 6 more. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Florida Everglades), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 435 places on iNaturalist.
