    Where the name comes from

    Vipera: probably from the Latin vivus (alive) and pario (to give birth) - because most vipers bear live young[1]

    transcaucasiana: ‘of Transcaucasia’ – named after where it lives

    Transcaucasian Long-nosed Viper: ‘Long-nosed’ – for its long nose; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.
