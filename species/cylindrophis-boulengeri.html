About

Boulenger's Pipe Snake (Cylindrophis boulengeri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Cylindrophiidae, found in Indonesia, Timor-Leste. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
