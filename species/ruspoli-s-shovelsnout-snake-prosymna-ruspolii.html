About

Ruspoli's Shovelsnout Snake (Prosymna ruspolii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 23 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
