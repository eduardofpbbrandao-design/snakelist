About

Sindh Thread Snake (Myriopholis blanfordi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Afghanistan, India, Iran, Pakistan, Saudi Arabia. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, dry woodland, scrub forest and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
