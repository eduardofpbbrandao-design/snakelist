About

Cope's Antilles Snake (Hypsirhynchus parvifrons) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bahamas, Dominican Republic, Haiti. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Recorded in 48 places on iNaturalist.
