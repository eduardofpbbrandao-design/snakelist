About

South Philippine Temple Pitviper (Tropidolaemus philippensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
