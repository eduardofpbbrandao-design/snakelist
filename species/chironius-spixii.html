About

Spix's Sipo (Chironius spixii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 114 places on iNaturalist.
