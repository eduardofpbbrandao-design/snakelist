About

Congo File Snake (Limaformosa savorgnani) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
