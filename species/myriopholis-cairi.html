About

Cairo Blind Snake (Myriopholis cairi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Mauritania, Niger and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 24 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
