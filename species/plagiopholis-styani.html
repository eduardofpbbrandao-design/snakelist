About

Chinese Mountain Snake (Plagiopholis styani) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
