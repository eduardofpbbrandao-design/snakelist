About

Neuwied's False Fer-de-lance (Xenodon neuwiedii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Common: recorded in 200 places on iNaturalist.
