About

Plumbeous Centipede Eater (Aparallactus lunulatus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Botswana, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 18 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 37 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
