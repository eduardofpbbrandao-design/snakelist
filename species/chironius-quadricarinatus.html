About

Central Sipo (quadricarinatus) (Chironius quadricarinatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 33 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
