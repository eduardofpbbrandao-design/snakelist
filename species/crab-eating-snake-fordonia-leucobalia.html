About

Crab-eating Snake (Fordonia leucobalia) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, eucalyptus forest, riverine forest, gallery forest and 37 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
