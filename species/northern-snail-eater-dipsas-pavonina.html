About

Northern Snail-eater (Dipsas pavonina) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Ecuador, French Guiana, Guyana and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 36 places on iNaturalist.
