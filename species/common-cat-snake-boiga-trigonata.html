About

Common Cat Snake (Boiga trigonata) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Iran, Nepal and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 200 places on iNaturalist.
