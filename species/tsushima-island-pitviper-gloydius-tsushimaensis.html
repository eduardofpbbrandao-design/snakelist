Where the name comes from

tsushimaensis: ‘from Tsushima Island (Japan)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Tsushima Island Pitviper: ‘Island’ – it lives on an island; ‘Pitviper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.
