About

Red-headed whip-snake (Coluber rubriceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Israel, Syria. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, forest edges, shrubland and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
