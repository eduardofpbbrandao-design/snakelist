About

Stoliczk's Tawny Cat Snake (Boiga stoliczkae) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 13 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
