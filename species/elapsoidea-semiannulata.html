About

Angolan Garter Snake (Elapsoidea semiannulata) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 24 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 21 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
