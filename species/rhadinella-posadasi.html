About

Posada's Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinella posadasi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
