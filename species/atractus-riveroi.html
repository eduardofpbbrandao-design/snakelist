About

Rivero's Ground Snake (Atractus riveroi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
