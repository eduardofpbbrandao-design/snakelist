About

Greater Seasnake (Hydrophis major) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, India, New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 33 more. Recorded in 59 places on iNaturalist.
