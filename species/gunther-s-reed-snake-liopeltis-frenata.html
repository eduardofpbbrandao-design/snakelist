About

Günther's Reed Snake (Liopeltis frenata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
