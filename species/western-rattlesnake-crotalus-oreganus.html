About

Western Rattlesnake (Crotalus oreganus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 663 places on iNaturalist.
