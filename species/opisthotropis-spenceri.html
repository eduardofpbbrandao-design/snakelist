About

Smith's Mountain Keelback (Opisthotropis spenceri) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
