About

Andasibe Blind Snake (Madatyphlops andasibensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, highlands, underground burrows and 10 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
