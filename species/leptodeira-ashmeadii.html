About

Ashmead's Banded Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira ashmeadii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 177 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Brazil: Roraima
  • Colombia: Arauca, Atlántico, Bolívar, Casanare, La Guajira, Magdalena, Meta, Vichada
  • Venezuela: Apure, Aragua, Barinas, Cojedes, Lara, Miranda, Nueva Esparta, Tachira, Yaracuy, Zulia
