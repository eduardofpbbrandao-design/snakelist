About

Lonely Spotbelly-Snake (Coniophanes longinquus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
