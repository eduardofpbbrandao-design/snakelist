South American Coral Snake

Micrurus lemniscatus

South American Coral Snake (Micrurus lemniscatus) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It is known to eat other snakes.
