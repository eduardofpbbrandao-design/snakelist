About

Short Racer (Platyceps brevis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 23 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
