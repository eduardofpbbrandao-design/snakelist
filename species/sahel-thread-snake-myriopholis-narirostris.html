About

Sahel Thread Snake (Myriopholis narirostris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast and 4 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
