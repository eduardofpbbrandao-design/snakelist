About

Kenya Beaked Snake (Rhinotyphlops unitaeniatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
