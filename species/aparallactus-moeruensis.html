About

Zaire Centipede-eater (Aparallactus moeruensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
