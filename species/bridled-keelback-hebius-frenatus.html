About

Bridled Keelback (Hebius frenatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 20 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
