About

Milk Snake (Lampropeltis triangulum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 5387 places on iNaturalist.
