About

Tucuman Threadsnake (Epictia albipuncta) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
