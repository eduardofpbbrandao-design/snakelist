About

Costal Lyre Snake (Trimorphodon biscutatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 146 places on iNaturalist.
