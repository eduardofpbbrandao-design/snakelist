About

Arizona Black Rattlesnake (Crotalus cerberus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, pine forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 156 places on iNaturalist.
