About

Rhino Rat Snake (Gonyosoma boulengeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, indochina rainforest. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
