About

Large Whip Snake (caspius) (Dolichophis caspius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Bulgaria, Crimea, Croatia, Greece and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 719 places on iNaturalist.
