Large Whip Snake (caspius)

Dolichophis caspius (Gmelin, 1789)

The Caspian Whipsnake is a fast, non-venomous snake of the Balkans and eastern Europe - perhaps the longest snake in Europe.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Very long, slender and fast.[1]

Size

Adults: Usually 140 to 160 cm long; a few exceed 2 m, and the record was about 2.5 m.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: The Balkans (Albania, Greece, Bulgaria, North Macedonia, Serbia, Croatia, Montenegro, Romania, southern Hungary), Turkey, Moldova, southern Ukraine and Russia, Kazakhstan, Georgia and Iran.[1,2]

Who described it

First described by Gmelin in 1789. The brackets around the authority, (Gmelin, 1789), mean it was first described in a different genus.

Where the name comes from

Dolichophis: ‘long snake’, from the Greek roots dolich- ‘long’ + -ophis ‘snake’

caspius: ‘of the Caspian Sea’ – named after where it lives

Large Whip Snake (caspius): ‘Large’ – its size.
