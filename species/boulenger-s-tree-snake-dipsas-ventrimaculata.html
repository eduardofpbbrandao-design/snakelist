About

Boulenger's Tree Snake (Dipsas ventrimaculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
