About

Brazilian Mussurana (Mussurana quimi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 14 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
