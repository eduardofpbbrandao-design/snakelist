About

Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra (Naja siamensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Cambodia: Koh Kong, Siem Reap
  • Laos
  • Thailand: Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai, Chon Buri, Kanchanaburi, Nakhon Ratchasima, Phetchaburi, Prachin Buri, Rayong, Suphan Buri, Trat, Udon Thani
  • Vietnam: Bình Thuận, Đông Nam Bộ
