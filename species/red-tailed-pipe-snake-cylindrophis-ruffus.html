About

Red-tailed Pipe Snake (Cylindrophis ruffus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Cylindrophiidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
