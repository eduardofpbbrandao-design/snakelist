About

Red-tailed Pipe Snake (Cylindrophis ruffus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Cylindrophiidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar and 3 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
