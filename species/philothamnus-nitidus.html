About

Green Bush Snake (Philothamnus nitidus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
