About

Ringed Keelback Water Snake (Trimerodytes annularis) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in China, Taiwan. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Recorded in 61 places on iNaturalist.
