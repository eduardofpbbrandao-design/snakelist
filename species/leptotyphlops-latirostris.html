About

Uvira Worm Snake (Leptotyphlops latirostris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
