About

Broad-ringed Coral Snake (Micrurus latifasciatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
