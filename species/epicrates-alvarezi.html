About

Argentinian Rainbow Boa (Epicrates alvarezi) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 11 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
