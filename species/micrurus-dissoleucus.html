About

Pygmy Coral Snake (Micrurus dissoleucus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia, Panama, Venezuela. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 5 more. Recorded in 30 places on iNaturalist.
