About

Cantil (Agkistrodon russeolus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 85 places on iNaturalist.
