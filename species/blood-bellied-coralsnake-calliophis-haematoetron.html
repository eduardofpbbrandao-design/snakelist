About

Blood-bellied Coralsnake (Calliophis haematoetron) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Sri Lanka. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, sri lanka rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
