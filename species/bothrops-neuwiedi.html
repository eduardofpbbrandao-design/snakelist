About

Jararaca Pintada (Bothrops neuwiedi) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 33 more. Recorded in 71 places on iNaturalist.
