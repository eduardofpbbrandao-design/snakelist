About

Southern Coral Snake (Micrurus frontalis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 14 more. Recorded in 39 places on iNaturalist.
