About

Brown Water Snake (rufulus) (Lycodonomorphus rufulus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Malawi, Mozambique and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 295 places on iNaturalist.
