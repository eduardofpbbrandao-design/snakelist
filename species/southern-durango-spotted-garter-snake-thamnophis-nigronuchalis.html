Where the name comes from

Thamnophis: from the Greek thamnos (bush or shrub) and ophis (snake)[1]

nigronuchalis: ‘black nape’, from Latin roots: nigro- ‘black’ + -nuchalis ‘nape’

Southern Durango Spotted Garter Snake: ‘Southern’ – where it lives (in the south); ‘Durango’ – the place where it lives; ‘Spotted’ – its spots; ‘Garter Snake’ – its stripes look like the stripes on old-fashioned sock garters.
