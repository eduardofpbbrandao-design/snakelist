About

Matana Mud Snake (Hypsiscopus matannensis) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
