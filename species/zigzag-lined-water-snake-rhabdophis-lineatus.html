About

Zigzag-lined Water Snake (Rhabdophis lineatus) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 17 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
