About

Rhombic Skaapsteker (Psammophylax rhombeatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 463 places on iNaturalist.
