Bogert's Shadow Snake (Synophis bogerti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
