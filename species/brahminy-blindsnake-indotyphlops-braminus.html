Brahminy Blindsnake

Indotyphlops braminus (Daudin, 1803)

Brahminy Blindsnake (Indotyphlops braminus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in American Samoa, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Comoros and 22 more. People introduced it to Florida, Hawaii.
