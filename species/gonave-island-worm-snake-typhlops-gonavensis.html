    Where the name comes from

    Typhlops: from the Greek typhlos (blind) and ōps (eye)[1]

    gonavensis: ‘from Gonâve Island (Haiti)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

    Gonave Island Worm Snake: ‘Island’ – it lives on an island; ‘Worm Snake’ – it is small and looks like a worm.
