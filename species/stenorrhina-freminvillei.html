About

Blood Snake (Stenorrhina freminvillei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 270 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Costa Rica: Alajuela, Guanacaste, Puntarenas, San José
  • El Salvador: Cuscatlán, La Libertad, San Miguel, San Salvador, San Vicente, Usulután
  • Guatemala: Escuintla, Guatemala
  • Honduras: Copán, Cortés, El Paraíso, Francisco Morazán, Lempira, Olancho
  • Mexico: Campeche, Chiapas, Guerrero, Michoacán, Oaxaca, Quintana Roo, Veracruz, Yucatán
