About

Tengchong Keelback Snake (Hebius septemlineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 20 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
