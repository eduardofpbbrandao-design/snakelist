About

Moluccan Flying Snake (Chrysopelea rhodopleuron) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
