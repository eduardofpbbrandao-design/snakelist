About

Plumbeous Mussurana (Clelia plumbea) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 24 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
