About

Ribboned Brittle-Snake (Urotheca lateristriga) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 7 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
