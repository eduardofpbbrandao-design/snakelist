Mulga Snake

Pseudechis australis (Gray, 1842)

The King Brown (Mulga) Snake is Australia's largest venomous land snake. Despite its name it belongs to the black snakes and is only distantly related to true brown snakes.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Large, heavy brown snake with a broad head.[1]

Appearance

Robust, with a head slightly wider than the body and prominent cheeks.[1]

Size

Adults: Can reach 2 to 2.5 m and 3 to 6 kg; the longest confirmed was 3.3 m. Males are bigger.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: All Australian states except Victoria and Tasmania - most of northern, western and central Australia.[1,2]

Behaviour

Mostly active at dusk, resting in soil cracks, burrows or under rocks and logs; in warm months it is active later into the night. Males wrestle in the breeding season.[1]

Reproduction

Lays eggs.[2]
