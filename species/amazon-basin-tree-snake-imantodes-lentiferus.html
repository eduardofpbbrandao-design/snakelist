About

Amazon Basin Tree Snake (Imantodes lentiferus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Recorded in 57 places on iNaturalist.
