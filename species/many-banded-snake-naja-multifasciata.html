Many-banded Snake

Naja multifasciata

Many-banded Snake (Naja multifasciata) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon.

    Where the name comes from

    Naja: from the Sanskrit nāga, meaning snake[1]

    multifasciata: ‘many-banded’, from Latin roots: multi- ‘many’ + -fasciata ‘banded’

    Many-banded Snake: ‘Many-banded’ – for its many bands.

    Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
