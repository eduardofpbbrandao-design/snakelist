About

Vogel's Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis vogeli) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 21 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
