About

West African Banded Cobra (Naja savannula) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Chad, Gambia, Ghana and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 20 more. Recorded in 23 places on iNaturalist.
