About

Gans' Egg Eater (Dasypeltis gansi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
