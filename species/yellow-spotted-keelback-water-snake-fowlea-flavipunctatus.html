About

Yellow-spotted Keelback Water Snake (Fowlea flavipunctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, Indonesia, Laos and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
