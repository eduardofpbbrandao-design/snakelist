About

Blue Nile Cat Snake (Telescopus gezirae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Sudan. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest and 9 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
