Southwestern Forest Marsh Snake

Natriciteres bipostocularis

Southwestern Forest Marsh Snake (Natriciteres bipostocularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania and Zambia.
