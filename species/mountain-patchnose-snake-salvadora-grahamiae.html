About

Mountain Patchnose Snake (Salvadora grahamiae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 39 more. Common: recorded in 270 places on iNaturalist.
