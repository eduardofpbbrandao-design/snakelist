About

Forest Flame Snake (Oxyrhopus petolarius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 618 places on iNaturalist.
