About

Chinese Leopard Snake (Elaphe bimaculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Mongolia. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 32 places on iNaturalist.
