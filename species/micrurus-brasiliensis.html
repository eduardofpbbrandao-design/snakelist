About

Brazilian coralsnake (Micrurus brasiliensis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 17 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
