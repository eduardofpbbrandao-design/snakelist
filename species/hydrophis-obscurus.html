About

Estuarine Sea Snake (Hydrophis obscurus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
