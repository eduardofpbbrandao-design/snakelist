Where the name comes from

bocourti: named after Marie Firmin Bocourt, a French zoologist

Bocourt's Black-headed Snake: ‘Bocourt's’ – named after Marie Firmin Bocourt, a French zoologist; ‘Black-headed’ – for its black head.
