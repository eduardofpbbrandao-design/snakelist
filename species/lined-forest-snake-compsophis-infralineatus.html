Where the name comes from

Compsophis: ‘elegant snake’, from the Greek roots comps- ‘elegant’ + -ophis ‘snake’

infralineatus: ‘lined underneath’

Lined Forest Snake: ‘Lined’ – its lines; ‘Forest’ – it lives in forests.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
