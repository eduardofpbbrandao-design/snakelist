About

Painted Keelback (cerasogaster) (Xenochrophis cerasogaster) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 20 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
