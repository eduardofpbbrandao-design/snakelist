About

Boomslang (Dispholidus typus) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi and 30 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 63 more. Common: recorded in 595 places on iNaturalist.
