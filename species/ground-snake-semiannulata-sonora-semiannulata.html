Ground Snake (semiannulata)

Sonora semiannulata Baird & Girard, 1853

The Western Ground Snake is a small, harmless snake of the south-western United States and northern Mexico that comes in many colours and patterns.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Small snake with very variable bands or stripes.[1]

Appearance

Brown, red or orange, with black bands, stripes, or no pattern at all; white or grey belly; smooth scales.[1]

Size

Adults: About 8 to 19 in (20 to 48 cm) long.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: The south-western and south-central USA and northern Mexico.[1,2]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Dry, rocky places with loose soil.[1]

Behaviour

Secretive and active at night, often found on roadsides foraging.[1]

Diet

Invertebrates: spiders, scorpions, centipedes, crickets and insect larvae.[1]

Reproduction

Lays eggs.[1]
