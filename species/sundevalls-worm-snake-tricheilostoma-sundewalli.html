About

Sundevalls Worm Snake (Tricheilostoma sundewalli) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Central African Republic, Ghana, Togo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
