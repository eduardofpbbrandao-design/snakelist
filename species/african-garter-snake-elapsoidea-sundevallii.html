About

African Garter Snake (Elapsoidea sundevallii) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Kenya, Mozambique, Namibia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
