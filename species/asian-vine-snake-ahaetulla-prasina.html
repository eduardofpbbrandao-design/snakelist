About

Asian Vine Snake (Ahaetulla prasina) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 961 places on iNaturalist.
