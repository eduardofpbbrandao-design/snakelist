About

Red-spotted Earth Snake (Uropeltis rubromaculata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Uropeltidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
