About

Katanga Mountain Bush Viper (Atheris katangensis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
