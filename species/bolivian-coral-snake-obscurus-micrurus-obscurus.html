About

Bolivian Coral Snake (obscurus) (Micrurus obscurus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
