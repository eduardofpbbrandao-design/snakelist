About

Khasi Keelback (Hebius khasiensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Laos, Myanmar and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 23 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
