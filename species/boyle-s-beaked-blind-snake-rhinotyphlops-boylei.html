About

Boyle's Beaked Blind Snake (Rhinotyphlops boylei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Namibia. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
