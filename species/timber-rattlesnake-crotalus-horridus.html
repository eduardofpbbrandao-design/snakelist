About

Timber Rattlesnake (Crotalus horridus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 3870 places on iNaturalist.
