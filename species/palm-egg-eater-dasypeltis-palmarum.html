About

Palm Egg Eater (Dasypeltis palmarum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Uganda. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
