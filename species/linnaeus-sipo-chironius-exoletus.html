About

Linnaeus' Sipo (Chironius exoletus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 55 more. Common: recorded in 311 places on iNaturalist.
