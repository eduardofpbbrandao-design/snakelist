About

Banded Kukri (Oligodon arnensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 151 places on iNaturalist.
