About

Mexican White-lipped Snake (Symphimus leucostomus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
