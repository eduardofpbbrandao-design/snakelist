Malayan Whipsnake

Ahaetulla mycterizans

Malayan Whipsnake (Ahaetulla mycterizans) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and Thailand.

Where the name comes from

Malayan Whipsnake: ‘Malayan’ – after Malaya; ‘Whipsnake’ – it is long and thin, like a whip.
