About

Malayan Whipsnake (Ahaetulla mycterizans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and 1 more. Common: recorded in 122 places on iNaturalist.
