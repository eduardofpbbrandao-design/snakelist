About

Whip Snake (Psammophis notostictus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, woodland, dry woodland, forest edges and 20 more. Common: recorded in 305 places on iNaturalist.
