About

Aruba Island Rattlesnake (Crotalus unicolor) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Aruba, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, forest edges, roadsides, caribbean dry forest. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
