Surroundings in the 3D viewer (species/3d/env/*.hdr)

These panoramas are Poly Haven HDRIs (https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain),
in the reduced 512 x 256 versions published in the npm package @pmndrs/assets 1.7.0 (CC0 1.0,
https://github.com/pmndrs/assets). They were decoded from that package's EXR files and saved as
run-length-encoded Radiance .hdr files with no other change.

  park.hdr     @pmndrs/assets hdri/park.exr     (Poly Haven "Rooitou Park")
  forest.hdr   @pmndrs/assets hdri/forest.exr   (Poly Haven "Forest Slope")
  sunrise.hdr  @pmndrs/assets hdri/sunrise.exr
  dawn.hdr     @pmndrs/assets hdri/dawn.exr     (Poly Haven "Kiara 1 Dawn")
  sky.hdr      @pmndrs/assets hdri/sky.exr
