About

Bangweulu Water Snake (Limnophis bangweolicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Namibia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 30 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
