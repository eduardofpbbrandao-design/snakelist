About

Kinghorn's Scrub Python (Simalia kinghorni) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 156 places on iNaturalist.
