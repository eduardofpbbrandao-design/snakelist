About

Western Blackhead Snake (Tantilla planiceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, shrubland, scrubland, chaparral and 12 more. Common: recorded in 150 places on iNaturalist.
