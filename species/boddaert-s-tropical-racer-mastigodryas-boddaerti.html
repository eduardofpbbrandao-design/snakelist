About

Boddaert's Tropical Racer (Mastigodryas boddaerti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Guyana, Peru and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 46 more. Common: recorded in 264 places on iNaturalist.
