Small-eyed File Snake

Mehelya stenophthalmus

Small-eyed File Snake (Mehelya stenophthalmus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 10 more. It is known to eat other snakes.
