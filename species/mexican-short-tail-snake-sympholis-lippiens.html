About

Mexican Short-tail Snake (Sympholis lippiens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 15 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
