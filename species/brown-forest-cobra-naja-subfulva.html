Brown Forest Cobra

Naja subfulva

Where the name comes from

Naja: from the Sanskrit nāga, meaning snake[1]

Brown Forest Cobra: ‘Brown’ – its colour; ‘Forest’ – it lives in forests; ‘Cobra’ – from the Portuguese cobra de capelo, ‘hooded snake’.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
