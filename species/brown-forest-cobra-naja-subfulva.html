About

Brown Forest Cobra (Naja subfulva) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 42 more. Common: recorded in 93 places on iNaturalist.
