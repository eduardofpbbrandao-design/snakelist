About

Dragon snake (Xenodermus javanicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Xenodermidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Recorded in 48 places on iNaturalist.
