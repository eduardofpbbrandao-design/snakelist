About

Chinese Mountain Pit Viper (Ovophis monticola) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India, Laos and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 17 more. Recorded in 63 places on iNaturalist.
