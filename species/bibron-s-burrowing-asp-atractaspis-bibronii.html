About

Bibron's Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis bibronii) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya and 8 more. Common: recorded in 160 places on iNaturalist.
