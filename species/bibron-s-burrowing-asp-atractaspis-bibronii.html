Where the name comes from

bibronii: named after Gabriel Bibron, a French zoologist

Bibron's Burrowing Asp: ‘Bibron's’ – named after Gabriel Bibron, a French zoologist; ‘Burrowing Asp’ – it burrows, and is venomous (asp is an old word for a venomous snake).

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
