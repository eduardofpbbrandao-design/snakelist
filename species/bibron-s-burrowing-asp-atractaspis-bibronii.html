Bibron's Burrowing Asp

Atractaspis bibronii

Bibron's Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis bibronii) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya and 8 more.
