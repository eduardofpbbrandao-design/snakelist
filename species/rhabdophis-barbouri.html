About

Barbour's Water Snake (Rhabdophis barbouri) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 17 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
