About

Flathead Snake (Tantilla gracilis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 489 places on iNaturalist.
