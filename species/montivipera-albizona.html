About

Mountain Viper (albizona) (Montivipera albizona) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Turkey. It lives in habitats such as rocky hillsides, rocky outcrops, rock crevices. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
