About

Red-headed Forest Racer (Dendrophidion rufiterminorum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
