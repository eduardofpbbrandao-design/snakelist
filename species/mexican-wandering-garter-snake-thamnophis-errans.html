About

Mexican Wandering Garter Snake (Thamnophis errans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, montane woodland and 24 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
