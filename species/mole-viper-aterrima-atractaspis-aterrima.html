About

Mole Viper (aterrima) (Atractaspis aterrima) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
