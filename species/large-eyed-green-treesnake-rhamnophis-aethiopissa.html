About

Large-eyed Green Treesnake (Rhamnophis aethiopissa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 32 places on iNaturalist.
