About

Arrow Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus sagittifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, forest edges and 8 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
