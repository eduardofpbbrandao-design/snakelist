About

Sunbeam Snake (Xenopeltis unicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Xenopeltidae, found in Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 8 more. Common: recorded in 428 places on iNaturalist.
