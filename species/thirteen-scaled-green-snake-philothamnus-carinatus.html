Thirteen-scaled Green Snake

Philothamnus carinatus

Thirteen-scaled Green Snake (Philothamnus carinatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 15 more.
