About

Costa Rica Worm Snake (Amerotyphlops costaricensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
