About

Sonoran Coralsnake (Micruroides euryxanthus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 187 places on iNaturalist.
