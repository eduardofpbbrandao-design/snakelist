About

Caatinga Coral Snake (Micrurus ibiboboca) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
