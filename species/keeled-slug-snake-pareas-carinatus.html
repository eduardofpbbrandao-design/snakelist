About

Keeled Slug Snake (Pareas carinatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 174 places on iNaturalist.
