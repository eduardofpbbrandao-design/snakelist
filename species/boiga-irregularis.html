About

Brown Tree Snake (Boiga irregularis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guam, Papua New Guinea. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Guam), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 805 places on iNaturalist.
