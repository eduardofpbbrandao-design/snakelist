About

Solomon Island Ground Boa (Candoia paulsoni) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands, Vanuatu. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.

Native range

  • Papua New Guinea: Milne Bay
  • Solomon Islands: Guadalcanal, Malaita, Rennell and Bellona, Western
  • Vanuatu: Shefa
