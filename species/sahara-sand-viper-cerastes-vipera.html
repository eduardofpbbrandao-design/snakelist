About

Sahara Sand Viper (Cerastes vipera) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Libya, Mali and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 26 more. Recorded in 64 places on iNaturalist.
