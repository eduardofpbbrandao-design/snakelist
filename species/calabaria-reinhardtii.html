About

Calabar Ground Python (Calabaria reinhardtii) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Recorded in 27 places on iNaturalist.
