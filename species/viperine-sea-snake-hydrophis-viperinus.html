About

Viperine Sea Snake (Hydrophis viperinus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Indonesia, Iran, Malaysia, Pakistan and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
