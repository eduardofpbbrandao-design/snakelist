About

Somali Giant Blind-snake (Afrotyphlops brevis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Uganda. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, open woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest and 13 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
