About

Two-lined Mexican Earth Snake (Conopsis biserialis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 9 more. Recorded in 66 places on iNaturalist.
