About

Levantine Viper (Macrovipera lebetinus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Afghanistan, Cyprus, India, Iran, Iraq and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate rainforest, pine forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 100 places on iNaturalist.
