About

Black Forest Racer (Drymobius melanotropis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
