About

Brazilian Burrowing Snake (Gomesophis brasiliensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.

Native range
