About

Western Patchnose Snake (Salvadora hexalepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 693 places on iNaturalist.
