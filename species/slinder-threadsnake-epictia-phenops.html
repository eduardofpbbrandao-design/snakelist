About

Slinder Threadsnake (Epictia phenops) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
