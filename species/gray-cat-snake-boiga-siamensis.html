Where the name comes from

siamensis: ‘from Siam (Thailand)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Gray Cat Snake: ‘Gray’ – its colour; ‘Cat Snake’ – its eyes have upright slit pupils, like a cat's.
