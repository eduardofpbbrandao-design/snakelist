About

Gray Cat Snake (Boiga siamensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
