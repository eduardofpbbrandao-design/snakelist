About

Peru Burrowing Snake (Apostolepis nigroterminata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
