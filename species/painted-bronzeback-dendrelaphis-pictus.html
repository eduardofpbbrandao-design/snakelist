About

Painted Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis pictus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 650 places on iNaturalist.
