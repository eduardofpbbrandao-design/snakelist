About

Black File Snake (Gracililima nyassae) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Recorded in 68 places on iNaturalist.
