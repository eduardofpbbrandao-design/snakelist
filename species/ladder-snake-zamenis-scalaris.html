About

Ladder Snake (Zamenis scalaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in France, Gibraltar, Monaco, Portugal, Spain. It is also invasive outside its native range (Ibiza and Formentera). It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 1502 places on iNaturalist.
