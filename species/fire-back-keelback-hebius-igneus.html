About

Fire-back Keelback (Hebius igneus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
