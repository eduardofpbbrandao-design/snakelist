About

Coastal House Snake (littoralis) (Boaedon littoralis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
