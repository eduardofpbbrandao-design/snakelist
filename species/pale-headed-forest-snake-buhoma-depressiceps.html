About

Pale-headed Forest Snake (Buhoma depressiceps) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
