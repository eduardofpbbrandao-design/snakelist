About

Central Plains Milksnake (Lampropeltis gentilis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 701 places on iNaturalist.
