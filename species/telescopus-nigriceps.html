About

Black-headed Snake (nigriceps) (Telescopus nigriceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Iran, Iraq, Jordan, Syria, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as desert, rocky desert, gravel desert, rocky hillsides and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
