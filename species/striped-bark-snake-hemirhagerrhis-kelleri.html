About

Striped Bark Snake (Hemirhagerrhis kelleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 15 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
