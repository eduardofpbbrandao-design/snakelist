About

Eastern Madagascar Water Snake (Thamnosophis epistibes) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
