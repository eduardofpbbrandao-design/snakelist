About

Beddome's Coral Snake (Calliophis beddomei) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in India. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
