About

DiCaprio’s Snail-eating Snake (Sibon irmelindicaprioae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, chocó rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
