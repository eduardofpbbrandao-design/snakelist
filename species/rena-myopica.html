About

Rena myopica is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
