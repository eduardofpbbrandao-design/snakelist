About

Southeast Asian Bockadam (Cerberus schneiderii) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 232 places on iNaturalist.
