About

Short-tail Alpine Garter Snake (Thamnophis scaliger) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 82 places on iNaturalist.
