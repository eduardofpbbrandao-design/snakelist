About

Chapa Mountain Keelback (Opisthotropis jacobi) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, riverine forest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
