About

Beddome's Cat Snake (Boiga beddomei) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest and 16 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
