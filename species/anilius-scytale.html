About

Coral Cylinder Snakes (Anilius scytale) is a non-venomous snake of the family Aniliidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 127 places on iNaturalist.
