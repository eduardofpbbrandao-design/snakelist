About

Eastern Wolf Snake (Lycophidion semiannule) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Mozambique. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, mopane woodland. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
