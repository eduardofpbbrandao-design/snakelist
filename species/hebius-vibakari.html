About

Japanese Keelback (Hebius vibakari) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Japan, North Korea, South Korea. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 179 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • China: Henan, Jiangsu
  • Japan: Aichi Prefecture, Ehime Prefecture, Gifu Prefecture, Kanagawa Prefecture, Kyōto Prefecture, Kōchi Prefecture, Nara Prefecture, Saitama Prefecture, Tokyo
  • North Korea
  • South Korea: Daejeon, Gangwon, Gyeonggi, Jeju, North Chungcheong, North Gyeongsang, North Jeolla, South Chungcheong, South Gyeongsang, South Jeolla
