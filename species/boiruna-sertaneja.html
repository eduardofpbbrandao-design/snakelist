About

Boiruna sertaneja is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
