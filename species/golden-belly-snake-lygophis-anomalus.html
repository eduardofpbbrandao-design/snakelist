About

Golden-belly snake (Lygophis anomalus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Common: recorded in 246 places on iNaturalist.
