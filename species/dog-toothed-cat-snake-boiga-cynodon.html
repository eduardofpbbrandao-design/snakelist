About

Dog-toothed Cat Snake (Boiga cynodon) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 189 places on iNaturalist.
