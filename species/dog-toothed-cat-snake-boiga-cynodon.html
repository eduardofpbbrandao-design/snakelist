About

Dog-toothed Cat Snake (Boiga cynodon) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 189 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

Native range

  • Indonesia: Bali, Bangka, Belitung, Flores, Java, Kalimantan (Borneo), Komodo, Mentawai Islands, Nias, Riau Archipelago, Sumatra, Sumbawa
  • Malaysia: Johor, Kedah, Pahang, Perak, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor
  • Myanmar
  • Philippines: CALABARZON (Region IV-A), Central Luzon (Region III), Central Visayas (Region VII), MIMAROPA (Region IV-B), Western Visayas (Region VI)
