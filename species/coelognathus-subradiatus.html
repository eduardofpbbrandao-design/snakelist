About

Sunda Rat Snake (Coelognathus subradiatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Timor-Leste. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
