About

Texas Coral Snake (Micrurus tener) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 850 places on iNaturalist.
