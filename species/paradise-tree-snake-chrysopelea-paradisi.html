About

Paradise Tree Snake (Chrysopelea paradisi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 247 places on iNaturalist.
