About

Mopane Snake (Hemirhagerrhis nototaenia) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 19 more. Common: recorded in 89 places on iNaturalist.
