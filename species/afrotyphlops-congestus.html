About

Blotched Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops congestus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
