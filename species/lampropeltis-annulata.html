About

Mexican Milksnake (Lampropeltis annulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 119 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Mexico: Coahuila, Hidalgo, Nuevo León, San Luis Potosí, Tamaulipas
