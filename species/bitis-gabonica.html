About

Gaboon Viper (Bitis gabonica) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 19 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.
