About

Triangle Keelback (Xenochrophis trianguligerus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Cambodia, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 261 places on iNaturalist.
