About

Laurent’s Green Tree-snake (Dipsadoboa viridis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
