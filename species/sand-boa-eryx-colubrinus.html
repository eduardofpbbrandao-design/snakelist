About

Sand Boa (Eryx colubrinus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Chad, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 33 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
