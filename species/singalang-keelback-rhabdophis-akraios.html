About

Singalang Keelback (Rhabdophis akraios) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
