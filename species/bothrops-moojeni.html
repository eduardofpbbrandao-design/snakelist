About

Brazilian Lancehead (Bothrops moojeni) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 264 places on iNaturalist.

  • Bolivia
  • Brazil: Bahia, Distrito Federal, Goiás, Mato Grosso, Mato Grosso do Sul, Minas Gerais, Paraná, Piauí, São Paulo, Tocantins
