About

Halmahera Keelback (Tropidonophis punctiventris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as wetlands, marshes, freshwater marshes, swamps and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
