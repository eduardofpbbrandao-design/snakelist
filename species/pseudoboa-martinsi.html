About

Pale Collared Scarlet Snake (Pseudoboa martinsi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 4 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
