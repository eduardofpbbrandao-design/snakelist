About

Bougainville coral snake (Parapistocalamus hedigeri) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
