Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6 of 24Non-venomous: 18

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brown Spotted Pitviper Protobothrops mucrosquamatus, Chinese Green Tree Viper Trimeresurus stejnegeri, Chinese Moccasin Deinagkistrodon acutus, Dabie Mountains Pit Viper Protobothrops dabieshanensis, Many-banded Krait Bungarus multicinctus.

The other venomous species has mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for it.
