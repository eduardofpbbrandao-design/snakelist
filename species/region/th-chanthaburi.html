Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 10

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Cardamom Mountains Green Pitviper Trimeresurus cardamomensis, Green Keelback Rhabdophis nigrocinctus, Vogel's Pit Viper Trimeresurus vogeli.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
