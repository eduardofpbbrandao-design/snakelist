Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 19

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Ceylon Krait Bungarus ceylonicus, Ceylon Pit Viper Craspedocephalus trigonocephalus, Hump-nosed Moccasin Hypnale hypnale, Indian Cobra Naja naja, Russell's Viper Daboia russelii, Sri Lanka Humpnose Viper Hypnale nepa, Zara Hypnale zara.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
