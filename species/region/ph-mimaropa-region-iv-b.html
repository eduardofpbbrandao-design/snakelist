Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8 of 24Non-venomous: 16

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Common Sea Krait Laticauda laticaudata, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Schultze's Pit Viper Trimeresurus schultzei, Two-stripped Coral Snake Calliophis bilineatus, Zigzag-lined Water Snake Rhabdophis lineatus.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
