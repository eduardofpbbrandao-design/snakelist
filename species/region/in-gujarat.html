Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16Non-venomous: 26

10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Annulated Sea Snake Hydrophis cyanocinctus, Bombay Sea Snake Hydrophis mamillaris, Common Seasnake Hydrophis schistosus, Dark Blue-Banded Sea Snake Polyodontognathus caerulescens, Indian Cobra Naja naja, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Short Sea Snake Hydrophis curtus, Sind Krait Bungarus sindanus, Yellow Sea Snake Hydrophis spiralis, Yellow-bellied Sea Snake Hydrophis platurus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
