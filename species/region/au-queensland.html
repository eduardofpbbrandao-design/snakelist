Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Queensland in particular. The other 90 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Eastern Brown Snake Pseudonaja textilis: Most habitats except dense forest, including grassland, woodland, scrub and especially farmland and the outskirts of cities. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Green Tree Python Morelia viridis: Tropical rainforest. Adults live in the canopy, while young snakes hunt nearer the ground. Source: Wikipedia, Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan.
  • Inland Taipan Oxyuranus microlepidotus: Remote semi-arid country in the interior of Australia. Source: Taronga Conservation Society Australia.
  • Mainland Tiger Snake Notechis scutatus: Often near water - wetlands, creeks, swamps and coastal areas - but also grassland and forest. Source: Wikipedia.
