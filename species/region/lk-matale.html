Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9 of 23Non-venomous: 14

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blood-bellied Coralsnake Calliophis haematoetron, Ceylon Pit Viper Craspedocephalus trigonocephalus, Hump-nosed Moccasin Hypnale hypnale, Indian Cobra Naja naja, Lead Keelback Rhabdophis plumbicolor, Sri Lanka Humpnose Viper Hypnale nepa.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
