Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 10

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Indonesian Pit Viper Trimeresurus hageni, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Sumatra Pit Viper Trimeresurus sumatranus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
