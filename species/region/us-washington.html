What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Washington in particular. The other 11 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Common Gartersnake Thamnophis sirtalis: Usually close to water: along rivers, lakes and reservoirs, in wetlands and in the strips of plants beside streams.
