Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16Non-venomous: 19

13 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Andaman Cobra Naja sagittifera, Andaman pitviper Trimeresurus andersonii, Cantor's Pit Viper Trimeresurus cantori, Central Nicobar Pit Viper Trimeresurus mutabilis, Common Krait Bungarus caeruleus, Common sea krait Laticauda laticaudata, Common Seasnake Hydrophis schistosus, David's pitviper Trimeresurus davidi, Island Pit Viper Trimeresurus labialis, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, South Andaman Krait Bungarus andamanensis, Yellow-bellied Sea Snake Hydrophis platurus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
