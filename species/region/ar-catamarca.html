Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8 of 13Non-venomous: 5

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Argentinian Coral Snake Micrurus pyrrhocryptus, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Yararanata Bothrops ammodytoides.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
