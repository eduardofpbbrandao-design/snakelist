Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Valle del Cauca in particular. The other 55 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor: A very adaptable snake found in rainforest, dry forest, savanna, scrub and farmland, both on the ground and in trees. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan.
  • Fer-de-lance Bothrops asper: Mainly humid lowland forest, but it adapts well to disturbed land: plantations, pastures, riverbanks and the edges of towns. It has been recorded from sea level up to about 2,640 m. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Wikipedia.
