Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10 of 54Non-venomous: 44

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brazilian Coral Snake Micrurus decoratus, Cotiara Bothrops cotiara, Jararaca Bothrops jararaca, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Jararacussu Bothrops jararacussu, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Urutu Bothrops alternatus.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
