Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Chattogram in particular. The other 21 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Burmese Python Python bivittatus: A habitat generalist. In its native range it lives in rainforest, swamps, grasslands and marshes. In southern Florida the Everglades suit it well: a warm climate, a mix of trees, brush and marsh, plenty of prey and few large predators. Source: Cleveland Metroparks Zoo resource library, U.S. Geological Survey, Wetland and Aquatic Research Center, The Orianne Society.
  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
