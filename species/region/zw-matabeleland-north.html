Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 9

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Anchieta's Cobra Naja anchietae, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Twig Snake Thelotornis capensis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
