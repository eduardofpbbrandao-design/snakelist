Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Veracruz State in particular. The other 120 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Fer-de-lance Bothrops asper: Mainly humid lowland forest, but it adapts well to disturbed land: plantations, pastures, riverbanks and the edges of towns. It has been recorded from sea level up to about 2,640 m. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Wikipedia.
  • Western Diamond-backed Rattlesnake Crotalus atrox: Very adaptable. It lives in deserts, grasslands, scrubland, rocky canyons and even pine-oak forest, from coastal lowlands up to about 2,100 m (7,000 ft). It shelters in rock crevices and in burrows dug by mammals. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Pajarito Environmental Education Center (PEEC) Nature.
