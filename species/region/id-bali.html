Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 14

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Burmese Python Python bivittatus, Indonesian Cobra Naja sputatrix, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Short Sea Snake Hydrophis curtus, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis, White-lipped Island Pitviper Trimeresurus insularis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
