Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16 of 39Non-venomous: 23

11 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Beaked Burrowing Asp Atractaspis duerdeni, Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera.

The other 5 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Gauteng in particular. The other 37 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis: Mostly a ground-dwelling snake of open country: moist and dry savanna, woodland, rocky hills, riverine forest and coastal bush. It hides in holes in the ground, rock crevices, hollow trees, old termite mounds and under tree stumps, and often returns to the same shelter for a long time. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, African Reptiles & Venom.
  • Puff Adder Bitis arietans: Open country such as savanna and grassland. Source: Wikipedia.
