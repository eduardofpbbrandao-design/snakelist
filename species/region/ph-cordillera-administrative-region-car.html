Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR) is a region of the Philippines. SnakeList records where snakes live by region, not by exact spot, so a species counted here may live in only part of it. The counts are unique living species; fossils, extinct species and introduced snakes are listed apart. All snakes of the Philippines.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 4 of 9Non-venomous: 5

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Barbour's Water Snake Rhabdophis barbouri, Barred Coral Snake (calligaster) Hemibungarus calligaster, Philippine Pitviper Trimeresurus flavomaculatus.

The other venomous species has mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for it.
