Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7Non-venomous: 14

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Green Keelback Rhabdophis nigrocinctus, Peters' Sea Snake Hydrophis bituberculatus, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Shore Pit Viper Trimeresurus purpureomaculatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
