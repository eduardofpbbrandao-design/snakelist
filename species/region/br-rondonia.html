Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16 of 60Non-venomous: 44

14 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Boicora Coral Snake Micrurus boicora, Brazil's Lancehead Bothrops brazili, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Fer-de-Lance Bothrops atrox, Green Jararaca Bothrops bilineatus, Hemprich's Coral Snake Micrurus hemprichii, Hognose Lancehead Bothrocophias hyoprora, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Mato Grosso Lancehead Bothrops mattogrossensis, Redtail Coral Snake Micrurus mipartitus, Small-eyed Toadhead Bothrocophias microphthalmus, Speckled Forest Pit Viper Bothrops taeniatus, White-banded Coral Snake Micrurus albicinctus.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
