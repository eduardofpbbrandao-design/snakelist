Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Sindh in particular. The other 9 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Common Krait Bungarus caeruleus: A wide range of lowland habitats, including fields, scrub and villages. It shelters in termite mounds, rat holes, brick piles and similar hiding places, and regularly enters houses. Source: Wikipedia, Journal of Herpetology 54(1).
  • Saw-scaled Viper Echis carinatus: Dry open places on a wide range of ground: sand, rock, soft soil and scrubland. It often hides under loose stones, and is sometimes found climbing in bushes up to about 2 m above the ground. Source: Wikipedia.
