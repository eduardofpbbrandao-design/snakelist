Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10 of 41Non-venomous: 31

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brazilian Lancehead Bothrops moojeni, Cerrado Lancehead (pauloensis) Bothrops pauloensis, Mato Grosso Lancehead Bothrops mattogrossensis, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Urutu Bothrops alternatus, Yellow Anaconda Eunectes notaeus.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
