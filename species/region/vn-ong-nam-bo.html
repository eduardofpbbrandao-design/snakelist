Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 23

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Burmese Python Python bivittatus, Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra Naja siamensis, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Malayan Pit Viper Calloselasma rhodostoma, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) Calliophis maculiceps, White-lipped Pitviper Trimeresurus albolabris.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
