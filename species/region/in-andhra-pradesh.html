Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11 of 32Non-venomous: 21

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Common Bamboo Viper Craspedocephalus gramineus, Common Seasnake Hydrophis schistosus, Indian Rock Python Python molurus, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Short Sea Snake Hydrophis curtus, Yellow Sea Snake Hydrophis spiralis.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Andhra Pradesh in particular. The other 31 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
