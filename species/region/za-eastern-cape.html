13 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Albany Adder Bitis albanica, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mountain Adder Bitis atropos, Plain Mountain Adder Bitis inornata, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Red Adder Bitis rubida, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus, Twig Snake Thelotornis capensis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
