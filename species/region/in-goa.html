Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 12Non-venomous: 16

11 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Annulated Sea Snake Hydrophis cyanocinctus, Bombay Sea Snake Hydrophis mamillaris, Castoe's Coralsnake Calliophis castoe, Common Bamboo Viper Craspedocephalus gramineus, Common Seasnake Hydrophis schistosus, Dark Blue-Banded Sea Snake Polyodontognathus caerulescens, Indian Rock Python Python molurus, Ornate Sea Snake Hydrophis ornatus, Short Sea Snake Hydrophis curtus, Yellow Sea Snake Hydrophis spiralis, Yellow-bellied Sea Snake Hydrophis platurus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
