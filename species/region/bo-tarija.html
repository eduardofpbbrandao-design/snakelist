Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 4Non-venomous: 4

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bolivian Coral Snake (serranus) Micrurus serranus, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Jonathan's Lancehead Bothrops jonathani.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
