Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 1

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Jararacussu Bothrops jararacussu, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Uruguayan coralsnake Micrurus altirostris, Urutu Bothrops alternatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
