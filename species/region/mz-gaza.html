Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8 of 18Non-venomous: 10

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
