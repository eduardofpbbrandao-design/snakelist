Snakes of La Pampa

A province of Argentina

14 living snake species are recorded in La Pampa, a province of Argentina, in SnakeList's data: 6 venomous and 8 non-venomous, from 4 families. 49 more are possible: recorded in Argentina without province detail.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6 of 14Non-venomous: 8

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Argentinian Coral Snake Micrurus pyrrhocryptus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Yararanata Bothrops ammodytoides.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
