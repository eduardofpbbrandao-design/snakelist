Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 14

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Dwarf Puff Adder Bitis peringueyi, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
