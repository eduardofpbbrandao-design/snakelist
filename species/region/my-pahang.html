10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Blue-necked Keelback Rhabdophis rhodomelas, Clouded Pit Viper Trimeresurus nebularis, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Indonesian Pit Viper Trimeresurus hageni, Malayan Brown Pit Viper Ovophis convictus, Red-headed Krait Bungarus flaviceps, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Sabah Pit Viper Trimeresurus sabahi, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
