Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 21

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Argentinian Coral Snake Micrurus pyrrhocryptus, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Mesopotamian coral snake Micrurus baliocoryphus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Urutu Bothrops alternatus, Yellow Anaconda Eunectes notaeus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
