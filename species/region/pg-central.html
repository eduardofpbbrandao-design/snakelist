Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 8

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Whip Snake Demansia vestigiata, Coastal Taipan Oxyuranus scutellatus, Grey-naped Snake Furina tristis, Guinea's Flat-tail Sea Snake Laticauda guineai, Olive Sea Snake Aipysurus laevis, Papuan Blacksnake Pseudechis papuanus, Smooth-scaled Death Adder Acanthophis laevis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
