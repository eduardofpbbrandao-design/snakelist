Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 14

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Cardamom Mountains Green Pitviper Trimeresurus cardamomensis, Green Keelback Rhabdophis nigrocinctus, Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra Naja siamensis, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Malayan Pit Viper Calloselasma rhodostoma, Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) Calliophis maculiceps, White-lipped Pitviper Trimeresurus albolabris.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
