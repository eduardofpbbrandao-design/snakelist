Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6 of 12Non-venomous: 6

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Habu Pit Viper Protobothrops himalayanus, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Orange-collared Keelback Rhabdophis himalayanus, Tibetan Pit Viper Trimeresurus tibetanus.

The other venomous species has mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for it.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Bagmati Zone in particular. The other 11 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
