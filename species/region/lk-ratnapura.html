Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7 of 28Non-venomous: 21

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blossom Krait Rhabdophis ceylonensis, Ceylon Pit Viper Craspedocephalus trigonocephalus, Hump-nosed Moccasin Hypnale hypnale, Indian Rock Python Python molurus, Russell's Viper Daboia russelii, Zara Hypnale zara.

The other venomous species has mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for it.
