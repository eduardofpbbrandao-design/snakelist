Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 6

10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Coral Shield Cobra Aspidelaps lubricus, Desert Mountain Adder Bitis xeropaga, Dwarf Puff Adder Bitis peringueyi, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Many-horned Adder Bitis cornuta, Namaqua Dwarf Adder Bitis schneideri, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Western Barred Spitting Cobra Naja nigricincta.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
