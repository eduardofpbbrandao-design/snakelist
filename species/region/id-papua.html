Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Papua in particular. The other 45 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Green Tree Python Morelia viridis: Tropical rainforest. Adults live in the canopy, while young snakes hunt nearer the ground. Source: Wikipedia, Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan.
  • Yellow-bellied Sea Snake Hydrophis platurus: Completely pelagic (living in the open ocean). It is often found along slicks and drift lines - the lines where surface currents meet and floating debris collects - and it is the only air-breathing marine predator known to hunt at these surface features. Source: Wikipedia.
