Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 15Non-venomous: 21

14 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Bush Viper Atheris squamigera, African Hairy Bush Viper Atheris hispida, African Rock Python Python sebae, African Tree Cobra Pseudohaje goldii, Black-necked Spitting Cobra Naja nigricollis, Brown Forest Cobra Naja subfulva, Forest Night Adder Causus lichtensteinii, Gaboon Viper Bitis gabonica, Great Lakes Bush Viper Atheris nitschei, Green Night Adder Causus resimus, Jameson's Mamba Dendroaspis jamesoni, Kirtland's Twig Snake Thelotornis kirtlandii, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) Bitis nasicornis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
