Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 14Non-venomous: 22

10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bamboo Pitviper Trimeresurus erythrurus, Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Greater Black Krait Bungarus niger, Habu Pit Viper Protobothrops himalayanus, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, MacLelland's Coral Snake Sinomicrurus macclellandi, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Northeastern Hill Krait Bungarus bungaroides, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Orange-collared Keelback Rhabdophis himalayanus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
