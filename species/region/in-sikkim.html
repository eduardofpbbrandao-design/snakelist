A state of India

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 14 of 36Non-venomous: 22

10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bamboo Pitviper Trimeresurus erythrurus, Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Greater Black Krait Bungarus niger, Habu Pit Viper Protobothrops himalayanus, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, MacLelland's Coral Snake Sinomicrurus macclellandi, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Northeastern Hill Krait Bungarus bungaroides, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Orange-collared Keelback Rhabdophis himalayanus.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Sikkim in particular. The other 35 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
