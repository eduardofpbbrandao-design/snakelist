Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 18

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Greater Black Krait Bungarus niger, Himalayan Pitviper Gloydius himalayanus, Indian Cobra Naja naja, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Nepal Pitviper Trimeresurus septentrionalis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
