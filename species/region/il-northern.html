Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6 of 18Non-venomous: 12

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Israeli Mole Viper Atractaspis engaddensis, Lebanon Viper Montivipera bornmuelleri, Palestine Saw-scaled Viper Echis coloratus, Palestine Viper Daboia palaestinae.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
