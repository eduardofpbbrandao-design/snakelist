Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 7

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mountain Adder Bitis atropos, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera, Snouted Night Adder Causus defilippii.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
