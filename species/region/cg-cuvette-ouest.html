Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 5Non-venomous: 6

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Bush Viper Atheris squamigera, African Rock Python Python sebae, Angolan Garter Snake Elapsoidea semiannulata, Gaboon Viper Bitis gabonica.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
