Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Ontario in particular. The other 11 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Eastern Hognose Snake Heterodon platirhinos: Open areas with sandy, loose soils. Loose soil is important for nesting and egg-laying, and for digging out prey. Source: Government of Canada, Species at Risk Public Registry, Wikipedia.
  • Milk Snake Lampropeltis triangulum: Rocky hillsides, open woodland, woodland edges, clearings and old fields, and also farm buildings and rock outcrops. It spends winter in rock crevices, mammal burrows or building foundations. Source: The Canadian Encyclopedia, Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife.
