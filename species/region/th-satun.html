Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7Non-venomous: 8

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Malayan Pit Viper Calloselasma rhodostoma, Orange-lipped Keelback Rhabdophis flaviceps, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Shore Pit Viper Trimeresurus purpureomaculatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
