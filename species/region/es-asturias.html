Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Asturias in particular. The other 5 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Iberian Grass Snake Natrix astreptophora: Slow-flowing and still fresh water - streams, rivers, ponds and their banks - usually in the shallows. In Iberian rivers it is more numerous where there are no invasive fish. Source: Fishipedia, Wikipedia.
  • Viperine Snake Natrix maura: Closely tied to fresh water: rivers, streams, ponds, lakes, irrigation channels and other wetlands, where it hunts in the water and basks on the banks. Source: U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, Wikipedia.

Who described them

The scientists who first described the species above, and when.

  • Smooth Snake Coronella austriaca - described by Laurenti in 1768
  • Viperine Snake Natrix maura - described by Linnaeus in 1758 (under another genus name)
  • Montpellier Snake Malpolon monspessulanus - described by Hermann in 1804 (under another genus name)
  • Ladder Snake Zamenis scalaris - described by Schinz in 1822 (under another genus name)
  • Southern Smooth Snake Coronella girondica - described by Daudin in 1803 (under another genus name)
  • Iberian Grass Snake Natrix astreptophora - described by Seoane in 1884 (under another genus name)
  • Seoane's Viper Vipera seoanei - described by Lataste in 1879
