Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Guerrero in particular. The other 89 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Blackneck Garter Snake Thamnophis cyrtopsis: Near water in desert scrub, grassland, chaparral and woodland. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Colombian Boa Boa imperator: Prefers rainforest, which gives it humidity, warmth, cover and plenty of prey. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Fiddle-string Snake Imantodes cenchoa: Cool, moist forests and rainforests, from sea level to about 1,700 m; often in low plants such as coffee bushes and bromeliads. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Green Rat Snake (triaspis) Senticolis triaspis: Evergreen forest and grassland. Source: Wikipedia.
  • Western Ribbonsnake Thamnophis proximus: Many habitats, usually not far from water, from sea level to nearly 2,500 m. Source: Wikipedia.
