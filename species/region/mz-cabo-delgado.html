Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8 of 16Non-venomous: 8

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Boulenger's Garter Snake Elapsoidea boulengeri, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
