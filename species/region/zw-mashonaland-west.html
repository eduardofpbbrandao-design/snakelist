Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10 of 17Non-venomous: 7

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Boulenger's Garter Snake Elapsoidea boulengeri, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Mashonaland West in particular. The other 15 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis: Mostly a ground-dwelling snake of open country: moist and dry savanna, woodland, rocky hills, riverine forest and coastal bush. It hides in holes in the ground, rock crevices, hollow trees, old termite mounds and under tree stumps, and often returns to the same shelter for a long time. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, African Reptiles & Venom.
  • Puff Adder Bitis arietans: Open country such as savanna and grassland. Source: Wikipedia.
