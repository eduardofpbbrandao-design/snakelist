Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 21 of 81Non-venomous: 60

15 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Brazilian Coralsnake Micrurus brasiliensis, Brazilian Lancehead Bothrops moojeni, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Cerrado Lancehead (pauloensis) Bothrops pauloensis, Green Anaconda Eunectes murinus, Jararaca Bothrops jararaca, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Lutz's Cerrado Lancehead Bothrops lutzi, Marbled Lancehead Bothrops marmoratus, Mato Grosso Lancehead Bothrops mattogrossensis, Sao Paulo Lancehead Bothrops itapetiningae, South American Coral Snake Micrurus lemniscatus, Urutu Bothrops alternatus.

The other 6 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
