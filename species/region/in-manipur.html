Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11 of 20Non-venomous: 9

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bamboo Pitviper Trimeresurus erythrurus, Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Maya's Pit Viper Trimeresurus mayaae, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Northeastern Hill Krait Bungarus bungaroides, Suzhen’s Krait Bungarus suzhenae.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Manipur in particular. The other 19 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
