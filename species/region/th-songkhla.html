Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11 of 34Non-venomous: 23

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Indonesian Pit Viper Trimeresurus hageni, Malayan Pit Viper Calloselasma rhodostoma, Red-headed Krait Bungarus flaviceps, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) Calliophis maculiceps, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
