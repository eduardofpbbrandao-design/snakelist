Venomous: 18Non-venomous: 19

12 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Beaked Burrowing Asp Atractaspis duerdeni, Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Coral Shield Cobra Aspidelaps lubricus, Desert Mountain Adder Bitis xeropaga, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Many-horned Adder Bitis cornuta, Namaqua Dwarf Adder Bitis schneideri, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Red Adder Bitis rubida.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
