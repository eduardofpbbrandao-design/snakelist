Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9 of 13Non-venomous: 4

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Dwarf Puff Adder Bitis peringueyi, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Western Barred Spitting Cobra Naja nigricincta.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Hardap in particular. The other 11 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis: Mostly a ground-dwelling snake of open country: moist and dry savanna, woodland, rocky hills, riverine forest and coastal bush. It hides in holes in the ground, rock crevices, hollow trees, old termite mounds and under tree stumps, and often returns to the same shelter for a long time. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, African Reptiles & Venom.
  • Puff Adder Bitis arietans: Open country such as savanna and grassland. Source: Wikipedia.
