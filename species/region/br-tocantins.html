Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9 of 25Non-venomous: 16

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Amazon Coral Snake Micrurus spixii, Brazilian Coralsnake Micrurus brasiliensis, Brazilian Lancehead Bothrops moojeni, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Lutz's Cerrado Lancehead Bothrops lutzi, Mato Grosso Lancehead Bothrops mattogrossensis, Speckled Forest Pit Viper Bothrops taeniatus.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
