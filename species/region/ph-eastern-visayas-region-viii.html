Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 5 of 12Non-venomous: 7

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Philippine Pitviper Trimeresurus flavomaculatus, Samar Cobra Naja samarensis, White-lined Water Snake Rhabdophis auriculatus, Zigzag-lined Water Snake Rhabdophis lineatus.

The other venomous species has mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for it.
