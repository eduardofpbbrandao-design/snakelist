Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9 of 16Non-venomous: 7

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Central Asian Cobra Naja oxiana, Chamba Pitviper Gloydius chambensis, Himalayan Pitviper Gloydius himalayanus, Levantine Viper Macrovipera lebetinus, Nepal Pitviper Trimeresurus septentrionalis, Saw-scaled Viper Echis carinatus.

The other 3 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
