Snakes of Sumatra

An island of Indonesia

135 living snake species are recorded in Sumatra, an island of Indonesia, in SnakeList's data: 40 venomous and 95 non-venomous, from 13 families. 29 more are possible: recorded in Indonesia without island detail.

Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Sumatra in particular. The other 133 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah: Mainly a forest snake: rainforest, bamboo thickets and mangrove swamps, often near streams and other water, from the lowlands up to about 2,000 m. It also turns up in plantations and farmland next to forest. Source: Young People's Trust for the Environment, Smithsonian's National Zoo.
  • Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus: Tropical forest, usually near rivers and other water. Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Active Wild.
