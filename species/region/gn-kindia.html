Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7 of 16Non-venomous: 9

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Mole Viper (aterrima) Atractaspis aterrima, Rhinoceros Viper Bitis rhinoceros, Spotted Night Adder Causus maculatus, West African Banded Cobra Naja savannula, Western Green Mamba Dendroaspis viridis.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
