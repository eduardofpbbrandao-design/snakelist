Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Sonora in particular. The other 66 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • California Kingsnake Lampropeltis californiae: Very adaptable: woodland, chaparral, grassland, desert, farmland and suburban areas, often near rocks or streams. Source: Wikipedia, CaliforniaHerps.com.
  • Sidewinder Crotalus cerastes: Sandy flats with creosote bush and mesquite, and occasionally rocky slopes. Source: U.S. National Park Service, Death Valley.
  • Western Diamond-backed Rattlesnake Crotalus atrox: Very adaptable. It lives in deserts, grasslands, scrubland, rocky canyons and even pine-oak forest, from coastal lowlands up to about 2,100 m (7,000 ft). It shelters in rock crevices and in burrows dug by mammals. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Pajarito Environmental Education Center (PEEC) Nature.
