Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Amapá in particular. The other 35 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor: A very adaptable snake found in rainforest, dry forest, savanna, scrub and farmland, both on the ground and in trees. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan.
  • Bushmaster Lachesis muta: Lowland tropical rainforest, where it spends much of its time on the forest floor. Source: Wikipedia, Los Angeles Zoo.
  • Emerald Tree Boa Corallus caninus: Tropical rainforest, where it lives in the trees and stays hidden among the foliage, its colour giving it excellent camouflage. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Wikipedia.
  • Rainbow Boa Epicrates cenchria: Mainly humid tropical forest and woodland; it is also found in more open, savanna-like country in parts of its range. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Lincoln Children's Zoo.
