Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 30

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Brazil's Lancehead (oligobalius) Bothrops oligobalius, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Dark Spotted Anaconda Eunectes deschauenseei, Fer-de-Lance Bothrops atrox, South American Coral Snake Micrurus lemniscatus, Trinidad coralsnake Micrurus diutius.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
