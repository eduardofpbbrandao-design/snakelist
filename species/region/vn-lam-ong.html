Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 27

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Krait Bungarus candidus, Brown Spotted Pitviper Protobothrops mucrosquamatus, Guangxi Coral Snake Sinomicrurus peinani, Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) Calliophis maculiceps, Tonkin pitviper Ovophis tonkinensis, Vogel's Pit Viper Trimeresurus vogeli, White-lipped Pitviper Trimeresurus albolabris.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
