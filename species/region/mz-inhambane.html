Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9Non-venomous: 6

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Brown Forest Cobra Naja subfulva, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
