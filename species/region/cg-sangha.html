Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 6

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Bush Viper Atheris squamigera, Jameson's Mamba Dendroaspis jamesoni, Kirtland's Twig Snake Thelotornis kirtlandii, Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) Bitis nasicornis, Ringed Water Cobra Naja annulata.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
