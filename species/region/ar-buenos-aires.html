View on SnakeList map

Buenos Aires Province is a province of Argentina. SnakeList records where snakes live by province, not by exact spot, so a species counted here may live in only part of it. The counts are unique living species; fossils, extinct species and introduced snakes are listed apart. SnakeList’s map has 2 areas called Buenos Aires; they are counted together here. The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
