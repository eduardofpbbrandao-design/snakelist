Snakes of Riau Archipelago

An island of Indonesia

11 living snake species are recorded in Riau Archipelago, an island of Indonesia, in SnakeList's data: 2 venomous and 9 non-venomous, from 2 families. 29 more are possible: recorded in Indonesia without island detail.
