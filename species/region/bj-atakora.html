Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 9 of 20Non-venomous: 11

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Rock Python Python sebae, African Saw-scaled Viper Echis ocellatus, Angolan Garter Snake Elapsoidea semiannulata, Black-necked Spitting Cobra Naja nigricollis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Spotted Night Adder Causus maculatus, West African Banded Cobra Naja savannula.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
