Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 22

8 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Jerdon's Pitviper Protobothrops jerdonii, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Maya's pit viper Trimeresurus mayaae, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Northeastern Hill Krait Bungarus bungaroides, Pope's Bamboo Pit Viper Trimeresurus popeiorum, Salazar's Pit Viper Trimeresurus salazar.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
