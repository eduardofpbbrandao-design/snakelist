Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 13 of 32Non-venomous: 19

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Boulenger's Garter Snake Elapsoidea boulengeri, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Lowland Swamp Viper Proatheris superciliaris, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Night Adder Causus defilippii.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
