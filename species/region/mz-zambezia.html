Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7 of 13Non-venomous: 6

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Mount Mabu Bush Viper Atheris mabuensis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Night Adder Causus defilippii.

The other 2 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
