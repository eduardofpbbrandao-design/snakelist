Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 13Non-venomous: 29

12 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Brown Spotted Pitviper Protobothrops mucrosquamatus, Burmese Python Python bivittatus, Chinese Cobra Naja atra, Chinese Green Tree Viper Trimeresurus stejnegeri, Hainan Island Keelback Snake Rhabdophis confusus, Hainan Keelback Rhabdophis adleri, Kellog's Coral Snake Sinomicrurus kelloggi, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Many-banded Krait Bungarus multicinctus, Tonkin pitviper Ovophis tonkinensis, White-lipped Pitviper Trimeresurus albolabris.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
