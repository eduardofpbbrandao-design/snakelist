Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Durango in particular. The other 54 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Western Diamond-backed Rattlesnake Crotalus atrox: Very adaptable. It lives in deserts, grasslands, scrubland, rocky canyons and even pine-oak forest, from coastal lowlands up to about 2,100 m (7,000 ft). It shelters in rock crevices and in burrows dug by mammals. Source: Animal Diversity Web, University of Michigan, Pajarito Environmental Education Center (PEEC) Nature.
