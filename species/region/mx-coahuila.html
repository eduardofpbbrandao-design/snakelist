Habitats from reviewed species accounts

What SnakeList’s reviewed, sourced accounts say about where each species lives in general, across its range. They do not describe Coahuila in particular. The other 47 species here have no reviewed account yet, so nothing is said about their habitats.

  • Western Diamond-backed Rattlesnake Crotalus atrox: Very adaptable. It lives in deserts, grasslands, scrubland, rocky canyons and even pine-oak forest, from coastal lowlands up to about 2,100 m (7,000 ft). It shelters in rock crevices and in burrows dug by mammals.
