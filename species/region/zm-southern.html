Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7Non-venomous: 8

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
