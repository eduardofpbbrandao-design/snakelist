Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16Non-venomous: 10

10 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Anchieta's Cobra Naja anchietae, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Boulenger's Garter Snake Elapsoidea boulengeri, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Speckled Shield Cobra Aspidelaps scutatus, Twig Snake Thelotornis capensis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
