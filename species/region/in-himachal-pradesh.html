Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8 of 16Non-venomous: 8

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Central Asian Cobra Naja oxiana, Chamba Pitviper Gloydius chambensis, Himalayan Pitviper Gloydius himalayanus, Nepal Pitviper Trimeresurus septentrionalis.

The other 4 venomous species have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
