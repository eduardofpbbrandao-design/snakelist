Redback Coffee Snake

Ninia sebae (Duméril, Bibron & Duméril, 1854)

The Redback Coffee Snake is a small ground snake of south-eastern Mexico and Central America. It looks a bit like a coral snake but is not venomous and rarely bites.[1]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Forest and savanna from sea level to about 2,200 m.[1]

Behaviour

Relies on hiding, escaping and bluffing: when alarmed it may flatten its whole body or freeze.[1]

Diet

Earthworms, slugs, small land snails and caecilians.[1]

Reproduction

Lays eggs.[1]

Where the name comes from

Redback Coffee Snake: ‘Coffee Snake’ – it is often found in coffee plantations.
