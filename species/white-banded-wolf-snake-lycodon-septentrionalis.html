White-banded Wolf Snake

Lycodon septentrionalis

White-banded Wolf Snake (Lycodon septentrionalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Laos, Myanmar and Vietnam.

What the name means

Lycodon: from the Greek lykos (wolf) and odon (tooth) - for the long, fang-like front teeth[1]
