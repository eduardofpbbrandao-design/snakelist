About

Black forest cobra (Naja guineensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Sierra Leone and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
