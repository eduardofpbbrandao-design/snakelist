About

Plainbelly Water Snake (Nerodia erythrogaster) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 3701 places on iNaturalist.
