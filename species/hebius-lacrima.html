About

Crying Keelback (Hebius lacrima) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 19 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
