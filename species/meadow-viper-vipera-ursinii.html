Meadow Viper

Vipera ursinii

Meadow Viper (Vipera ursinii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia and 9 more.

What the name means

Vipera: probably from the Latin vivus (alive) and pario (to give birth) - because most vipers bear live young[1]
