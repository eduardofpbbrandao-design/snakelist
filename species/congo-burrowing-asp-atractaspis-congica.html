About

Congo Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis congica) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Namibia and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
