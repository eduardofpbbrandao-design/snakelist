About

Sauvage's Snail-eater (Dipsas albifrons) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 20 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
