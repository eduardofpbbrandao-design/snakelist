About

Black Ground Snake (Atractus elaps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 39 places on iNaturalist.
