About

Royal snake (Spalerosophis atriceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
