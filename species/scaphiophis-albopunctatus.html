About

African Shovel-nosed Snake (Scaphiophis albopunctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
