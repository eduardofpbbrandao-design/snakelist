About

Brahminy Blindsnake (Indotyphlops braminus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in American Samoa, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Comoros and 22 more. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Florida, Hawaii), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 51 more. Common: recorded in 2300 places on iNaturalist.
