About

Brown Spotted Pitviper (Protobothrops mucrosquamatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, India, Laos, Myanmar, Taiwan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 604 places on iNaturalist.
