Aurora House Snake

Lamprophis aurora

Aurora House Snake (Lamprophis aurora) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho and South Africa.

Where the name comes from

Lamprophis: ‘shiny snake’, from the Greek roots lampr- ‘shiny’ + -ophis ‘snake’

aurora: ‘dawn’

Aurora House Snake: ‘House Snake’ – it is often found near houses.
