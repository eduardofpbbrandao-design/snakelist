About

Aurora House Snake (Lamprophis aurora) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, coastal forest, dry woodland, dry savanna and 15 more. Common: recorded in 83 places on iNaturalist.
