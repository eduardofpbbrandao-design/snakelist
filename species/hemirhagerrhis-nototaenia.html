About

Mopane Snake (Hemirhagerrhis nototaenia) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 19 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 89 places on iNaturalist.
