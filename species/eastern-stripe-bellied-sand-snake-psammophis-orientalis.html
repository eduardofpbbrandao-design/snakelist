Eastern Stripe-bellied Sand Snake

Eastern Stripe-bellied Sand Snake (Psammophis orientalis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Sudan and 2 more.
