About

Eastern Stripe-bellied Sand Snake (Psammophis orientalis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Sudan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 112 places on iNaturalist.
