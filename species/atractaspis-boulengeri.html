About

Boulenger's Mole Viper (Atractaspis boulengeri) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
