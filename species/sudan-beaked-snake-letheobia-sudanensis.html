About

Sudan Beaked Snake (Letheobia sudanensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Sudan. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
