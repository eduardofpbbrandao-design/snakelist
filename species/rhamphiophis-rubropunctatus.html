About

Red Beaked Snake (Rhamphiophis rubropunctatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 20 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
