About

Werner's Garter Snake (Elapsoidea laticincta) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
