About

Southern Brown Egg Eater (Dasypeltis inornata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Eswatini, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 11 more. Recorded in 54 places on iNaturalist.
