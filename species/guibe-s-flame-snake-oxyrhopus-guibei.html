About

Guibe's Flame Snake (Oxyrhopus guibei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 334 places on iNaturalist.
