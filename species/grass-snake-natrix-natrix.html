About

Grass Snake (Natrix natrix) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bulgaria and 31 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 46 more. Common: recorded in 9242 places on iNaturalist.
