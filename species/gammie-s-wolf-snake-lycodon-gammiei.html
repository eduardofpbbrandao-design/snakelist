About

Gammie's Wolf Snake (Lycodon gammiei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 15 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
