About

Blomber’s Tree Boa (Corallus blombergi) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Colombia, Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
