About

Pale-headed Snake (Hoplocephalus bitorquatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 22 more. Recorded in 67 places on iNaturalist.
