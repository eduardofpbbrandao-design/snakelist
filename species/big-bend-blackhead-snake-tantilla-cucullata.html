About

Big Bend Blackhead Snake (Tantilla cucullata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest, scrubland and 11 more. Recorded in 47 places on iNaturalist.
