About

Feeble Gracile Blind-snake (Letheobia debilis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Central African Republic, South Sudan. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
