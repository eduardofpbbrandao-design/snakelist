About

Aesculapian False Coral Snake (Erythrolamprus aesculapii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 50 more. Common: recorded in 234 places on iNaturalist.
