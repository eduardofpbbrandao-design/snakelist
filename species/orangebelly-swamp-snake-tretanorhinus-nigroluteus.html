About

Orangebelly Swamp Snake (Tretanorhinus nigroluteus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala and 4 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
