About

Brazilian Sipo (Chironius laevicollis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
