About

Peter's Thread Snake (Leptotyphlops scutifrons) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Eswatini, Kenya, Lesotho and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 24 more. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.
