About

Lesser African Python (Python natalensis) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 48 more. Common: recorded in 555 places on iNaturalist.
