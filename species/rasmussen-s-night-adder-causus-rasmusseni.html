About

Rasmussen’s Night Adder (Causus rasmusseni) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Malawi, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, woodland, open woodland, savanna and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
