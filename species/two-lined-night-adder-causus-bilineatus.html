About

Two-lined Night Adder (Causus bilineatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
