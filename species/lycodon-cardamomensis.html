About

Cardamom Mountains Wolf Snake (Lycodon cardamomensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 10 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
