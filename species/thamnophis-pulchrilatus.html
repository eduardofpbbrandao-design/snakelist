About

Yellow-throated gartersnake (Thamnophis pulchrilatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 39 more. Common: recorded in 157 places on iNaturalist.
