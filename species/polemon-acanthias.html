About

Reinhardt's Snake-eater (Polemon acanthias) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Nigeria and 2 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
