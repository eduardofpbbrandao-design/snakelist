About

Dwarf Beaked Snake (Dipsina multimaculata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Botswana, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, dry savanna, shrubland, scrubland and 10 more. Recorded in 68 places on iNaturalist.
