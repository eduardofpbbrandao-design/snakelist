About

Pacific seasnake (Hydrophis pacificus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Guinea, Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
