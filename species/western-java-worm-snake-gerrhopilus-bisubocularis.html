About

Western Java Worm Snake (Gerrhopilus bisubocularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Gerrhopilidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
