Where the name comes from

capensis: ‘from the Cape (South Africa)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Common File Snake: ‘Common’ – it is the commonest or best-known of its kind; ‘File Snake’ – its rough, baggy skin feels like a file (the metal tool).
