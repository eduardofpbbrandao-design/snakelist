About

Peters' Sea Snake (Hydrophis bituberculatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Sri Lanka, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
