About

Sinharaja tree snake (Dendrelaphis effrenis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, secondary forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
