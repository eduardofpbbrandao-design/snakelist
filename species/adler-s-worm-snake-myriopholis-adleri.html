About

Adler's Worm Snake (Myriopholis adleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 18 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
