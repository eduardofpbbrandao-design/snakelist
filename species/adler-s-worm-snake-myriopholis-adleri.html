Adler's Worm Snake

Myriopholis adleri

Adler's Worm Snake (Myriopholis adleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 5 more.

    Where the name comes from

    adleri: named after a person called Adler

    Adler's Worm Snake: ‘Adler's’ – named after a person called Adler; ‘Worm Snake’ – it is small and looks like a worm.
