About

Bolivian Tree Snake (Dipsas turgida) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 83 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Argentina: Chaco, Corrientes, Entre Ríos, Formosa, Jujuy, Misiones, Salta, Santa Fe, Santiago del Estero, Tucumán
  • Bolivia: Santa Cruz
  • Brazil: Mato Grosso, Rio Grande do Sul
  • Paraguay: Central, Itapúa, Ñeembucú
  • Uruguay: Paysandú, Río Negro, Salto
