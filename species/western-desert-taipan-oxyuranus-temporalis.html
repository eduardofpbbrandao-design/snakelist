Where the name comes from

Oxyuranus: from the Greek oxys (sharp, needle-like) and ouranos (arch, vault of the sky) - for a needle-like point on the arch of the palate[1]

temporalis: ‘of the temples’

Western Desert Taipan: ‘Western’ – where it lives (in the west); ‘Desert’ – it lives in deserts.
