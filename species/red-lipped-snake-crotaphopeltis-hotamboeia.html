Red-lipped Snake

Crotaphopeltis hotamboeia

Red-lipped Snake (Crotaphopeltis hotamboeia) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi and 33 more.
