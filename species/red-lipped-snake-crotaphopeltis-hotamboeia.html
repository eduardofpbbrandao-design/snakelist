About

Red-lipped Snake (Crotaphopeltis hotamboeia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi and 33 more. Common: recorded in 728 places on iNaturalist.
