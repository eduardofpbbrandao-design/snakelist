About

Short-nosed Groundsnake (Taeniophallus brevirostris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Peru and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 35 places on iNaturalist.
