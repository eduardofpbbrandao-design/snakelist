About

Smooth Slug Snake (Asthenodipsas laevis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 66 places on iNaturalist.
