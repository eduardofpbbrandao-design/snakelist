About

White-bellied Worm Snake (Myriopholis albiventer) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Burkina Faso, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Mali. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
