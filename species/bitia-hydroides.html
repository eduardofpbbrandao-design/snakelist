About

Keel-bellied Water Snake (Bitia hydroides) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 18 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
