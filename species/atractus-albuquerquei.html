About

Albuquerque Ground Snake (Atractus albuquerquei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 17 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
