Green Rat Snake (triaspis)

Senticolis triaspis (Cope, 1866)

The Green Rat Snake is a slender, non-venomous snake from southern Arizona and New Mexico through Mexico to Costa Rica. It is the only species in its genus.[1,2]

How to recognise it

Size

Native: South-eastern Arizona and south-western New Mexico, Mexico, and Central America to Costa Rica.[1,2]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Evergreen forest and grassland.[1]

Behaviour

Mainly active by day.[1]

Diet

Lizards, birds and bats, killed by squeezing.[1]

Reproduction

Lays up to nine eggs.[1]
