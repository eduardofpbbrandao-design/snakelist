About

Western Hognose Snake (Heterodon nasicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 734 places on iNaturalist.
