Greater Green Snake

Ptyas major (Günther, 1858)

The Chinese Green Snake is a slender, bright green, gentle snake of China and nearby countries. It rarely bites.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Plain bright green, slim snake.[1]

Appearance

Bright green above and greenish-yellow below, with smooth scales.[1]

Size

Adults: About 75 to 90 cm, sometimes up to 120 cm.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: Central and southern China, Taiwan, northern Vietnam and Laos.[1,2]

Behaviour

Active by day and partly lives in trees and bushes.[1]

Diet

Earthworms, insect larvae and other soft-bodied invertebrates.[1]

Reproduction
