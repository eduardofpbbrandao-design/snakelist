About

Zaire Snake-eater (Polemon robustus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
