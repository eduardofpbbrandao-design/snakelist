About

Chequered Keelback (Fowlea piscator) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 52 more. Common: recorded in 1070 places on iNaturalist.
