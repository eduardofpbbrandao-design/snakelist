About

Borneo Pit Viper (Craspedocephalus borneensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
