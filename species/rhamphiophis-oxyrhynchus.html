About

Western Beaked Snake (Rhamphiophis oxyrhynchus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon and 27 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
