About

Sabi Quill-snouted Snake (Xenocalamus sabiensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as sand dunes, underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
