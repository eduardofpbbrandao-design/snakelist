About

Dusky Wolf Snake (Lycodon albofuscus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
