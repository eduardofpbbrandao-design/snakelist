Flat-headed Kukri Snake (Oligodon planiceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, leaf litter, fallen logs and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
