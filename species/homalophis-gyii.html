About

Kapuas Mud Snake (Homalophis gyii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
