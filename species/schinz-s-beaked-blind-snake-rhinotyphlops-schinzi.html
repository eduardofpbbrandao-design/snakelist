About

Schinz's Beaked Blind Snake (Rhinotyphlops schinzi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, dry savanna, shrubland, scrubland and 13 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
