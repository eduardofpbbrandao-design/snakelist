Where the name comes from

schinzi: named after a person called Schinz

Schinz's Beaked Blind Snake: ‘Schinz's’ – named after a person called Schinz; ‘Beaked’ – its beak-like snout; ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
