About

Blackneck Garter Snake (Thamnophis cyrtopsis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 950 places on iNaturalist.
