Where the name comes from

bocagei: named after José Vicente Barbosa du Bocage, a Portuguese zoologist

Bocage’s Brown House Snake: ‘Bocage’s’ – named after José Vicente Barbosa du Bocage, a Portuguese zoologist; ‘Brown’ – its colour; ‘House Snake’ – it is often found near houses.
