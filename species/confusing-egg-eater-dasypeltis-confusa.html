About

Confusing Egg Eater (Dasypeltis confusa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Chad, Ethiopia and 15 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
