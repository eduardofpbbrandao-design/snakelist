About

Solomon's Small-eyed Snake (Loveridgelaps elapoides) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Solomon Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, solomon islands rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
