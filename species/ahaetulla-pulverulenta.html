About

Brown-speckled Whipsnake (Ahaetulla pulverulenta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
