About

Texas Patchnose Snake (Salvadora lineata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 481 places on iNaturalist.
