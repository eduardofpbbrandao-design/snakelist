About

Few-scaled Worm Snake (Gerrhopilus oligolepis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Gerrhopilidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
