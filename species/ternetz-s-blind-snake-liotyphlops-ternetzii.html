About

Ternetz's Blind Snake (Liotyphlops ternetzii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Argentina, Brazil, French Guiana, Paraguay, Suriname and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 32 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
