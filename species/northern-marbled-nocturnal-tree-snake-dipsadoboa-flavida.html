About

Northern Marbled Nocturnal Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa flavida) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
