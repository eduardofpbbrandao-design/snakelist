About

Black-striped Snake (imperialis) (Coniophanes imperialis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 355 places on iNaturalist.
