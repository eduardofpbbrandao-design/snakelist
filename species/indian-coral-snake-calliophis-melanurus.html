Indian Coral Snake

Calliophis melanurus

Indian Coral Snake (Calliophis melanurus) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in India. It is known to eat other snakes.

    Where the name comes from

    Calliophis: ‘beautiful snake’, from the Greek roots calli- ‘beautiful’ + -ophis ‘snake’

    melanurus: ‘black-tailed’

    Indian Coral Snake: ‘Indian’ – it lives in India.

    Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
