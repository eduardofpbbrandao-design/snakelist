About

Peters' Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis fallax) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 20 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
