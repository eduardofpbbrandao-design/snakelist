About

Gabou File Snake (Mehelya gabouensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Senegal. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
