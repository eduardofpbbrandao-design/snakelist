About

Neotropical Whip Snake (Masticophis mentovarius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala and 5 more. Common: recorded in 689 places on iNaturalist.
