Neotropical Whip Snake

Masticophis mentovarius (Duméril, Bibron & Duméril, 1854)

The Neotropical Whip Snake is a whipsnake of Mexico, Central America and northern South America.[1]

Who described it

First described by Duméril, Bibron & Duméril in 1854. The brackets around the authority, (Duméril, Bibron & Duméril, 1854), mean it was first described in a different genus.

Where the name comes from

Neotropical Whip Snake: ‘Neotropical’ – after the Neotropics (Central and South America); ‘Whip Snake’ – it is long and thin, like a whip.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
