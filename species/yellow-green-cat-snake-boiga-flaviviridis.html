About

Yellow-green Cat Snake (Boiga flaviviridis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 10 more. Recorded in 35 places on iNaturalist.
