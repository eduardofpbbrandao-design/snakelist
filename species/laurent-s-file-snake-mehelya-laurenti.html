About

Laurent's File Snake (Mehelya laurenti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 1 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
