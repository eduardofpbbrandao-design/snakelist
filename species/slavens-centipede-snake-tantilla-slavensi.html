About

Slavens' Centipede Snake (Tantilla slavensi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
