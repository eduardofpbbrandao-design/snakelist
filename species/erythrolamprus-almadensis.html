About

Almaden Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus almadensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 42 more. Recorded in 40 places on iNaturalist.
