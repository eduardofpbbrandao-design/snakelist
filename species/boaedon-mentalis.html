About

Bug-eyed House Snake (Boaedon mentalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Namibia, Rwanda, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, dry woodland and 19 more. Recorded in 57 places on iNaturalist.
