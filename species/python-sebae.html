About

African Rock Python (Python sebae) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon and 31 more. It has also been introduced outside its native range (South Florida), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 42 more. Common: recorded in 279 places on iNaturalist.
