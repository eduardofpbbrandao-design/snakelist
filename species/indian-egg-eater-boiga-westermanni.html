About

Indian Egg-eater (Boiga westermanni) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 27 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
