About

Pampas Lancehead (Bothrops pubescens) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
