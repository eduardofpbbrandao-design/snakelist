About

Gongshan Wolf Snake (Lycodon gongshan) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
