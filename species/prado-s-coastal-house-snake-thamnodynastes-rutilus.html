About

Prado's Coastal House Snake (Thamnodynastes rutilus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 25 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
