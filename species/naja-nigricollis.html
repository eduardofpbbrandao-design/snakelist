About

Black-necked Spitting Cobra (Naja nigricollis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 29 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 47 more. Common: recorded in 132 places on iNaturalist.
