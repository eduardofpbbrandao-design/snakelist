About

Transvaal Quillsnout Snake (Xenocalamus transvaalensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Botswana, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest, sand dunes and 3 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
