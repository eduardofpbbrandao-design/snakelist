About

Hook-snouted Worm Snake (Myriopholis macrorhyncha) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Oman and 3 more. Recorded in 39 places on iNaturalist.
