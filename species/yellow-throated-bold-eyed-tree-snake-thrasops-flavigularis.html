About

Yellow-throated Bold-eyed Tree Snake (Thrasops flavigularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Liberia and 1 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
