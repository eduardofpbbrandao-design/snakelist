Yellow-throated Bold-eyed Tree Snake

Thrasops flavigularis

Yellow-throated Bold-eyed Tree Snake (Thrasops flavigularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Liberia and Nigeria.

    Where the name comes from

    flavigularis: ‘yellow throat’, from Latin roots: flavi- ‘yellow’ + -gularis ‘throat’

    Yellow-throated Bold-eyed Tree Snake: ‘Yellow-throated’ – for its yellow throat; ‘Tree Snake’ – it lives in trees.
