About

Worontzow's Spotted Night Snake (Siphlophis worontzowi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
