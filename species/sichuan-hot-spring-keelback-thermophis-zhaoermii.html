Sichuan Hot-Spring Keelback (Thermophis zhaoermii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
