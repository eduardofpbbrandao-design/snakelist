About

Sulu Islands Banded Coral Snake (Calliophis suluensis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
