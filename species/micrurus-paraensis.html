About

Pará Coral Snake (Micrurus paraensis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
