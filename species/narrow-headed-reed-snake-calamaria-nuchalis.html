About

Narrow-headed Reed Snake (Calamaria nuchalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
