About

Anderson's Reed Snake (Calamaria andersoni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
