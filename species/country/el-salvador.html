How precisely they are recorded

Of the 71 living species recorded in El Salvador, 34 are recorded by department in SnakeList’s data; 37 are recorded only for El Salvador as a whole, so the department pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by department, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a department may live in only part of it.
