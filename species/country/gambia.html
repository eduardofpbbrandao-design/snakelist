How precisely they are recorded

Of the 49 living species recorded in the Gambia, 9 are recorded by division in SnakeList’s data; 40 are recorded only for the Gambia as a whole, so the division pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by division, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a division may live in only part of it.
