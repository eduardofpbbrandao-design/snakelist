How precisely they are recorded

Of the 9 living species recorded in Djibouti, 1 is recorded by region in SnakeList’s data; 8 are recorded only for Djibouti as a whole, so the region pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by region, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a region may live in only part of it.
