How precisely they are recorded

Of the 11 living species recorded in Cyprus, 7 are recorded by district in SnakeList’s data; 4 are recorded only for Cyprus as a whole, so the district pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by district, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a district may live in only part of it.
