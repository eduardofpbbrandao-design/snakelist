How precisely they are recorded

Of the 5 living species recorded in Fiji, 2 are recorded by division in SnakeList’s data; 3 are recorded only for Fiji as a whole, so the division pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by division, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a division may live in only part of it.
