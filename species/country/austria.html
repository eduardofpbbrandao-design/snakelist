Fossil snakes found here

These snakes are known only from fossils found in Austria. They are not living species and are not counted in this page’s totals. The geological ages given are approximate: they come from the rock layers the fossils were found in, and the dating and classification of fossil snakes are revised as new material is studied.

How precisely they are recorded

Of the 8 living species recorded in Austria, 7 are recorded by state in SnakeList’s data; 1 is recorded only for Austria as a whole, so the state pages list it as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by state, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a state may live in only part of it.
