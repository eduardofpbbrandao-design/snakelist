Snakes of Tanzania

175 living snake species are recorded in Tanzania in SnakeList: 62 venomous and 113 non-venomous, from 13 families.

These totals count the unique living snake species SnakeList records in Tanzania. A species counts if it is recorded anywhere in the country, so not every snake lives in every part of it.

How precisely they are recorded

Of the 175 living species recorded in Tanzania, 75 are recorded by region in SnakeList’s data; 100 are recorded only for Tanzania as a whole, so the region pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by region, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a region may live in only part of it.
