How precisely they are recorded

Of the 5 living species recorded in Bosnia and Herzegovina, 3 are recorded by canton or region in SnakeList’s data; 2 are recorded only for Bosnia and Herzegovina as a whole, so the canton or region pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by canton or region, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a canton may live in only part of it.
