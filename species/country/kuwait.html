How precisely they are recorded

Of the 6 living species recorded in Kuwait, 1 is recorded by governorate in SnakeList’s data; 5 are recorded only for Kuwait as a whole, so the governorate pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by governorate, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a governorate may live in only part of it.
