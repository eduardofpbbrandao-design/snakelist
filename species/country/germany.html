Snakes of Germany

7 living snake species are recorded in Germany in SnakeList: 2 venomous and 5 non-venomous, from 2 families. 17 fossil species found here are listed separately and not counted.

How precisely they are recorded

Of the 7 living species recorded in Germany, 7 are recorded by state in SnakeList’s data. SnakeList records each species by state, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a state may live in only part of it.
