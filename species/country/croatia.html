How precisely they are recorded

Of the 12 living species recorded in Croatia, 11 are recorded by county in SnakeList’s data; 1 is recorded only for Croatia as a whole, so the county pages list it as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by county, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a county may live in only part of it.
