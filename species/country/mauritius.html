How precisely they are recorded

Of the 3 living species recorded in Mauritius, 2 are recorded by district in SnakeList’s data; 1 is recorded only for Mauritius as a whole, so the district pages list it as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by district, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a district may live in only part of it.
