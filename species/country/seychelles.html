How precisely they are recorded

Of the 4 living species recorded in the Seychelles, 1 is recorded by district in SnakeList’s data; 3 are recorded only for the Seychelles as a whole, so the district pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by district, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a district may live in only part of it.
