How precisely they are recorded

Of the 3 living species recorded in Latvia, 2 are recorded by municipality in SnakeList’s data; 1 is recorded only for Latvia as a whole, so the municipality pages list it as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by municipality, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a municipality may live in only part of it.
