How precisely they are recorded

Of the 9 living species recorded in North Macedonia, 3 are recorded by municipality in SnakeList’s data; 6 are recorded only for North Macedonia as a whole, so the municipality pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by municipality, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a municipality may live in only part of it.
