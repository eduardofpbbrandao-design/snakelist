How precisely they are recorded

Of the 33 living species recorded in Saudi Arabia, 11 are recorded by province in SnakeList’s data; 22 are recorded only for Saudi Arabia as a whole, so the province pages list them as possible rather than recorded. SnakeList records each species by province, not by exact spot, so a species listed for a province may live in only part of it.
