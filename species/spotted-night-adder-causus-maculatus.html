About

Spotted Night Adder (Causus maculatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 21 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 77 places on iNaturalist.
