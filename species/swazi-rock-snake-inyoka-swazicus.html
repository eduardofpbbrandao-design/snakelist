About

Swazi Rock Snake (Inyoka swazicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eswatini, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, mopane woodland. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
