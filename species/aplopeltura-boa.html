About

Blunthead Slug Snake (Aplopeltura boa) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 147 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Brunei: Temburong
  • Indonesia: Bangka, Java, Kalimantan (Borneo), Nias, Sumatra
  • Malaysia: Kedah, Pahang, Penang, Perak, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor
  • Philippines: MIMAROPA (Region IV-B)
  • Thailand: Nakhon Si Thammarat, Narathiwat, Phatthalung, Surat Thani, Yala
