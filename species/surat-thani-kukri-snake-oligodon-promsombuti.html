About

Surat Thani Kukri Snake (Oligodon promsombuti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
