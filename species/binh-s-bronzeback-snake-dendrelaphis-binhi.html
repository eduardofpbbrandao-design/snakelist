About

Binh’s Bronzeback Snake (Dendrelaphis binhi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, forest edges, roadsides and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
