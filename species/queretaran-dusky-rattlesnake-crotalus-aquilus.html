About

Queretaran Dusky Rattlesnake (Crotalus aquilus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 152 places on iNaturalist.
