About

Ringneck Snake (Diadophis punctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 64 more. Common: recorded in 7793 places on iNaturalist.
