About

Fischer's Cat Snake (Toxicodryas pulverulenta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
