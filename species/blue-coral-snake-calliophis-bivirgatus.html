About

Blue Coral Snake (Calliophis bivirgatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 88 places on iNaturalist.
