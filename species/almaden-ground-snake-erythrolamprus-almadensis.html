Almaden Ground Snake

Erythrolamprus almadensis

Almaden Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus almadensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru and Uruguay.
