About

Godman's Garter Snake (Thamnophis godmani) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 31 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
