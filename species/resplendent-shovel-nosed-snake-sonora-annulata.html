About

Resplendent Shovel-nosed Snake (Sonora annulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, pine forest, shrubland, scrubland and 12 more. Common: recorded in 115 places on iNaturalist.
