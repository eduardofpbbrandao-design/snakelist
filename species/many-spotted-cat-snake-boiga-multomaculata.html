About

Many-spotted Cat Snake (Boiga multomaculata) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Laos and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 188 places on iNaturalist.
