About

Steppe Viper (Vipera renardi) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia, Russia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, mountain forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 397 places on iNaturalist.
