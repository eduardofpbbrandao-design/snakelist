Leopard Keelback

Helicops leopardinus

Leopard Keelback (Helicops leopardinus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, French Guiana, Paraguay and Suriname.

Where the name comes from

leopardinus: ‘leopard-like’

Leopard Keelback: ‘Keelback’ – its scales have a ridge (a keel) along the middle.
