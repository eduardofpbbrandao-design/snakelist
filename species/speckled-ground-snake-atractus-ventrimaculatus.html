About

Speckled Ground Snake (Atractus ventrimaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
