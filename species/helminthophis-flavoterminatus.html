About

Yellowtail Blind Snake (Helminthophis flavoterminatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs and 1 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
