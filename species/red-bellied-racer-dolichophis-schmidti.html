Where the name comes from

Dolichophis: ‘long snake’, from the Greek roots dolich- ‘long’ + -ophis ‘snake’

schmidti: named after a person called Schmidt

Red-Bellied Racer: ‘Red-Bellied’ – for its red belly; ‘Racer’ – a name for fast-moving snakes.
