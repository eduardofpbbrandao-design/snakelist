About

Dwarf Crowned Snake (Cacophis krefftii) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 115 places on iNaturalist.
