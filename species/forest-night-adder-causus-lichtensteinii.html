About

Forest Night Adder (Causus lichtensteinii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
