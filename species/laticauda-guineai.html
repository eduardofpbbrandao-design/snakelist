About

Guinea's Flat-tail Sea Snake (Laticauda guineai) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
