About

Persian Horned Viper (Pseudocerastes persicus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, steppe, shrubland and 25 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
