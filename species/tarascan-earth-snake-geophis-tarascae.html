About

Tarascan Earth Snake (Geophis tarascae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, underground burrows and 4 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
