About

Chinese Kukri Snake (Oligodon chinensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 171 places on iNaturalist.
