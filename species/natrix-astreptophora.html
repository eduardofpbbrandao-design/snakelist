About

Iberian Grass Snake (Natrix astreptophora) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in France, Gibraltar, Portugal, Spain. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 738 places on iNaturalist.
