About

Chihuahuan Blackhead Snake (Tantilla wilcoxi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest and 23 more. Recorded in 46 places on iNaturalist.
