About

Sipo (carinatus) (Chironius carinatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 93 places on iNaturalist.
