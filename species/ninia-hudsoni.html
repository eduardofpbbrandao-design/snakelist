About

Hudson's Coffee Snake (Ninia hudsoni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
