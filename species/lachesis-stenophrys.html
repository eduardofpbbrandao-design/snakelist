About

Central American Bushmaster (Lachesis stenophrys) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
