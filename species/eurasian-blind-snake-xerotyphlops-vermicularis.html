Eurasian Blind Snake

Xerotyphlops vermicularis

Eurasian Blind Snake (Xerotyphlops vermicularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Albania, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Greece, Montenegro and 3 more.

Where the name comes from

vermicularis: ‘worm-like’

Eurasian Blind Snake: ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
