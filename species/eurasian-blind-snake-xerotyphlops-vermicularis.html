About

Eurasian Blind Snake (Xerotyphlops vermicularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Albania, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Greece, Montenegro and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 289 places on iNaturalist.
