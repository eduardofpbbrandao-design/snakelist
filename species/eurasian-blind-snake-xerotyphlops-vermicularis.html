About

Eurasian Blind Snake (Xerotyphlops vermicularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Albania, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Greece, Montenegro and 3 more. Common: recorded in 289 places on iNaturalist.
