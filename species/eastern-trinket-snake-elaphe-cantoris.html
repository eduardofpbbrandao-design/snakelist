About

Eastern Trinket Snake (Elaphe cantoris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 15 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
