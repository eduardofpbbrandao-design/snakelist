Matschie's African Ground Snake

Gonionotophis klingi

Matschie's African Ground Snake (Gonionotophis klingi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Nigeria and Togo. It is known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

klingi: named after a person called Kling

Matschie's African Ground Snake: ‘Matschie's’ – named after a person called Matschie; ‘African’ – it lives in Africa; ‘Ground Snake’ – it lives on or under the ground.
