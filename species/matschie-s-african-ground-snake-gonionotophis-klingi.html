About

Matschie's African Ground Snake (Gonionotophis klingi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Nigeria and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
