About

Malayan Brown Pit Viper (Ovophis convictus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
