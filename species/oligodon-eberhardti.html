About

Eberhardt's Kukri Snake (Oligodon eberhardti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 9 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
