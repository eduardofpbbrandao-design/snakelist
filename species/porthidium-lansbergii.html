About

Lansberg's Hognosed Pit Viper (Porthidium lansbergii) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Colombia, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 135 places on iNaturalist.

  • Panama: Chiriquí, Coclé, Darién, Herrera, Panamá, Veraguas
