About

Pine Snake (Pituophis melanoleucus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 11 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
