About

Wucherer's Ground Snake (Xenopholis scalaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 17 more. Recorded in 73 places on iNaturalist.
