About

Texiguat Centipede Snake (Tantilla olympia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Honduras. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
