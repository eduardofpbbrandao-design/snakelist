About

African Brown Water Snake (Afronatrix anoscopus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Ghana and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 30 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
