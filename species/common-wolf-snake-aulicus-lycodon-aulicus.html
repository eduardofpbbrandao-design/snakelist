Common Wolf Snake (aulicus)

Lycodon aulicus

Common Wolf Snake (aulicus) (Lycodon aulicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Maldives and 4 more.

Where the name comes from

Lycodon: from the Greek lykos (wolf) and odon (tooth) - for the long, fang-like front teeth[1]

Common Wolf Snake (aulicus): ‘Common’ – it is the commonest or best-known of its kind.
