About

Dugès' Earth Snake (Geophis dugesii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 19 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
