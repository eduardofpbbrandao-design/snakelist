About

Forest Thread Snake (Leptotyphlops sylvicolus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest, shrubland and 12 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
