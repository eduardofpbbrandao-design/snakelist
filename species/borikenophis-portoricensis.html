About

Puerto Rican Racer (Borikenophis portoricensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in British Virgin Islands, Puerto Rico, US Virgin Islands, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Common: recorded in 102 places on iNaturalist.
