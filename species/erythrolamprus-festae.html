About

Drab Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus festae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
