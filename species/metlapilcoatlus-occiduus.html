About

Western Jumping Pitviper (Metlapilcoatlus occiduus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
