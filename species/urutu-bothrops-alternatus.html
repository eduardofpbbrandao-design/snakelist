About

Urutu (Bothrops alternatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 403 places on iNaturalist.
