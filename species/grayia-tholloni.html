About

Tholloni's African Water Snake (Grayia tholloni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 42 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
