About

Travancore Wolf Snake (Lycodon travancoricus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 150 places on iNaturalist.
