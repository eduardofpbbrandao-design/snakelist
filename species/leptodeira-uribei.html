About

Uribe's False Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira uribei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
