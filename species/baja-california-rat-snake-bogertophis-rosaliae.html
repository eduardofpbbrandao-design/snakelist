About

Baja California Rat Snake (Bogertophis rosaliae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, shrubland, scrubland, chaparral and 14 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
