About

Rustyhead Snake (Amastridium veliferum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Mexico and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
