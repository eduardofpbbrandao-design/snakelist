About

Tanganyika Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus bicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
