About

Castoe's Coralsnake (Calliophis castoe) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in India. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
