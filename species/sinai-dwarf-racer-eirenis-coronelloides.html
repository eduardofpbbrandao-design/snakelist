About

Sinai dwarf racer (Eirenis coronelloides) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Syria and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
