About

African Dwarf Snake (Eirenis africanus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Djibouti, Eritrea, Somalia, Sudan. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 20 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
