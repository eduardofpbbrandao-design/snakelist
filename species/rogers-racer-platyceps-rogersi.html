About

Rogers' Racer (Platyceps rogersi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Egypt, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Lebanon and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 22 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
