About

Fiddle-string Snake (Imantodes cenchoa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 55 more. Common: recorded in 1005 places on iNaturalist.
