About

Striped Kukri Snake (Oligodon taeniatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.

  • Laos
  • Thailand: Bangkok, Chachoengsao, Chaiyaphum, Kalasin, Khon Kaen, Lampang, Nakhon Ratchasima, Nonthaburi, Pathum Thani, Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya, Prachin Buri, Sa Kaeo, Samut Prakan, Samut Sakhon, Saraburi
