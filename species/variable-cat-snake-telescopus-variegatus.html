About

Variable Cat Snake (Telescopus variegatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
