About

Tibetan Pit Viper (Trimeresurus tibetanus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bhutan, China, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
