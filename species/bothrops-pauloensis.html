About

Cerrado Lancehead (pauloensis) (Bothrops pauloensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 21 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
