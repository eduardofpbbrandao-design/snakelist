About

Corn Snake (Pantherophis guttatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is also invasive outside its native range (Cayman Islands). It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 2111 places on iNaturalist.
