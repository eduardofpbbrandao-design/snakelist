About

White-lipped Snake (Drysdalia coronoides) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, eucalyptus forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 277 places on iNaturalist.
