About

Bamboo Snake (Pseudoxenodon bambusicola) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
