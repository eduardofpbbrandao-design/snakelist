About

African Cobra (Naja pallida) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Egypt, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 31 more. Recorded in 27 places on iNaturalist.
