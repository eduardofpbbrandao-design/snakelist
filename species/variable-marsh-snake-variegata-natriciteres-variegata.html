Variable Marsh Snake (variegata)

Natriciteres variegata

Variable Marsh Snake (variegata) (Natriciteres variegata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more.
