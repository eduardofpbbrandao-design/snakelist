About

Damara Tiger Snake (Telescopus finkeldeyi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Namibia. It lives in habitats such as rocky hillsides, rocky outcrops, rock crevices. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
