About

Miranda Green Racer (mattogrossensis) (Philodryas mattogrossensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, gallery forest, woodland and 14 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
