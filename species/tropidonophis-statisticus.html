About

Papua New Guinea Montane Keelback (Tropidonophis statisticus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
