About

Duges' Threadsnake (Rena dugesii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
