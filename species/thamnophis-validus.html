About

West Coast Garter Snake (Thamnophis validus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 76 places on iNaturalist.
