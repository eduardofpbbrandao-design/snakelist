About

Harvey’s Blackhead (Apostolepis multicincta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, semi-desert and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
