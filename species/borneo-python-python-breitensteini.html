About

Borneo Python (Python breitensteini) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
