About

Dark-headed Sabah Groundsnake (Stegonotus caligocephalus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
