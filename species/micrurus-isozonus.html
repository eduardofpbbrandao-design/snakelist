About

Venezuela Coral Snake (Micrurus isozonus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Guyana, Venezuela. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
