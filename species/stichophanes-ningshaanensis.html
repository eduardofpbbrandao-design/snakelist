About

Ningshaan Kukri Snake (Stichophanes ningshaanensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, evergreen forest, broadleaf forest, chinese subtropical forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
