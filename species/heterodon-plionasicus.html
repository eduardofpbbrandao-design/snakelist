Heterodon plionasicus

Heterodon plionasicus is a prehistoric snake of the family Colubridae, known only from fossils. It died out in the late Pliocene, about 3 million years ago.

Where the name comes from

Heterodon: Greek for "different tooth" - after the enlarged teeth at the back of the upper jaw, used to puncture toads that puff themselves up[1]
