Where the name comes from

Bothrops: from the Greek bothros (pit) and ops (eye or face) - for the heat-sensing pits on the head[1]

erythromelas: ‘red and black’

Caatinga Lancehead: ‘Lancehead’ – its head is shaped like the point of a lance.
