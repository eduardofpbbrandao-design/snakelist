Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis)

Calliophis intestinalis

Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) (Calliophis intestinalis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand.
