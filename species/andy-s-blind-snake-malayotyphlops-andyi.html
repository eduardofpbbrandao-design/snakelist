Andy’s Blind Snake

Malayotyphlops andyi Wynn, Diesmos & Brown, 2016

Andy's Blind Snake is a blind snake (family Typhlopidae) from northern Luzon in the Philippines. Scientists described it only in 2016, from specimens collected in the Sierra Madre mountains, and almost nothing has been published about how it lives - its size range, diet, breeding and behaviour are not documented.[1,2]

Identification

Appearance

It looks very like its relative Malayotyphlops luzonensis in size and colour. The two are told apart by details of the head scales - for example, which small scales beside the nostril touch the scales of the upper lip - so a specimen has to be examined closely.[1]

Range

Published distribution

Known range: Known only from Cagayan Province in northern Luzon, Philippines. The type specimen (the one the species is named from) came from a reforestation project area at Nassiping, Gattaran, in the Sierra Madre.[1,2]
