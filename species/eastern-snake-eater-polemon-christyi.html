About

Eastern Snake-eater (Polemon christyi) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi, Rwanda and 4 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 18 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
