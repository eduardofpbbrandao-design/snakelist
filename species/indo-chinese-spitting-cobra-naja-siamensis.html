Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra

Naja siamensis

Where the name comes from

Naja: from the Sanskrit nāga, meaning snake[1]

siamensis: ‘from Siam (Thailand)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra: ‘Spitting Cobra’ – it can spit venom at the eyes of an attacker.
