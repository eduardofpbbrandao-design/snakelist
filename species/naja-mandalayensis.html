About

Mandalay Cobra (Naja mandalayensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, forest edges, abandoned buildings and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
