About

Greater Black Krait (Bungarus niger) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 9 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
