About

Bullsnake (Pituophis catenifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest, montane woodland and 24 more. Common: recorded in 5539 places on iNaturalist.
