About

Buff Striped Keelback (Amphiesma stolatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, China, India, Myanmar, Nepal and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 50 more. Common: recorded in 775 places on iNaturalist.
