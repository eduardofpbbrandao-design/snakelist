About

Lycophidion semicinctum is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
