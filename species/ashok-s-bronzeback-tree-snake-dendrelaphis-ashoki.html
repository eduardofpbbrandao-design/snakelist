About

Ashok's Bronzeback Tree Snake (Dendrelaphis ashoki) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 8 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
