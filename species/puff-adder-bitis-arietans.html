Puff Adder

Bitis arietans

The Puff Adder is a thick-bodied viper found across most of Africa and in south-western Arabia. It is thought to cause more snakebite deaths than any other African snake - because it is so widespread, often lives near people, and is quick to bite when disturbed.[1,2]

Ecology

Habitat

Open country such as savanna and grassland.[1]

Behaviour

Named for the way it hisses and puffs itself up when approached.[1]

Reproduction

Gives birth to live young. Very large females, especially from East Africa, have the biggest litters: a female from Kenya kept in a Czech zoo gave birth to 156 babies, the largest litter recorded for any snake.[1]
