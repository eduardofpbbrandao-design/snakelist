About

Brongersma's Worm Snake (Amerotyphlops brongersmianus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 33 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
