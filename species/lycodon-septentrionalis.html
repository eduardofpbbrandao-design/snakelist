About

White-banded Wolf Snake (Lycodon septentrionalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Laos, Myanmar and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 17 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
