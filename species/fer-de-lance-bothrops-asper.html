About

Fer-de-lance (Bothrops asper) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Guatemala and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 39 more. Common: recorded in 1210 places on iNaturalist.
