About

Amazon Coral Snake (Micrurus spixii) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
