About

Northern Keelback (Hebius deschauenseei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 17 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
