About

Mussurana (maculata) (Boiruna maculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 17 more. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
