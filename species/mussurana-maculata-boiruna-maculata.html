About

Mussurana (maculata) (Boiruna maculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It is known to eat other snakes. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
