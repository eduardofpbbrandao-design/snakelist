Mussurana (maculata)

Boiruna maculata

Mussurana (maculata) (Boiruna maculata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay and Uruguay. It is known to eat other snakes.
