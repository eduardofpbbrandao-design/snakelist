About

Dione Snake (Elaphe dione) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, China, Georgia, Iran and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 955 places on iNaturalist.
