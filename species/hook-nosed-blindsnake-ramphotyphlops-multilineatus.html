Where the name comes from

multilineatus: ‘many-lined’, from Latin roots: multi- ‘many’ + -lineatus ‘lined’

Hook-nosed Blindsnake: ‘Blindsnake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
