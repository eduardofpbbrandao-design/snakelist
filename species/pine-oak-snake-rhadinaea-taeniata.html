About

Pine-Oak Snake (Rhadinaea taeniata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 54 places on iNaturalist.
