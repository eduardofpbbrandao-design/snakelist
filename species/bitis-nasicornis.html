About

Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) (Bitis nasicornis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 17 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Recorded in 71 places on iNaturalist.
