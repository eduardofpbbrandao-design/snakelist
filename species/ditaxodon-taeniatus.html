About

Hensel's Snake (Ditaxodon taeniatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
