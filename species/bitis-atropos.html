About

Mountain Adder (Bitis atropos) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Eswatini, Lesotho, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 135 places on iNaturalist.
