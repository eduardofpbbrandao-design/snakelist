About

Saddled Coral Snake (Micrurus bernadi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
