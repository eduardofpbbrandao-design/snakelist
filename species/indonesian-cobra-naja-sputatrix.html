About

Indonesian Cobra (Naja sputatrix) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 96 places on iNaturalist.
