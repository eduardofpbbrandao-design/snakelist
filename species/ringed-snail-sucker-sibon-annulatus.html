About

Ringed Snail Sucker (Sibon annulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Honduras and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 54 places on iNaturalist.
