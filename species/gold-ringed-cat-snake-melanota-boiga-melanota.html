About

Gold-ringed Cat Snake (melanota) (Boiga melanota) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 145 places on iNaturalist.
