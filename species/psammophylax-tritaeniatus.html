About

Striped Skaapsteker (Psammophylax tritaeniatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 107 places on iNaturalist.
