About

Ocellated Kukri Snake (Oligodon ocellatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 5 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
