About

Dwarf Sand Snake (Psammophis angolensis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Malawi and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 32 more. Recorded in 60 places on iNaturalist.
