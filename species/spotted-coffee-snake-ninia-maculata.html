Spotted Coffee Snake

Ninia maculata

Spotted Coffee Snake (Ninia maculata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua and Panama.

Where the name comes from

maculata: ‘spotted’

Spotted Coffee Snake: ‘Spotted’ – its spots; ‘Coffee Snake’ – it is often found in coffee plantations.
