About

Shreve's Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa shrevei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 7 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
