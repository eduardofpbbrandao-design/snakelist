About

Michell's Quill-snouted Snake (Xenocalamus michelli) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
