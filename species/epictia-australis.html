About

Freiberg's Blind Snake (Epictia australis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
