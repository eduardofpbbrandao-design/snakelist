Where the name comes from

anops: ‘eyeless’

Cope's Blind Snake: ‘Cope's’ – named after Edward Drinker Cope, an American palaeontologist and herpetologist; ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
