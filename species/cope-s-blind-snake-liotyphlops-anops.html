About

Cope's Blind Snake (Liotyphlops anops) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
