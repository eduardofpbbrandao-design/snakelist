About

Black Centipede-eater (Aparallactus niger) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Sierra Leone. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
