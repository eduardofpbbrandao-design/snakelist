About

Caesar's African Water Snake (Grayia caesar) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 24 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
