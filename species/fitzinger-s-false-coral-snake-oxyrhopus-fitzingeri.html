Where the name comes from

fitzingeri: named after Leopold Fitzinger, an Austrian zoologist

Fitzinger's False Coral Snake: ‘Fitzinger's’ – named after Leopold Fitzinger, an Austrian zoologist; ‘False’ – it looks like one, but isn't.
