About

Dark Ground Snake (Atractus melas) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, underground burrows and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
