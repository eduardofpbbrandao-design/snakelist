About

Erythrolamprus taeniogaster is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 31 more. Recorded in 47 places on iNaturalist.
