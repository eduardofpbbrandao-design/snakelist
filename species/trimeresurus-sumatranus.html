About

Sumatra Pit Viper (Trimeresurus sumatranus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
