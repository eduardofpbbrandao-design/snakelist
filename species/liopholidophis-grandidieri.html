About

Grandidier's Water Snake (Liopholidophis grandidieri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
