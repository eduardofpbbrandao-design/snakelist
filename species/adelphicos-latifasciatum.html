About

Oaxacan Burrowing Snake (Adelphicos latifasciatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
