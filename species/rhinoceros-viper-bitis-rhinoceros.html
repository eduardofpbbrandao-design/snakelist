About

Rhinoceros Viper (Bitis rhinoceros) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Ivory Coast, Liberia and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
