About

Ceylon Pit Viper (Craspedocephalus trigonocephalus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Recorded in 48 places on iNaturalist.
