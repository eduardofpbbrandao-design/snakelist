About

Brazilian Green Racer (Philodryas aestiva) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, subtropical forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 137 places on iNaturalist.
