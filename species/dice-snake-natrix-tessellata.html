Dice Snake

Natrix tessellata

Dice Snake (Natrix tessellata) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Crimea and 17 more.

Where the name comes from

Natrix: the classical Latin word for a water snake, related to the German Natter and English adder[1]

tessellata: ‘chequered’

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
