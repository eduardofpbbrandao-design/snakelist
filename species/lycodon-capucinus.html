About

Common Wolf Snake (capucinus) (Lycodon capucinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia, Guinea. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Christmas Island), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 458 places on iNaturalist.
