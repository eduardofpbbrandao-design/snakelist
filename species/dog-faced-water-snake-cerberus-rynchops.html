About

Dog-faced Water Snake (Cerberus rynchops) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 77 places on iNaturalist.
