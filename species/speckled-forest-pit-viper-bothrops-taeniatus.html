About

Speckled Forest Pit Viper (Bothrops taeniatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Recorded in 57 places on iNaturalist.
