About

Tanganyika Sand Snake (Psammophis tanganicus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya, Libya, Somalia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 36 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
