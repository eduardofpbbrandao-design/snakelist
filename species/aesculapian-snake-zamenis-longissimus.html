About

Aesculapian Snake (Zamenis longissimus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Andorra, Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, France and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 33 more. Common: recorded in 2655 places on iNaturalist.
