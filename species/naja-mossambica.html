About

Mozambique Spitting Cobra (Naja mossambica) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 304 places on iNaturalist.
