About

Yabassi Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa duchesnii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 4 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
