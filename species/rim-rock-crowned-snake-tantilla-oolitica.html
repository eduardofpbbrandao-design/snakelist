About

Rim Rock Crowned Snake (Tantilla oolitica) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest, underground burrows and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
