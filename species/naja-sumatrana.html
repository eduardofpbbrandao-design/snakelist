About

Equatorial Spitting Cobra (Naja sumatrana) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 179 places on iNaturalist.

Native range

  • Malaysia: Johor, Kedah, Kelantan, Negeri Sembilan, Pahang, Penang, Perak, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor
  • Singapore: Central Singapore Community Development Council, South East Community Development Council, South West Community Development Council
