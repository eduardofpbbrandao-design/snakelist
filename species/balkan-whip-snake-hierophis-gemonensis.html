About

Balkan Whip Snake (Hierophis gemonensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, France, Greece and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 367 places on iNaturalist.
