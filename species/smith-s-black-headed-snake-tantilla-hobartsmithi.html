About

Smith's Black-headed Snake (Tantilla hobartsmithi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 203 places on iNaturalist.
