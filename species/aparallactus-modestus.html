About

Western Forest Centipede-eater (Aparallactus modestus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
