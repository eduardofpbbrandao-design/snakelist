About

Guiral's File Snake (Limaformosa guirali) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
