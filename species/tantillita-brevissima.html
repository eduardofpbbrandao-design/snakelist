About

Speckled Dwarf Short-tail Snake (Tantillita brevissima) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
