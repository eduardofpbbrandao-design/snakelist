About

Oriental Coral Snake (Sinomicrurus sauteri) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Taiwan. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, mountain forest, mountain slopes and 1 more. Recorded in 45 places on iNaturalist.
