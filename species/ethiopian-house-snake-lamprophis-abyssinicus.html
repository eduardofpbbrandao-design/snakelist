About

Ethiopian House Snake (Lamprophis abyssinicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Ethiopia. It lives in habitats such as rocky hillsides, rocky outcrops, rock crevices. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
