About

Mato Grosso Blind Snake (cupinensis) (Siagonodon cupinensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 19 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
