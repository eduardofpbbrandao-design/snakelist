About

Underwood's Bronzeback Snake (Dendrelaphis underwoodi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 8 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
