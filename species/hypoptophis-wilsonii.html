About

African Bighead Snake (Hypoptophis wilsonii) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
