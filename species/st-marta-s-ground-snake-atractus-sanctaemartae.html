About

St. Marta's Ground Snake (Atractus sanctaemartae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
