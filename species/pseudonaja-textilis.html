About

Eastern Brown Snake (Pseudonaja textilis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, temperate rainforest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 1539 places on iNaturalist.
