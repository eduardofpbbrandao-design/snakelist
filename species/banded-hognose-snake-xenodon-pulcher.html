Where the name comes from

Xenodon: ‘strange tooth’, from the Greek roots xen- ‘strange’ + -odon ‘tooth’

pulcher: ‘beautiful’

Banded Hognose Snake: ‘Banded’ – its bands; ‘Hognose Snake’ – its upturned snout looks like a pig's.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
