About

Banded Hognose Snake (Xenodon pulcher) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland and 16 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.

  • Brazil: Mato Grosso do Sul
