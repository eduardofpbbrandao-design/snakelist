About

Pale Wolf Snake (Lycophidion irroratum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
