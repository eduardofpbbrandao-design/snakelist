About

Devil's Head Spotted Night Snake (Siphlophis ayauma) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
