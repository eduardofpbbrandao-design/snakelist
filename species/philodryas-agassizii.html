About

Burrowing Night Snake (Philodryas agassizii) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
