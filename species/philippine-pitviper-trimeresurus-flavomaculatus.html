About

Philippine pitviper (Trimeresurus flavomaculatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 12 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
