About

Schlegel's Golden Snake (Cercophis auratus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 14 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
