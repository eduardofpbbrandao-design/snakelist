About

Cascabel Rattlesnake (Crotalus durissus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 60 more. Common: recorded in 647 places on iNaturalist.
