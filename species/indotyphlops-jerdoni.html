About

Jerdon's Worm Snake (Indotyphlops jerdoni) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
