About

Sahyadri Vine Snake (Ahaetulla sahyadrensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 17 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
