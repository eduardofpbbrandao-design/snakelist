About

Richardson's mangrove snake (Myron richardsonii) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 47 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
