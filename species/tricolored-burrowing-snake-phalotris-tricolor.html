About

Tricolored Burrowing Snake (Phalotris tricolor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 17 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
