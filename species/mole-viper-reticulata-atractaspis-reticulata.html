Mole Viper (reticulata)

Atractaspis reticulata

Mole Viper (reticulata) (Atractaspis reticulata) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon.
