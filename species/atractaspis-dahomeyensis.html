About

Mole Viper (dahomeyensis) (Atractaspis dahomeyensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Chad, Gambia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
