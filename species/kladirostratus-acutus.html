About

Beaked Skaapsteker (Kladirostratus acutus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
