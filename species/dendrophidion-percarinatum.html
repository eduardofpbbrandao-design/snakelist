About

South American Forest Racer (Dendrophidion percarinatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Honduras, Nicaragua and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 103 places on iNaturalist.
