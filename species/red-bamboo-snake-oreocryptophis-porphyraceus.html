About

Red Bamboo Snake (Oreocryptophis porphyraceus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Indonesia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 91 places on iNaturalist.
