About

Wadi Racer (Platyceps rhodorachis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, India, Iran, Israel, Jordan and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, woodland, open woodland and 34 more. Common: recorded in 86 places on iNaturalist.
