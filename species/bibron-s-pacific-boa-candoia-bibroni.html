Where the name comes from

bibroni: named after Gabriel Bibron, a French zoologist

Bibron's Pacific Boa: ‘Bibron's’ – named after Gabriel Bibron, a French zoologist; ‘Pacific’ – after the Pacific; ‘Boa’ – from boa, a Latin word for a large snake.
