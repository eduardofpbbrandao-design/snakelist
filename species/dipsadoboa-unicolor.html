About

Günther's Green Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa unicolor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
