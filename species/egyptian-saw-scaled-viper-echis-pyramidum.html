Where the name comes from

Echis: the Latin spelling of the Greek word echis, meaning viper[1]

Egyptian Saw-scaled Viper: ‘Egyptian’ – it lives in Egypt; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.
