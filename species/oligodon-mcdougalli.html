About

McDougall's Kukri Snake (Oligodon mcdougalli) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
