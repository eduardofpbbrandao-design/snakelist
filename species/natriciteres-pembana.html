About

Variable Marsh Snake (pembana) (Natriciteres pembana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, wetlands, marshes, freshwater marshes and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
