About

West Mexican Coral Snake (Micrurus distans) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 19 more. Recorded in 50 places on iNaturalist.
