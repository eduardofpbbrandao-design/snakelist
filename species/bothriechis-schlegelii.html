About

Eyelash Viper (Bothriechis schlegelii) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Colombia, Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 83 places on iNaturalist.
