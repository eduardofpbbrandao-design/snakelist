About

Asian Racer (Hemorrhois nummifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Cyprus, Greece, Iran, Israel and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, montane woodland and 29 more. Common: recorded in 154 places on iNaturalist.
