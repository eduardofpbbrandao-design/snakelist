Where the name comes from

sumatranus: ‘of Sumatra’ – named after where it lives

Sumatra Pit Viper: ‘Sumatra’ – the place where it lives; ‘Pit Viper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
