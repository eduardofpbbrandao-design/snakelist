About

Puff Adder (Bitis arietans) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi and 26 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 67 more. Common: recorded in 1411 places on iNaturalist.
