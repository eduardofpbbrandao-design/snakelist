About

Allen's Coral Snake (Micrurus alleni) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 81 places on iNaturalist.
