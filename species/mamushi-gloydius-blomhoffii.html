About

Mamushi (Gloydius blomhoffii) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Japan, Russia, Southern Kuril Islands. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest and 4 more. Common: recorded in 287 places on iNaturalist.
