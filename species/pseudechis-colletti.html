About

Collett's Snake (Pseudechis colletti) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 19 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
