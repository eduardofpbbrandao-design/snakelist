Where the name comes from

Hydrophis: ‘water snake’, from the Greek roots hydr- ‘water’ + -ophis ‘snake’

fasciatus: ‘banded’

Striped Sea Snake: ‘Striped’ – its stripes; ‘Sea Snake’ – it lives in the sea.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
