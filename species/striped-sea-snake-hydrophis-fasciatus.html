About

Striped Sea Snake (Hydrophis fasciatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
