About

Derafshi Snake (Lytorhynchus ridgewayi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan. It lives in habitats such as grassland, temperate grassland, steppe, desert and 10 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
