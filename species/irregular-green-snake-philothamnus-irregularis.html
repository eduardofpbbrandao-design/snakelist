About

Irregular Green Snake (Philothamnus irregularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 22 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
