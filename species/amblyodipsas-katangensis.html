About

Katanga Purple-glossed Snake (Amblyodipsas katangensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
