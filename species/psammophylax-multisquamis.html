About

Kenyan striped skaapsteker (Psammophylax multisquamis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, scrub forest and 12 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
