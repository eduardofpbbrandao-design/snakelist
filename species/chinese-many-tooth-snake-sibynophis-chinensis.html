About

Chinese Many-tooth Snake (Sibynophis chinensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, South Korea, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 170 places on iNaturalist.
