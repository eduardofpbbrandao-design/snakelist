About

Transcaucasian Rat Snake (Zamenis hohenackeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cyprus, Georgia, Israel and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, mountain forest and 11 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
