About

Colombian Boa (Boa imperator) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 1619 places on iNaturalist.
