About

White-bellied Rat Snake (Ptyas fusca) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 117 places on iNaturalist.
