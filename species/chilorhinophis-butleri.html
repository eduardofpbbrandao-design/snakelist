About

Butler's Two-headed Snake (Chilorhinophis butleri) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Burundi, Kenya, Mozambique, Rwanda, South Sudan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, open woodland and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
