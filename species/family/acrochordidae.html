About the family

Fully aquatic snakes of Southeast Asia, New Guinea and northern Australia that are rarely seen on land. Their loose, wrinkled skin and supple bodies help them seize and hold struggling fish. The Little File Snake gives birth to live young and can live in fresh or salty water.[1,2]
