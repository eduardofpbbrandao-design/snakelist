About

Equatorial File Snake (Mehelya poensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 13 more. It is known to eat other snakes. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
