Scarlet Snake

Cemophora coccinea (Blumenbach, 1788)

The Scarlet Snake is a small, harmless burrowing snake of the south-eastern United States, banded red, black and white or yellow.[1]

Identification

How to recognise it

  • Red, black and white or yellow bands on a grey body.[1]

Appearance

Light grey with black-bordered red blotches separated by white or yellow.[1]

Size

Adults: About 36 to 66 cm long.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: The south-eastern USA, from Florida north to New Jersey and west to eastern Texas and Oklahoma.[1,2]

Behaviour

Active at night and only in summer; by day it hides under logs and pine litter or underground. It is often seen crossing roads at night.[1]

Reproduction

Lays 2 to 9 eggs.[1]
