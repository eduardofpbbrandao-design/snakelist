About

Blackbelly Garter Snake (Thamnophis melanogaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 102 places on iNaturalist.
