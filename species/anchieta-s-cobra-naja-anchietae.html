About

Anchieta's Cobra (Naja anchietae) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Malawi, Namibia, Zambia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, gallery forest, woodland, dry woodland and 16 more. Recorded in 48 places on iNaturalist.
