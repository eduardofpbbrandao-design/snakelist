About

Phalotris concolor is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
