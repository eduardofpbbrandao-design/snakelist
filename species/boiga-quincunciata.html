About

Assamese Cat Snake (Boiga quincunciata) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 11 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
