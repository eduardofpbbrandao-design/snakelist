About

Rwanda Forest Green Snake (Philothamnus ruandae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Uganda. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
