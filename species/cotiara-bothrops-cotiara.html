About

Cotiara (Bothrops cotiara) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
