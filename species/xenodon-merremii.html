About

Wagler's Snake (Xenodon merremii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 411 places on iNaturalist.
