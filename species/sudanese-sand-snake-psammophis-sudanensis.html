About

Sudanese Sand Snake (Psammophis sudanensis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Ghana and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 21 more. Recorded in 24 places on iNaturalist.
