About

Southern Blind Snake (unguirostris) (Rena unguirostris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, grassland and 13 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
