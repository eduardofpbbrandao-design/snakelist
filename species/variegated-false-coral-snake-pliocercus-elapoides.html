About

Variegated False Coral Snake (Pliocercus elapoides) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.
