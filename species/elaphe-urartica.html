About

Urartian Rat Snake (Elaphe urartica) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran, Iraq and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as mountain forest, alpine meadow, alpine grassland, alpine shrubland and 6 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
