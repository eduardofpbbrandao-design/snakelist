About

Talamanca Glasstail Snake (Urotheca myersi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, central american dry forest, costa rican rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
