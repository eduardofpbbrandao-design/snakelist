About

Kalahari Purple-glossed Snake (Amblyodipsas ventrimaculata) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Malawi, Namibia, South Africa and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 14 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
