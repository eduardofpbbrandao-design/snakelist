About

Gomes' Burrowing Snake (Apostolepis cearensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Recorded in 32 places on iNaturalist.
