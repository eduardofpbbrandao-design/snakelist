About

Marley’s Snail-eating Snake (Sibon marleyae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, chocó rainforest. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
