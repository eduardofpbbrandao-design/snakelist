About

Guatemalan Vine Snake (Oxybelis koehleri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 232 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Costa Rica: Alajuela, Cartago, Guanacaste, Heredia, Limón, Puntarenas, San José
  • El Salvador: La Libertad, San Miguel, Usulután
  • Guatemala: Jutiapa, Santa Rosa
  • Honduras: Atlántida, Bay Islands, Colón, Comayagua, Cortés, Francisco Morazán, Santa Bárbara
  • Nicaragua: Carazo, Granada, Managua, Rivas, Río San Juan, South Caribbean Coast
