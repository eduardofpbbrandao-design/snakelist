About

Angolan Shovel-snout (Prosymna ambigua) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
