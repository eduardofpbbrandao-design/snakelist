About

Reuss' Mud Snake (Miralia alternans) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
