About

Mexican Snake Eater (Clelia scytalina) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
