Where the name comes from

nattereri: named after Johann Natterer, an Austrian naturalist who explored Brazil

Paraguay Green Racer: ‘Paraguay’ – the place where it lives; ‘Green’ – its colour; ‘Racer’ – a name for fast-moving snakes.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
