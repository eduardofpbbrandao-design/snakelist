About

Paraguay Green Racer (Philodryas nattereri) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 215 places on iNaturalist.
