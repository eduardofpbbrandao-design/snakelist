About

Dice Snake (Natrix tessellata) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Crimea and 17 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 2431 places on iNaturalist.
