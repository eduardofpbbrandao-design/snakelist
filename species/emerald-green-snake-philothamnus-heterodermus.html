About

Emerald Green Snake (Philothamnus heterodermus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 17 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
