Where the name comes from

Sibynophis: ‘spear snake’, from the Greek roots sibyn- ‘spear’ + -ophis ‘snake’

geminatus: ‘paired’

Boie's Many-Tooth Snake: ‘Boie's’ – named after Friedrich Boie, a German zoologist.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
