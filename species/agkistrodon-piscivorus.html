Cottonmouth (Agkistrodon piscivorus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 3300 places on iNaturalist.
