About

Argentinian Coral Snake (Micrurus pyrrhocryptus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland and 19 more. Common: recorded in 132 places on iNaturalist.
