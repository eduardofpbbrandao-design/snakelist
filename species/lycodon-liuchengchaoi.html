About

Sichuan Wolf Snake (Lycodon liuchengchaoi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
