About

Flat-snouted Wolf Snake (Lycophidion depressirostre) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Central African Republic, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
