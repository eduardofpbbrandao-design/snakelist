About

Oriental Ratsnake (Ptyas mucosa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 71 more. Common: recorded in 1315 places on iNaturalist.
