About

Gervais' Reed Snake (Calamaria gervaisii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 10 more. Recorded in 30 places on iNaturalist.
