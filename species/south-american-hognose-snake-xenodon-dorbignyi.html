About

South American Hognose Snake (Xenodon dorbignyi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 220 places on iNaturalist.
