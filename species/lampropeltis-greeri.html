About

Greer’s Kingsnake (Lampropeltis greeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, montane woodland and 16 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
