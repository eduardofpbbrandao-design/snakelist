About

Big-scaled Blind Snake (Trilepida macrolepis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Guyana and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 18 more. Recorded in 19 places on iNaturalist.
