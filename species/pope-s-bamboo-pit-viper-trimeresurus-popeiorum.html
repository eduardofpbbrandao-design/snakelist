About

Pope's Bamboo Pit Viper (Trimeresurus popeiorum) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Laos and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Recorded in 24 places on iNaturalist.
