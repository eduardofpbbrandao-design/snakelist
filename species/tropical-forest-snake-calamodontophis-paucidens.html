About

Tropical Forest Snake (Calamodontophis paucidens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
