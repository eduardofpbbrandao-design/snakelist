About

Mountain Slug Snake (Pareas margaritophorus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Cambodia, China, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 255 places on iNaturalist.
