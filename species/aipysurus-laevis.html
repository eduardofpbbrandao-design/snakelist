About

Olive Sea Snake (Aipysurus laevis) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, New Caledonia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 124 places on iNaturalist.
