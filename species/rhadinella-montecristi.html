About

Monte Cristi Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinella montecristi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 2 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
