About

Indian Rock Python (Python molurus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Indonesia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 439 places on iNaturalist.
