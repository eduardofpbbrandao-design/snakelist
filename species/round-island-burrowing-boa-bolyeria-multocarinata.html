Round Island Burrowing Boa

Bolyeria multocarinata

Round Island Burrowing Boa (Bolyeria multocarinata) was a non-venomous snake of the family Bolyeriidae that lived in Mauritius. Extinct since 1975: last seen on Round Island, Mauritius.
