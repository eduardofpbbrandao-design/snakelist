About

Olive Keelback Wart Snake (Atretium schistosum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 101 places on iNaturalist.
