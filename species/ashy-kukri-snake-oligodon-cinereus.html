About

Ashy Kukri Snake (Oligodon cinereus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 21 more. Recorded in 74 places on iNaturalist.
