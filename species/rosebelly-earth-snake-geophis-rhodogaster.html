About

Rosebelly Earth Snake (Geophis rhodogaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
