About

Asia Minor Dwarf Racer (Eirenis modestus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Greece, Israel and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, pine forest, woodland, open woodland and 16 more. Common: recorded in 114 places on iNaturalist.
