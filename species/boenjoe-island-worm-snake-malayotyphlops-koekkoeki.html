About

Boenjoe Island Worm Snake (Malayotyphlops koekkoeki) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
