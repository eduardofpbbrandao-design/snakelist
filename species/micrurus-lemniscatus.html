About

South American Coral Snake (Micrurus lemniscatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 35 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
