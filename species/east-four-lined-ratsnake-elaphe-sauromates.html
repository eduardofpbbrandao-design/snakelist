About

East-Four-lined Ratsnake (Elaphe sauromates) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bulgaria, Crimea, Greece, Kazakhstan, Moldova and 4 more. Common: recorded in 213 places on iNaturalist.
