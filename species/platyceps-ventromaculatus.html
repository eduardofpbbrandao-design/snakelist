About

Glossy-bellied Racer (Platyceps ventromaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, India, Jordan, Pakistan, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest and 23 more. Recorded in 27 places on iNaturalist.
