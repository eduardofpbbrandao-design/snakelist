About

Western Natal Green Snake (Philothamnus occidentalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Eswatini, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 203 places on iNaturalist.
