About

Yellow-lipped Sea Krait (Laticauda colubrina) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Solomon Islands and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 440 places on iNaturalist.
