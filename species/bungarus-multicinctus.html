About

Many-banded Krait (Bungarus multicinctus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in China, Taiwan. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 615 places on iNaturalist.
