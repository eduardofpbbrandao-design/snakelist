About

Futsing Wolf Snake (Lycodon futsingensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 107 places on iNaturalist.
