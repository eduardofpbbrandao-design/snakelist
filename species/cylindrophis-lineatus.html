About

Blanford's Pipe Snake (Cylindrophis lineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Cylindrophiidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
