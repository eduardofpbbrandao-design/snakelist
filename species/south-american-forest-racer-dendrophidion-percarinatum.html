South American Forest Racer

Dendrophidion percarinatum

South American Forest Racer (Dendrophidion percarinatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Honduras, Nicaragua and 2 more.
