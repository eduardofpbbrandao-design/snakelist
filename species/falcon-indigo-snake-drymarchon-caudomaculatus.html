About

Falcon indigo snake (Drymarchon caudomaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as forest edges, roadsides. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
