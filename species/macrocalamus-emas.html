About

Golden-bellied Reed Snake (Macrocalamus emas) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
