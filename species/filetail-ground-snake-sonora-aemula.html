About

Filetail Ground Snake (Sonora aemula) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 11 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
