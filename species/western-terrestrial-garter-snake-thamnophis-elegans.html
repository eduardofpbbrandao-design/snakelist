About

Western Terrestrial Garter Snake (Thamnophis elegans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest, pine forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 2865 places on iNaturalist.
