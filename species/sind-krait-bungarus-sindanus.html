About

Sind Krait (Bungarus sindanus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in India, Pakistan. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest and 10 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
