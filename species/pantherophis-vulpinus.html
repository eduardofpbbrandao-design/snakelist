About

Eastern Fox Snake (Pantherophis vulpinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 849 places on iNaturalist.
