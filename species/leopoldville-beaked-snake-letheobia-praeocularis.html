About

Léopoldville Beaked Snake (Letheobia praeocularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
