About

Yellow striped Snake (Coelognathus flavolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 225 places on iNaturalist.
