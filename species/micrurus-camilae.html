About

Camila's Coralsnake (Micrurus camilae) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 3 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
