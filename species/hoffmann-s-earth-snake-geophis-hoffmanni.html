About

Hoffmann's Earth Snake (Geophis hoffmanni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 90 places on iNaturalist.
