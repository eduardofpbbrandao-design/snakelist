About

Owen Stanley Range Forest Snake (Toxicocalamus stanleyanus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
