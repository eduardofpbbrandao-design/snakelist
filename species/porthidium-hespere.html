About

Western Hognose Viper (Porthidium hespere) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, central american dry forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
