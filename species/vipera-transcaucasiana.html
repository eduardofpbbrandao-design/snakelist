About

Transcaucasian Long-nosed Viper (Vipera transcaucasiana) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Georgia, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as mountain forest, alpine meadow, alpine grassland, alpine shrubland and 1 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
