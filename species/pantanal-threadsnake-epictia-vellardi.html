About

Pantanal Threadsnake (Epictia vellardi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, wet savanna and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
