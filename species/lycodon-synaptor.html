About

Boehme's Wolf Snake (Lycodon synaptor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
