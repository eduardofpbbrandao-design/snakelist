About

Racer (Coluber constrictor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Canada, Guatemala, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, temperate rainforest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 7512 places on iNaturalist.
