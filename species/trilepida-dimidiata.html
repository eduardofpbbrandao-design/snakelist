About

Dainty Blind Snake (Trilepida dimidiata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
