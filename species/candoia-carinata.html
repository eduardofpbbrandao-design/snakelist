About

Pacific Boa (carinata) (Candoia carinata) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea, Vanuatu. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 6 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
