About

Lushai Hills Dragon Snake (Stoliczkia vanhnuailianai) is a non-venomous snake of the family Xenodermidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as bamboo forest, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
