About

Floodplain Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus obscuriventris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, coastal forest, riverine forest and 24 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
