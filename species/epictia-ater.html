About

Black Blind Snake (ater) (Epictia ater) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in China, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 46 places on iNaturalist.
