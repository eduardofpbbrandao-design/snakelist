About

Spotted Keelback Water Snake (Fowlea punctulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Myanmar, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as wetlands, marshes, freshwater marshes, swamps and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
