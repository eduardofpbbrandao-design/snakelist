About

Green Mamba (Dendroaspis angusticeps) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 23 more. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
