About

Striped Coral Snake (nigrotaeniatus) (Calliophis nigrotaeniatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
