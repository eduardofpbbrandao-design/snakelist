About

Barbados Threadsnake (Tetracheilostoma carlae) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Barbados. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
