Tanganyika Water Snake

Lycodonomorphus bicolor

Tanganyika Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus bicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Rwanda, Tanzania, Zambia.
