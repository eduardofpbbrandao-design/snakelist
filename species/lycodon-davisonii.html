About

Blanford's Bridal Snake (Lycodon davisonii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 87 places on iNaturalist.
