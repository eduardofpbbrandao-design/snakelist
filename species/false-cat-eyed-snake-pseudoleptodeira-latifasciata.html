About

False Cat-eyed Snake (Pseudoleptodeira latifasciata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 44 places on iNaturalist.
