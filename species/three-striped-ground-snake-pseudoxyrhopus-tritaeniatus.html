About

Three-striped Ground Snake (Pseudoxyrhopus tritaeniatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 10 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
