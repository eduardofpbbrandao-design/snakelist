About

Trans-pecos Ratsnake (Bogertophis subocularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 233 places on iNaturalist.
