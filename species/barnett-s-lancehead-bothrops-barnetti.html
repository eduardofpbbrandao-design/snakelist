Where the name comes from

Bothrops: from the Greek bothros (pit) and ops (eye or face) - for the heat-sensing pits on the head[1]

barnetti: named after a person called Barnett

Barnett's Lancehead: ‘Barnett's’ – named after a person called Barnett; ‘Lancehead’ – its head is shaped like the point of a lance.
