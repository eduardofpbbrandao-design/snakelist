About

Brown Vinesnake (Oxybelis aeneus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 70 more. Common: recorded in 224 places on iNaturalist.
