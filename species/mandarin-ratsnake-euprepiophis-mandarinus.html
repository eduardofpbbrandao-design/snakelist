Mandarin Ratsnake

Mandarin Ratsnake (Euprepiophis mandarinus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Laos, Myanmar, Taiwan and Vietnam.

Where the name comes from

Euprepiophis: ‘good-looking snake’, from the Greek roots euprepi- ‘good-looking’ + -ophis ‘snake’

Mandarin Ratsnake: ‘Ratsnake’ – it eats rats and other rodents.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
