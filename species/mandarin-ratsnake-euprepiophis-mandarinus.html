About

Mandarin Ratsnake (Euprepiophis mandarinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Laos, Myanmar, Taiwan and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 160 places on iNaturalist.
