About

Reticulate Worm Snake (Amerotyphlops reticulatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 47 places on iNaturalist.
