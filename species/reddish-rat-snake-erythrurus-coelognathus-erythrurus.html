About

Reddish Rat Snake (erythrurus) (Coelognathus erythrurus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 14 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
