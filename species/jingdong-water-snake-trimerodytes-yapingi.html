About

Jingdong Water Snake (Trimerodytes yapingi) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 20 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
