About

White-banded Coral Snake (Micrurus albicinctus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
