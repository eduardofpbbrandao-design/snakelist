    Where the name comes from

    Leptotyphlops: from the Greek leptos (thin) added to Typhlops (blind eye)[1]

    mbanjensis: named after the place where it was found (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

    Mbanja Worm Snake: ‘Worm Snake’ – it is small and looks like a worm.
