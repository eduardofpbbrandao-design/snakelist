About

Rough-tailed Sand Boa (Eryx conicus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, montane woodland and 16 more. Common: recorded in 354 places on iNaturalist.
