About

Potosí Earth Snake (Geophis latifrontalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 18 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
