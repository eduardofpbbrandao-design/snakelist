About

Himalayan Pitviper (Gloydius himalayanus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in India, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 10 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
