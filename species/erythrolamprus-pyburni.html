About

Pyburn's Tropical Forest Snake (Erythrolamprus pyburni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as gallery forest, forest edges, savanna, tropical savanna and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
