About

Hoge's Ground Snake (Lygophis paucidens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 15 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
