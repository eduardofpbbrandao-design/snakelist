About

Mexican Blind Snake (Anomalepis mexicana) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
