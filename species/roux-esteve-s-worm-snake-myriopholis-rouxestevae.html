About

Roux-Estève's Worm Snake (Myriopholis rouxestevae) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Guinea, Senegal, Uganda. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
