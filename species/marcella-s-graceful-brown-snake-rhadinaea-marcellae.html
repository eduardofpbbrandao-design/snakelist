About

Marcella's Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinaea marcellae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
