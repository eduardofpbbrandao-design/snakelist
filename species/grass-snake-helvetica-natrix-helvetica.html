About

Grass Snake (helvetica) (Natrix helvetica) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Austria, Belgium, France, Germany, Italy and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 39 more. Common: recorded in 3777 places on iNaturalist.
