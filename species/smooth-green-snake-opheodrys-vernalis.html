About

Smooth Green Snake (Opheodrys vernalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 2055 places on iNaturalist.
