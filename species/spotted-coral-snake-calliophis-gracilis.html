About

Spotted Coral Snake (Calliophis gracilis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
