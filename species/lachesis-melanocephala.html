About

Black-headed Bushmaster (Lachesis melanocephala) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, central american dry forest, costa rican rainforest. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
