Where the name comes from

lemniscatus: ‘ribboned’

Dumeril's Diadem Snake: ‘Dumeril's’ – named after Duméril, the name of two French zoologists, André Duméril and his son Auguste; ‘Diadem Snake’ – the crown-like pattern on its head.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
