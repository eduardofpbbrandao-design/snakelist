About

Golden Flying Snake (Chrysopelea ornata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 647 places on iNaturalist.
