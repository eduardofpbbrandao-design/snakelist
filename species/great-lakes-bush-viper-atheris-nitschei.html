Great Lakes Bush Viper

Atheris nitschei

Great Lakes Bush Viper (Atheris nitschei) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Tanzania and Uganda.
