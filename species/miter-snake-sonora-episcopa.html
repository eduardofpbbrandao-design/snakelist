About

miter snake (Sonora episcopa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 433 places on iNaturalist.
