About

Desert Kingsnake (Lampropeltis splendida) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 570 places on iNaturalist.
