About

Thamnodynastes silvai is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
