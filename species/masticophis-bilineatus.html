About

Sonoran Whipsnake (Masticophis bilineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 280 places on iNaturalist.
