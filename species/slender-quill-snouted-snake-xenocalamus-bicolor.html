About

Slender Quill-snouted Snake (Xenocalamus bicolor) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
