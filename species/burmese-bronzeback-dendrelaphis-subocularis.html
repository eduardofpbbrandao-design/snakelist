About

Burmese Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis subocularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 15 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
