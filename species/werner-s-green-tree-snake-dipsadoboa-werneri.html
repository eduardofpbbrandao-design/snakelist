About

Werner's Green Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa werneri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, coastal scrub, east african coastal forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
