About

Wall's Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis cyanochloris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Indonesia, Malaysia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Recorded in 63 places on iNaturalist.
