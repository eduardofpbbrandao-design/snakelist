About

Plin's Banded Racer (Platyceps plinii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
