Cape Wolf Snake (capense)

Lycophidion capense

Cape Wolf Snake (capense) (Lycophidion capense) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 18 more.
