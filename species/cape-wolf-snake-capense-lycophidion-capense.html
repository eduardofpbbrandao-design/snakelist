About

Cape Wolf Snake (capense) (Lycophidion capense) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 18 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 236 places on iNaturalist.
