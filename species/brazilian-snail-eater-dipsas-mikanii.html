About

Brazilian Snail Eater (Dipsas mikanii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 281 places on iNaturalist.
