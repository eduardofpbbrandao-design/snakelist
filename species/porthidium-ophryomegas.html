About

Slender Hognose Viper (Porthidium ophryomegas) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 71 places on iNaturalist.
