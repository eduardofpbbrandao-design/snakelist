Brown Spotted Pitviper

Protobothrops mucrosquamatus (Cantor, 1839)

The Brown-spotted Pit Viper (Taiwanese Habu) is a venomous pit viper of north-eastern India, South-east Asia, southern China and Taiwan.[1]

Who described it

First described by Cantor in 1839. The brackets around the authority, (Cantor, 1839), mean it was first described in a different genus.

Where the name comes from

Brown Spotted Pitviper: ‘Brown’ – its colour; ‘Spotted’ – its spots; ‘Pitviper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
