About

Northern Calico Snake (Oxyrhopus occipitalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Guyana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 83 places on iNaturalist.
