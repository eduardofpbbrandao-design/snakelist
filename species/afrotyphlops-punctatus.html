About

Spotted Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops punctatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 18 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 24 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
