About

Gold-ringed Cat Snake (dendrophila) (Boiga dendrophila) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Cambodia, China, Indonesia, Malaysia and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 131 places on iNaturalist.
