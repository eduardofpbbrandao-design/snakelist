About

Fea Viper (Azemiops feae) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Myanmar, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 78 places on iNaturalist.
