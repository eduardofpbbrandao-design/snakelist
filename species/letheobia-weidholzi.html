About

Weidholz's Pink Blindsnake (Letheobia weidholzi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Cameroon, Chad, Nigeria. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
