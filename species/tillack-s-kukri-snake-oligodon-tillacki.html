About

Tillack's Kukri Snake (Oligodon tillacki) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 80 places on iNaturalist.
