About

Ringed Tree Boa (Corallus annulatus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Guatemala, Honduras and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
