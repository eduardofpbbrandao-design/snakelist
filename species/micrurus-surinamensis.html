About

Aquatic Coral Snake (Micrurus surinamensis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 3 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 148 places on iNaturalist.
