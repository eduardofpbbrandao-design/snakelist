About

Burmese Krait (Bungarus magnimaculatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Myanmar. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 3 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
