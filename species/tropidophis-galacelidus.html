About

Pilsbry's Dwarf Boa (galacelidus) (Tropidophis galacelidus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Tropidophiidae, found in Cuba. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
