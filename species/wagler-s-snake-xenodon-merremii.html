Where the name comes from

Xenodon: ‘strange tooth’, from the Greek roots xen- ‘strange’ + -odon ‘tooth’

merremii: named after Blasius Merrem, a German zoologist

Wagler's Snake: ‘Wagler's’ – named after Johann Georg Wagler, a German herpetologist.
