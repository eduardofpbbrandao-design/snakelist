About

Sipo (flavopictus) (Chironius flavopictus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 110 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Colombia: Antioquia, Caldas, Cauca, Chocó, Nariño, Risaralda, Valle del Cauca
  • Costa Rica: Cartago, Limón, Puntarenas, San José
  • Ecuador: Esmeraldas, Los Ríos, Manabí, Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas
  • Panama: Chiriquí, Darién, Los Santos, Panamá, Veraguas, comarca Ngäbe-Buglé
