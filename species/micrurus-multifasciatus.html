About

Many-banded Coral Snake (Micrurus multifasciatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Recorded in 26 places on iNaturalist.
