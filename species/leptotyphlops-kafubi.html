About

Shaba Thread Snake (Leptotyphlops kafubi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
