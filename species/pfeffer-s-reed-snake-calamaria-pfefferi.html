About

Pfeffer's Reed Snake (Calamaria pfefferi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Japan. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
