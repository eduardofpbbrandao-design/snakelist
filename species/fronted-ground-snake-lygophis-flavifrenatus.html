About

Fronted Ground Snake (Lygophis flavifrenatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
