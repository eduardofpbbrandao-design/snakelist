About

Boulenger's False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus marcapatae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
