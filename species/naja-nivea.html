About

Cape Cobra (Naja nivea) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Lesotho, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, dry woodland, forest edges, dry savanna and 20 more. Common: recorded in 452 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Botswana: Ghanzi, Kgalagadi
  • Lesotho
  • Namibia: Erongo, Hardap, Karas, Khomas
  • South Africa: Eastern Cape, Free State, North West, Northern Cape, Western Cape
