About

Common Wolf Snake (aulicus) (Lycodon aulicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Maldives and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 59 more. Common: recorded in 479 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Bangladesh: Chattogram, Rajshahi, Sylhet
  • Bhutan: Wangdue Phodrang
  • China: Yunnan
  • India: Assam, Gujarat, Kerala, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal
  • Maldives: Alif Alif Atoll, Alif Dhaal Atoll, Baa Atoll

  • Nepal: Gandaki Zone, Janakpur Zone
