About

Deuve's Water Snake (Homalopsis nigroventralis) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 11 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
