About

Forest Cobra (Naja melanoleuca) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
