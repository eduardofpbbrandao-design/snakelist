Atahuallpa Saphenophis Snake (Saphenophis atahuallpae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
