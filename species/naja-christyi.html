About

Christy's Water Cobra (Naja christyi) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
