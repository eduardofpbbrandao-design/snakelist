Chinese Green Tree Viper

Trimeresurus stejnegeri Schmidt, 1925

The Chinese Green Tree Viper (Bamboo Pit Viper) is a venomous green pit viper of southern China, Taiwan and nearby countries.[1]

Who described it

Where the name comes from

stejnegeri: named after Leonhard Stejneger, a Norwegian-American zoologist

Chinese Green Tree Viper: ‘Chinese’ – it lives in China; ‘Green’ – its colour; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
