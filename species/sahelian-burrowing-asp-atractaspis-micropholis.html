    Where the name comes from

    micropholis: ‘small scales’, from Greek roots: micro- ‘small’ + -pholis ‘scales’

    Sahelian Burrowing Asp: ‘Burrowing Asp’ – it burrows, and is venomous (asp is an old word for a venomous snake).
