About

Black-headed Snake (melanocephalus) (Rhynchocalamus melanocephalus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Cyprus, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, mountain forest and 14 more. Recorded in 55 places on iNaturalist.
