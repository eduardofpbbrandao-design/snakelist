About

Night Snake (Hypsiglena torquata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.
