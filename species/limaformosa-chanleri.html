About

Unicolor File Snake (Limaformosa chanleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea, Kenya, Rwanda and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
