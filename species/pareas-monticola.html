About

Common Slug Snake (Pareas monticola) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 11 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
