About

Alashan pitviper (Gloydius cognatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Kazakhstan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, mountain forest, subalpine forest and 14 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
