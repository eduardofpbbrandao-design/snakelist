About

Yellow Sea Snake (Hydrophis spiralis) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, China, India, Indonesia, Iran and 13 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 24 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
