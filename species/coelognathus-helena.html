About

Trinket Snake (Coelognathus helena) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Pakistan and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 106 places on iNaturalist.
