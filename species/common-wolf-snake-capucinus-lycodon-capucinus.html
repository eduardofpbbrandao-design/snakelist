Common Wolf Snake (capucinus)

Lycodon capucinus

Common Wolf Snake (capucinus) (Lycodon capucinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 8 more.
