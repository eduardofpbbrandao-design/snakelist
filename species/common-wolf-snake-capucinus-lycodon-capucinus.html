About

Common Wolf Snake (capucinus) (Lycodon capucinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 8 more. It is also invasive outside its native range (Christmas Island). It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 458 places on iNaturalist.
