Striped Lowland Snake

Leptodrymus pulcherrimus

Striped Lowland Snake (Leptodrymus pulcherrimus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and Nicaragua.
