About

Eastern Indigo Snake (Drymarchon couperi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest, forest edges and 6 more. Recorded in 70 places on iNaturalist.
