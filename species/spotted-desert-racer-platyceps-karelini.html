About

Spotted Desert Racer (Platyceps karelini) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Iraq, Kazakhstan, Pakistan and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as grassland, temperate grassland, steppe, desert and 9 more. Recorded in 33 places on iNaturalist.
