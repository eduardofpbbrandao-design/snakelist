About

White-lipped Island Pitviper (Trimeresurus insularis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Indonesia, Timor-Leste. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 146 places on iNaturalist.
