About

Laboute's Sea Snake (Hydrophis laboutei) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, New Caledonia. It lives in habitats such as shallow coastal waters, new caledonia forest. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
