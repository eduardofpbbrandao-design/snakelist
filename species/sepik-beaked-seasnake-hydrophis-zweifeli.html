About

Sepik Beaked Seasnake (Hydrophis zweifeli) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 23 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
