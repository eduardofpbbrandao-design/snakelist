About

Padang Reed Snake (Calamaria abstrusa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
