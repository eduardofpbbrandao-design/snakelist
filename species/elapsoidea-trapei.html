About

Trape's Garter Snake (Elapsoidea trapei) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Guinea, Mali, Mauritania, Senegal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
