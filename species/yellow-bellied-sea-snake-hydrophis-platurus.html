About

Yellow-bellied Sea Snake (Hydrophis platurus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Indonesia, Japan, Pakistan, Philippines and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 302 places on iNaturalist.
