About

Viperine Snake (Natrix maura) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in France, Gibraltar, Italy, Portugal, Spain and 1 more. It is also invasive outside its native range (Mallorca and Menorca). It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 2475 places on iNaturalist.
