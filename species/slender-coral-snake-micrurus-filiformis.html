About

Slender Coral Snake (Micrurus filiformis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
