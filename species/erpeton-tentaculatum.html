About

Tentacled Snake (Erpeton tentaculatum) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, India, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 16 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
