About

Papuan Blacksnake (Pseudechis papuanus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
