About

Chlorosoma laticeps is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 22 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
