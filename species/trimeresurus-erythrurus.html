About

Bamboo Pitviper (Trimeresurus erythrurus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 16 more. Recorded in 56 places on iNaturalist.
