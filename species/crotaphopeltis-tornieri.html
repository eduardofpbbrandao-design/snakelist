About

Tornier's Cat Snake (Crotaphopeltis tornieri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Kenya, Malawi, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
