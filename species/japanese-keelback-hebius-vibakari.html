About

Japanese Keelback (Hebius vibakari) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Japan, North Korea, South Korea. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 179 places on iNaturalist.
