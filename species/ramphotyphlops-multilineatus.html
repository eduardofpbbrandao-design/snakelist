About

Hook-nosed Blindsnake (Ramphotyphlops multilineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
