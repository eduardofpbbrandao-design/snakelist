About

Northeastern Hill Krait (Bungarus bungaroides) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar, Nepal and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 9 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
