About

Olive Forest Racer (Dendrophidion dendrophis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 83 places on iNaturalist.
