About

Theobald's Kukri Snake (Oligodon theobaldi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Myanmar, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 7 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
