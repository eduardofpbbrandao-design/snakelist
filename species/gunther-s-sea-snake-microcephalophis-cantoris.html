About

Günther's sea snake (Microcephalophis cantoris) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Iran, Malaysia, Myanmar and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
