About

Beautiful Cat Snake (Telescopus pulcher) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ethiopia, Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, scrub forest, thorn forest, semi-desert and 5 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
