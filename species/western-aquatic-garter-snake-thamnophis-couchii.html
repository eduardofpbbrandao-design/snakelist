About

Western Aquatic Garter Snake (Thamnophis couchii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, forest edges, shrubland and 25 more. Common: recorded in 310 places on iNaturalist.
