About

Tana worm snake (Myriopholis tanae) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, scrub forest, thorn forest, semi-desert and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
