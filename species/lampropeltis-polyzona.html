About

Atlantic Central American Milksnake (Lampropeltis polyzona) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 364 places on iNaturalist.
