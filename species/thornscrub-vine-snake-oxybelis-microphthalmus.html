About

Thornscrub Vine Snake (Oxybelis microphthalmus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, pine forest, forest edges, scrubland and 7 more. Common: recorded in 258 places on iNaturalist.
