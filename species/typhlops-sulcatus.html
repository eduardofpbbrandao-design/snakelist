About

Island Worm Snake (Typhlops sulcatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Dominican Republic, Haiti. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
