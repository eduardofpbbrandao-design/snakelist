About

Slaty-grey snake (Stegonotus australis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 128 places on iNaturalist.
