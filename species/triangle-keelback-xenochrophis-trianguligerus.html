Triangle Keelback

Xenochrophis trianguligerus

Triangle Keelback (Xenochrophis trianguligerus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Cambodia and Myanmar.

    Where the name comes from

    trianguligerus: ‘triangle-bearing’

    Triangle Keelback: ‘Keelback’ – its scales have a ridge (a keel) along the middle.
