About

Mole Viper (corpulenta) (Atractaspis corpulenta) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
