About

Cape Sand Snake (Psammophis leightoni) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in South Africa. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, forest edges, dry savanna, shrubland and 15 more. Common: recorded in 145 places on iNaturalist.
