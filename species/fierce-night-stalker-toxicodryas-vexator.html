About

Fierce Night Stalker (Toxicodryas vexator) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, South Sudan, Tanzania and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
