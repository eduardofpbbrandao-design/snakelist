About

Western Threadsnake (Rena humilis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 263 places on iNaturalist.
