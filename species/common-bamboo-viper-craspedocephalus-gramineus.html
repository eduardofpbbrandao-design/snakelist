About

Common Bamboo Viper (Craspedocephalus gramineus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cambodia, India, Indonesia, Japan, Malaysia and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 191 places on iNaturalist.
