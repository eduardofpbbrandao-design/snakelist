About

Many-spotted Snake (Amplorhinus multimaculatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Lesotho, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 7 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
