About

Maren's Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis marenae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 87 places on iNaturalist.
