About

African Bush Viper (Atheris squamigera) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 55 places on iNaturalist.
