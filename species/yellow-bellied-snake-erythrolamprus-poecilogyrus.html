About

Yellow-bellied Snake (Erythrolamprus poecilogyrus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Guyana, Paraguay and 3 more. Common: recorded in 691 places on iNaturalist.
