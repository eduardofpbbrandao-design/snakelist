About

Coastal House Snake (strigatus) (Thamnodynastes strigatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 21 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
