About

Earthworm Blind Snake (Typhlops lumbricalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Bahamas, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Haiti, Jamaica. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
