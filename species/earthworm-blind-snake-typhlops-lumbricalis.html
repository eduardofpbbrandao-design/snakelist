Where the name comes from

Typhlops: from the Greek typhlos (blind) and ōps (eye)[1]

lumbricalis: ‘worm-like’

Earthworm Blind Snake: ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
