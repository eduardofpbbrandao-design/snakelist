About

Kirtland's Snake (Clonophis kirtlandii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, meadow and 14 more. Common: recorded in 106 places on iNaturalist.
