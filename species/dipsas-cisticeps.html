About

Neotropical Snail-eater (cisticeps) (Dipsas cisticeps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 12 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
