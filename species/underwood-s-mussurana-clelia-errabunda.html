Underwood's Mussurana

Clelia errabunda

Underwood's Mussurana (Clelia errabunda) was a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae that lived in Saint Lucia. Extinct since the 1880s: last seen on Saint Lucia. It was known to eat other snakes.
