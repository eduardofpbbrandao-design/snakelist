Saint Croix Racer

Borikenophis sanctaecrucis

Saint Croix Racer (Borikenophis sanctaecrucis) was a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae that lived in US Virgin Islands. Extinct since the early 1900s: last seen on Saint Croix.
