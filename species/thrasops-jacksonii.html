About

Jackson's Black Tree Snake (Thrasops jacksonii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
