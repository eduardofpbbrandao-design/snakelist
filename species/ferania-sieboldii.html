About

Siebold's Mud Snake (Ferania sieboldii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Myanmar, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, bamboo forest and 18 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
