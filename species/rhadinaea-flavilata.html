About

Pine Woods Snake (Rhadinaea flavilata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 334 places on iNaturalist.
