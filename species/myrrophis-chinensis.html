About

Chinese Mud Snake (Myrrophis chinensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, evergreen forest, broadleaf forest, bamboo forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 100 places on iNaturalist.
