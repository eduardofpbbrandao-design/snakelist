About

Black-headed Ground Snake (Atractus nigricauda) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Peru. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
