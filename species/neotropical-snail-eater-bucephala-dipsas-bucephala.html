About

Neotropical Snail-eater (bucephala) (Dipsas bucephala) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, open woodland, savanna, tropical savanna and 5 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
