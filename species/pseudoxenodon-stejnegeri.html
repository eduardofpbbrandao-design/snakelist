About

Stejneger's Bamboo Snake (Pseudoxenodon stejnegeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Taiwan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 76 places on iNaturalist.
