Palaeophis maghrebianus

Palaeophis maghrebianus is a prehistoric snake of the family Palaeophiidae, known only from fossils. It died out in the early Eocene, about 52 million years ago.

Fossils and geological context

Geological age

It lived in the early Eocene, about 52 million years ago (dated from the rocks its fossils were found in).

Where it was found

1952, Camille Arambourg (Ouled Abdoun phosphates, Morocco).

Size estimate

Large vertebrae suggest it may have been longer than any living snake (estimate, Houssaye et al. 2013).
