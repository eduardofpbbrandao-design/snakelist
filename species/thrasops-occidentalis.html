About

Black Tree Snake (occidentalis) (Thrasops occidentalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 11 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
