About

Short Sea Snake (Hydrophis curtus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Indonesia, Iran, Malaysia, Oman and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 31 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
