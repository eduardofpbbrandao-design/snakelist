About

Plains Garter Snake (Thamnophis radix) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 49 more. Common: recorded in 1896 places on iNaturalist.
