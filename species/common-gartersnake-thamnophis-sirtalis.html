About

Common Gartersnake (Thamnophis sirtalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest and 72 more. Common: recorded in 16821 places on iNaturalist.
