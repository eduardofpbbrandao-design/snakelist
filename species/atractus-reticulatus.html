About

Reticulate Ground Snake (Atractus reticulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
