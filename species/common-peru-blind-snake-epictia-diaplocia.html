About

Common Peru Blind Snake (Epictia diaplocia) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
