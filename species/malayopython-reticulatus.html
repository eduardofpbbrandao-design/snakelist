About

Reticulated Python (Malayopython reticulatus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 42 more. Common: recorded in 698 places on iNaturalist.
