Calabar Ground Python

Calabaria reinhardtii

Calabar Ground Python (Calabaria reinhardtii) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more.
