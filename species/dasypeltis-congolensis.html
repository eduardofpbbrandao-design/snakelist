About

Dasypeltis congolensis is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Gabon, Republic of the Congo, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
