About

Sri Lanka Cat Snake (Boiga ceylonensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Recorded in 32 places on iNaturalist.
