About

Bushmaster (Lachesis muta) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 229 places on iNaturalist.
