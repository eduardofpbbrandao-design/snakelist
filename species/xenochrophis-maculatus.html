About

Spotted Keelback (Xenochrophis maculatus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.
