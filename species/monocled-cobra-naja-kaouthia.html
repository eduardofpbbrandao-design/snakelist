About

Monocled Cobra (Naja kaouthia) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, India, Laos and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 47 more. Common: recorded in 256 places on iNaturalist.
