About

Pigmy Black-backed Coralsnake (Micrurus scutiventris) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
