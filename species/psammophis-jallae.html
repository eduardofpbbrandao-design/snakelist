About

Jalla's Sand Snake (Psammophis jallae) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Namibia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
