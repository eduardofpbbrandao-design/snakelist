Black Milksnake

Lampropeltis micropholis

Black Milksnake (Lampropeltis micropholis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Mexico, Panama and Venezuela. It is known to eat other snakes.

What the name means

Lampropeltis: from the Greek lampros (shiny) and peltē (small shield) - for the smooth, glossy scales[1]
