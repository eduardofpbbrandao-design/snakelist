About

Ashe's Spitting Cobra (Naja ashei) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 26 more. Recorded in 30 places on iNaturalist.
