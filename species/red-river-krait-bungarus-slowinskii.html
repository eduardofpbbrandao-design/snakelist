About

Red River Krait (Bungarus slowinskii) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 4 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
