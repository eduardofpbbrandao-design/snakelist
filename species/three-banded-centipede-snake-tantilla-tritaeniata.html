About

Three-banded Centipede Snake (Tantilla tritaeniata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Honduras. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
