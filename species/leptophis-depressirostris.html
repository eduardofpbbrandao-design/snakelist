About

Cope's Parrot Snake (Leptophis depressirostris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 93 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Costa Rica: Alajuela, Cartago, Guanacaste, Heredia, Limón, Puntarenas
  • Honduras
  • Nicaragua: South Caribbean Coast
  • Panama: Guna Yala, Panamá, Veraguas, comarca Ngäbe-Buglé
