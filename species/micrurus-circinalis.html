About

Trinidad Northern Coral Snake (Micrurus circinalis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
