About

Narrowhead Watersnake (Thamnophis rufipunctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 35 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
