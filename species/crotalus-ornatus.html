About

Eastern Black-tailed Rattlesnake (Crotalus ornatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 464 places on iNaturalist.
