About

Prairie Rattlesnake (Crotalus viridis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 2531 places on iNaturalist.
