About

Pastuso Threadsnake (Trilepida pastusa) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
