About

Karaganda pitviper (Gloydius caraganus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan. It lives in habitats such as grassland, temperate grassland, steppe, desert and 2 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
