About

Bangkok Mud Snake (Enhydris jagorii) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 10 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
