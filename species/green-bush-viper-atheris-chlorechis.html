About

Green Bush Viper (Atheris chlorechis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
