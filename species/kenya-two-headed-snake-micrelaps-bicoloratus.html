About

Kenya Two-headed Snake (Micrelaps bicoloratus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Kenya, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
