About

Taeniophallus occipitalis is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 24 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
