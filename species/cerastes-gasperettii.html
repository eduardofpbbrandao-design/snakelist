About

Arabian Sand Viper (Cerastes gasperettii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Oman and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 19 more. Common: recorded in 100 places on iNaturalist.
