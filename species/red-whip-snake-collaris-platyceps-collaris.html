About

Red Whip Snake (collaris) (Platyceps collaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bulgaria, Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 22 more. Common: recorded in 78 places on iNaturalist.
