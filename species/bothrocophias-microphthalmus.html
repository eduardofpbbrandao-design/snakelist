About

Small-eyed Toadhead (Bothrocophias microphthalmus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
