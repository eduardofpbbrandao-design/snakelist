About

White-bellied Reed Snake (Calamaria albiventer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
