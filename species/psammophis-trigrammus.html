About

Western Sand Snake (Psammophis trigrammus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Ghana, Namibia, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 24 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
