About

Incognito Thread Snake (Leptotyphlops incognitus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 14 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
