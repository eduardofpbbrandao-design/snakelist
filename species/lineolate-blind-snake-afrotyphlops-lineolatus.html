About

Lineolate Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops lineolatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 22 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 31 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
