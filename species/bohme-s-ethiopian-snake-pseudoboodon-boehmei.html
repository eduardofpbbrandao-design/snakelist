About

Böhme's Ethiopian Snake (Pseudoboodon boehmei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Ethiopia. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
