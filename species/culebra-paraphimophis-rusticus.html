About

Culebra (Paraphimophis rusticus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 136 places on iNaturalist.
