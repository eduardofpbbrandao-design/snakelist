About

Ornate Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira ornata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Common: recorded in 465 places on iNaturalist.
