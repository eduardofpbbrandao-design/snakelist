About

Mocquard's Swamp Snake (Tretanorhinus mocquardi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
