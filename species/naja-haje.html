About

Egyptian Cobra (Naja haje) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Algeria, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 20 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 44 more. Common: recorded in 76 places on iNaturalist.
