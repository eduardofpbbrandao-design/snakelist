About

Jararaca (Bothrops jararaca) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 623 places on iNaturalist.
