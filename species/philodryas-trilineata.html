About

Mousehole Snake (Philodryas trilineata) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 14 more. Common: recorded in 141 places on iNaturalist.
