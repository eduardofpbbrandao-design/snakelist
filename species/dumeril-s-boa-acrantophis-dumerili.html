Where the name comes from

dumerili: named after Duméril, the name of two French zoologists, André Duméril and his son Auguste

Dumeril's Boa: ‘Dumeril's’ – named after Duméril, the name of two French zoologists, André Duméril and his son Auguste; ‘Boa’ – from boa, a Latin word for a large snake.
