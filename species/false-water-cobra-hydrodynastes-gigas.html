About

False Water Cobra (Hydrodynastes gigas) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 46 more. Common: recorded in 185 places on iNaturalist.
