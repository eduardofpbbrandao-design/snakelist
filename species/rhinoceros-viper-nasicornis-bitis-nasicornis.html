Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis)

Bitis nasicornis

Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) (Bitis nasicornis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 17 more.
