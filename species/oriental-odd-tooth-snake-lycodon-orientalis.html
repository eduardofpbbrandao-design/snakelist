About

Oriental Odd-tooth Snake (Lycodon orientalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Japan, Russia. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest and 8 more. Common: recorded in 89 places on iNaturalist.
