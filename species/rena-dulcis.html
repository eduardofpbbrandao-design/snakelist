About

Texas Blind Snake (Rena dulcis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 711 places on iNaturalist.
