About

Khasi Hills Trinket Snake (Gonyosoma frenatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 86 places on iNaturalist.
