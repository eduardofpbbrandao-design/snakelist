About

São Paulo Reticulated Collared Snake (Phalotris mertensi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
