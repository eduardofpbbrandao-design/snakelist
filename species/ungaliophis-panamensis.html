About

Panamanian Dwarf Boa (Ungaliophis panamensis) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
