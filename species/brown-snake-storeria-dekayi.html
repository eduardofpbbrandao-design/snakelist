About

Brown Snake (Storeria dekayi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, temperate deciduous forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 8653 places on iNaturalist.
