About

Marsh Snake (fuliginoides) (Natriciteres fuliginoides) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 25 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
