About

Watson's Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis watsoni) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
