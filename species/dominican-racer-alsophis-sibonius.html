About

Dominican Racer (Alsophis sibonius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Dominica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
