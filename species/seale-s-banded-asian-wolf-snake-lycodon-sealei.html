About

Seale’s Banded Asian Wolf Snake (Lycodon sealei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Malaysia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
