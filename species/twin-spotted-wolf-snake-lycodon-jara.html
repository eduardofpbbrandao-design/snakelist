About

Twin-spotted Wolf Snake (Lycodon jara) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 15 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
