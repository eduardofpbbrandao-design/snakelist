About

Pygmy Mulga Snake (Pseudechis weigeli) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, eucalyptus forest, woodland, dry woodland and 18 more. Recorded in 47 places on iNaturalist.
