Where the name comes from

Thamnophis: from the Greek thamnos (bush or shrub) and ophis (snake)[1]

exsul: ‘exile’

Montane Garter Snake: ‘Montane’ – it lives in mountains; ‘Garter Snake’ – its stripes look like the stripes on old-fashioned sock garters.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
