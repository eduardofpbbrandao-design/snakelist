About

Jamaican Black Groundsnake (Hypsirhynchus funereus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Jamaica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
