About

Shire Slug Eater (Duberria shirana) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
