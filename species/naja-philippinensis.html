About

Philippine Cobra (Naja philippinensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Pakistan, Philippines, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
