About

Madagascar Night Snake (Madagascarophis colubrinus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 102 places on iNaturalist.
