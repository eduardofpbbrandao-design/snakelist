About

Canopy Snail-eating Snake (Sibon canopy) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, central american dry forest, panamanian rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
