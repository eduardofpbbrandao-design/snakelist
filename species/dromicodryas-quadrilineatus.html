About

Four-striped Snake (Dromicodryas quadrilineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 14 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
