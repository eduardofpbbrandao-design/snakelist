About

La Villa's Snail-eater (Dipsas lavillai) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
