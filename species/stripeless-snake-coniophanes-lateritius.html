About

Stripeless Snake (Coniophanes lateritius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 16 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
