About

Green Anaconda (Eunectes murinus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 42 more. Common: recorded in 503 places on iNaturalist.
