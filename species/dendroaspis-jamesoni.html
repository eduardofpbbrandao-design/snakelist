About

Jameson's Mamba (Dendroaspis jamesoni) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
