About

Gloyd's Moccasin (Agkistrodon howardgloydi) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 14 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
