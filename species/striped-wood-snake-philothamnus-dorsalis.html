Striped Wood Snake

Philothamnus dorsalis

Striped Wood Snake (Philothamnus dorsalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon and Republic of the Congo.
