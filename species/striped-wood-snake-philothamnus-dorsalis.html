About

Striped Wood Snake (Philothamnus dorsalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 14 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
