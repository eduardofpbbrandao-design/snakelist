About

Pale Ground Snake (Urotheca decipiens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
