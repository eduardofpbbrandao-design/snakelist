About

Woma (Aspidites ramsayi) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 78 places on iNaturalist.
