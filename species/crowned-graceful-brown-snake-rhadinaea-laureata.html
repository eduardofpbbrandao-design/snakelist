About

Crowned Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinaea laureata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, montane woodland and 13 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
