About

Bibron's Coral Snake (Calliophis bibroni) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in India. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 4 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
