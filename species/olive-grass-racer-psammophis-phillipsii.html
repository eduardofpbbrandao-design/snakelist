About

Olive Grass Racer (Psammophis phillipsii) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 23 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 29 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
