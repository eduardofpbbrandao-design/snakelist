About

Jerdon's Many-tooth Snake (Sibynophis subpunctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 14 more. Recorded in 74 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • India: Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Haryana, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Puducherry, Tamil Nadu, Telangana
