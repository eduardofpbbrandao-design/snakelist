About

Senegalese Cobra (Naja senegalensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
