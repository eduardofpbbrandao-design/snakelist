About

Western Shovelnose Snake (Sonora occipitalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 165 places on iNaturalist.
