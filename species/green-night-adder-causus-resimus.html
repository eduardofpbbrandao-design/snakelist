About

Green Night Adder (Causus resimus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 13 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
