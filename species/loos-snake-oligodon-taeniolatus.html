About

Loos Snake (Oligodon taeniolatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, montane woodland, mountain forest, subalpine forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 312 places on iNaturalist.
