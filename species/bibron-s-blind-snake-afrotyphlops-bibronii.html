Bibron's Blind Snake

Afrotyphlops bibronii

Bibron's Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops bibronii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, South Africa, Zimbabwe.
