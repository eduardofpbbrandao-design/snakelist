About

Western Whip Snake (Hierophis viridiflavus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in France, Malta, Monaco, San Marino, Spain and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 25 more. Common: recorded in 2751 places on iNaturalist.
