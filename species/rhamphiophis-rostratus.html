About

Rufous Beaked Snake (Rhamphiophis rostratus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 81 places on iNaturalist.
