About

Checkered Garter Snake (Thamnophis marcianus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, El Salvador, Guatemala, Mexico, Nicaragua and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 57 more. Common: recorded in 1486 places on iNaturalist.
