About

McClung's Philippine Coral Snake (Hemibungarus mcclungi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
