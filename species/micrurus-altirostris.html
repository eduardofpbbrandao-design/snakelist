About

Uruguayan coralsnake (Micrurus altirostris) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Common: recorded in 133 places on iNaturalist.
