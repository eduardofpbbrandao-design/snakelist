About

Mt Simpson Forest Snake (Toxicocalamus mattisoni) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
