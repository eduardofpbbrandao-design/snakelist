About

Variable Marsh Snake (variegata) (Natriciteres variegata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 30 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
