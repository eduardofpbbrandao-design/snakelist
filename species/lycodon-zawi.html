About

Zaw's Wolf Snake (Lycodon zawi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 14 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
