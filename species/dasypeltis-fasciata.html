About

Central African Egg-eating Snake (Dasypeltis fasciata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
