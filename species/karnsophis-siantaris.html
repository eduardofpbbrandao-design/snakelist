About

Sumatran Short-tailed Snake (Karnsophis siantaris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 18 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
