About

Werner's Diadem Snake (Spalerosophis dolichospilus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Morocco, Tunisia. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, montane woodland, mountain forest and 16 more. Recorded in 24 places on iNaturalist.
