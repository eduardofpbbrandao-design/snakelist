Where the name comes from

Bothrops: from the Greek bothros (pit) and ops (eye or face) - for the heat-sensing pits on the head[1]

sanctaecrucis: ‘of Santa Cruz’ – named after where it lives

Bolivian Lancehead: ‘Bolivian’ – it lives in Bolivia; ‘Lancehead’ – its head is shaped like the point of a lance.
