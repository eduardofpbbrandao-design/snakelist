Atlantic Central American Milksnake

Lampropeltis polyzona

Atlantic Central American Milksnake (Lampropeltis polyzona) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, United States.
