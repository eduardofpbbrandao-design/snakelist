About

North African Catsnake (Telescopus tripolitanus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Burkina Faso, Central African Republic, Libya, Mali, Mauritania and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 25 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
