About

Mair's Keelback (Tropidonophis mairii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 50 more. Common: recorded in 461 places on iNaturalist.
