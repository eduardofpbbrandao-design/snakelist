About

Eastern Ribbonsnake (Thamnophis saurita) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, subtropical forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 1540 places on iNaturalist.
