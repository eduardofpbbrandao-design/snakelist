About

Wide Ground Snake (Psomophis obtusus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Recorded in 26 places on iNaturalist.
