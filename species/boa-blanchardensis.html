Boa blanchardensis

Boa blanchardensis is a prehistoric snake of the family Boidae, known only from fossils. It died out in the late Pleistocene, about 12,000 years ago.

Fossils and geological context

Geological age

It lived in the late Pleistocene, about 12,000 years ago (dated from the rocks its fossils were found in).

Where it was found

2018, Corentin Bochaton and Salvador Bailon (Blanchard Cave, Marie-Galante, Guadeloupe).

Size estimate

Estimate: 73-139 cm, a dwarf island boa.

What is uncertain

Probably extinct at the end of the Pleistocene; it may possibly have survived a little into the Holocene.
