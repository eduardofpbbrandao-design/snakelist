About

Dark-headed Dwarf Racer (persicus) (Eirenis persicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Armenia, Iran, Iraq, Pakistan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as montane woodland, mountain forest, grassland, temperate grassland and 19 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
