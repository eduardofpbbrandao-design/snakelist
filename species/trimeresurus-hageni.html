About

Indonesian Pit Viper (Trimeresurus hageni) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 88 places on iNaturalist.
