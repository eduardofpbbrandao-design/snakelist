About

Natterer's Hognose Snake (Xenodon nattereri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 20 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
