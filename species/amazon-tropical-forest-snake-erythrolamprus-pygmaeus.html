Amazon Tropical Forest Snake

Erythrolamprus pygmaeus

Amazon Tropical Forest Snake (Erythrolamprus pygmaeus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela.
