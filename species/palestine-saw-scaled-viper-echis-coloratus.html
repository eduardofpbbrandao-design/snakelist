About

Palestine Saw-scaled Viper (Echis coloratus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Egypt, Israel, Jordan, Oman, Saudi Arabia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 19 more. Recorded in 66 places on iNaturalist.
