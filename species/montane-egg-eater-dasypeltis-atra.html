About

Montane Egg-eater (Dasypeltis atra) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
