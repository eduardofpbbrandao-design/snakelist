About

Coachwhip (Masticophis flagellum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 68 more. Common: recorded in 3528 places on iNaturalist.
