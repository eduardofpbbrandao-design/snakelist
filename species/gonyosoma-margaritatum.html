About

Rainbow Tree Snake (Gonyosoma margaritatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
