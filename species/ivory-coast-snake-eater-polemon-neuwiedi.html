Ivory Coast Snake-eater

Polemon neuwiedi

Ivory Coast Snake-eater (Polemon neuwiedi) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Mali and Togo. It is known to eat other snakes.
