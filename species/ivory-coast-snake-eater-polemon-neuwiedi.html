About

Ivory Coast Snake-eater (Polemon neuwiedi) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Ivory Coast, Mali and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
