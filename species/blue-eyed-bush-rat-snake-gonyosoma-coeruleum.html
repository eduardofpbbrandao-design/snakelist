Blue-eyed Bush Rat Snake

Gonyosoma coeruleum

Blue-eyed Bush Rat Snake (Gonyosoma coeruleum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam.
