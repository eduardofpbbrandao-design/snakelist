About

Battersby's Green Snake (Philothamnus battersbyi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
