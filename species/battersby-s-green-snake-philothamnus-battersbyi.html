Battersby's Green Snake

Philothamnus battersbyi

Battersby's Green Snake (Philothamnus battersbyi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia and 3 more.
