About

Beautiful calico snake (Oxyrhopus formosus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 27 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
