About

Variable Reed Snake (Calamaria lumbricoidea) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
