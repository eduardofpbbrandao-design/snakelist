About

Rio Grande do Sul Blind Snake (Epictia munoai) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 24 places on iNaturalist.
