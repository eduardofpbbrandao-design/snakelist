About

Amazon Banded Snake (Rhinobothryum lentiginosum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
