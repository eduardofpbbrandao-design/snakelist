About

Mexican Yellowbelly Brown Snake (Storeria hidalgoensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
