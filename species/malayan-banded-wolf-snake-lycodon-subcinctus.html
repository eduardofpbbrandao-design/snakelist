About

Malayan Banded Wolf Snake (Lycodon subcinctus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 121 places on iNaturalist.
