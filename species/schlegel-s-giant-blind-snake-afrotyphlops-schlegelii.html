About

Schlegel's Giant Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops schlegelii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya and 8 more. Common: recorded in 108 places on iNaturalist.
