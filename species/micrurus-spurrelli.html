About

Chocó Coral Snake (Micrurus spurrelli) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, chocó rainforest. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
