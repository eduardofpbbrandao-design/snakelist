About

Banded Cat Snake (Boiga saengsomi) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
