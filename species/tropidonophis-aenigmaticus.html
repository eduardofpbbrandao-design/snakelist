About

East Papuan Keelback (Tropidonophis aenigmaticus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
