About

Bornean Reed Snake (Calamaria borneensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
