About

Horseshoe Whip Snake (Hemorrhois hippocrepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Gibraltar, Italy, Portugal, Spain. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Ibiza, Formentera and Mallorca), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, shrubland and 17 more. Common: recorded in 913 places on iNaturalist.
