About

Santa Cruz Garter Snake (Thamnophis atratus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, forest edges, shrubland and 26 more. Common: recorded in 336 places on iNaturalist.
