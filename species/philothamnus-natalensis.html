About

Natal Green Snake (Philothamnus natalensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Eswatini, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 84 places on iNaturalist.
