Black-necked Spitting Cobra

Naja nigricollis

Black-necked Spitting Cobra (Naja nigricollis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 29 more.
