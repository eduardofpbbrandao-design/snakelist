About

Japanese Four-lined Ratsnake (Elaphe quadrivirgata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Japan. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 454 places on iNaturalist.
