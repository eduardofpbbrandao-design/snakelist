About

Two-colored Blind Snake (Tricheilostoma bicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Chad, Ghana and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 18 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
