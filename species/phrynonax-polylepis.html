About

Northeastern Puffing Snake (Phrynonax polylepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
