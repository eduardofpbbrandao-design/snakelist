About

Eastern Russel's Viper (Daboia siamensis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Myanmar and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 26 more. Recorded in 54 places on iNaturalist.
