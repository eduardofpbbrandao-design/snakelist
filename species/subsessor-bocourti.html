About

Bocourt's Mud Snake (Subsessor bocourti) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, China, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 8 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
