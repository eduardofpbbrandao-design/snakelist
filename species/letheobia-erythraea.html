About

Eritrean Blind Snake (Letheobia erythraea) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Eritrea. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest, montane woodland and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
