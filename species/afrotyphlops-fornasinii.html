About

Fornasini's Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops fornasinii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest, underground burrows and 7 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
