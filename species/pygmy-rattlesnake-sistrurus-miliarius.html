About

Pygmy Rattlesnake (Sistrurus miliarius) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 317 places on iNaturalist.
