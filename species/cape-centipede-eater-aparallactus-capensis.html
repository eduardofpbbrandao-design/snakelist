About

Cape Centipede Eater (Aparallactus capensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Lesotho, Malawi and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 282 places on iNaturalist.
