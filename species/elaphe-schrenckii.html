About

Amur Ratsnake (Elaphe schrenckii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Russia. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 3 more. Common: recorded in 220 places on iNaturalist.
