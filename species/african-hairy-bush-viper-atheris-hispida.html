About

African Hairy Bush Viper (Atheris hispida) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
