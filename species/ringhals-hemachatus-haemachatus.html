About

Ringhals (Hemachatus haemachatus) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Eswatini, Lesotho, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 163 places on iNaturalist.
