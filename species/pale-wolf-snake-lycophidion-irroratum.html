About

Pale Wolf Snake (Lycophidion irroratum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 12 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
