About

Mesopotamian coral snake (Micrurus baliocoryphus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Paraguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, grassland, temperate grassland and 1 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
