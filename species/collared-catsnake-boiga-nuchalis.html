About

Collared Catsnake (Boiga nuchalis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 19 more. Recorded in 62 places on iNaturalist.
