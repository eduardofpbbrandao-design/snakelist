About

Mertens' Coral Snake (Micrurus mertensi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Ecuador, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 5 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
