About

Meadow Viper (Vipera ursinii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Albania, Austria, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 99 places on iNaturalist.
