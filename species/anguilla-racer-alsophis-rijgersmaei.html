About

Anguilla Racer (Alsophis rijgersmaei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Anguilla, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin, Sint Maarten. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, caribbean dry forest. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
