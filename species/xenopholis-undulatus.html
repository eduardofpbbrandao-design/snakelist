About

Jensen's Ground Snake (Xenopholis undulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 22 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
