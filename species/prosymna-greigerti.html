About

Southern Sahel Speckled Shovel-snout (Prosymna greigerti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
