About

Guyana Blackback Coral Snake (Micrurus collaris) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
