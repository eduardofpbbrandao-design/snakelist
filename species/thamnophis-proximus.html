About

Western Ribbonsnake (Thamnophis proximus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 72 more. Common: recorded in 2805 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Mexico: Campeche, Chiapas, Coahuila, Guerrero, Hidalgo, Nuevo León, Oaxaca, Puebla, Quintana Roo, San Luis Potosí, Tamaulipas, Veracruz, Yucatán
  • United States: AL, AR, IL, IN, KS, LA, MO, MS, NE, NM, OK, TN, TX, WI
