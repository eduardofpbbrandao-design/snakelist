About

Large Whip Snake (jugularis) (Dolichophis jugularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bulgaria, Cyprus, Greece, Iran, Iraq and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 199 places on iNaturalist.
