About

Zug's Odd-scaled Snake (Achalinus zugorum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Xenodermidae, found in China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
