About

Snail-eating Thirst Snake (Dipsas brevifacies) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 3 more. Common: recorded in 104 places on iNaturalist.
