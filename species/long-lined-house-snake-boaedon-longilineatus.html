About

Long-lined House Snake (Boaedon longilineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Cameroon, Chad. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
