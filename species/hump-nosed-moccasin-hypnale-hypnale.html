About

Hump-nosed Moccasin (Hypnale hypnale) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in India, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 213 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

Native range

  • Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura, Badulla, Colombo, Galle, Gampaha, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Matale, Matara, Monaragala, Nuwara Eliya, Ratnapura
