About

Himalayan Stripe-necked Snake (Liopeltis rappi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
