About

Yellow-bellied Reed Snake (Calamaria suluensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
