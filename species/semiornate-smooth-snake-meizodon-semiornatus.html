About

Semiornate Smooth Snake (Meizodon semiornatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, coastal forest, woodland, dry woodland and 13 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
