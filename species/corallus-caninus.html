About

Emerald Tree Boa (Corallus caninus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana, Guyana and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 91 places on iNaturalist.
