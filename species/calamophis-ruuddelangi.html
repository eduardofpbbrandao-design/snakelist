About

Kebar Stout-tailed Snake (Calamophis ruuddelangi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
