About

Armenian Viper (Montivipera raddei) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as mountain forest, steppe, alpine meadow, alpine grassland and 13 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
