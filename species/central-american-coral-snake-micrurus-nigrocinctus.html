About

Central American Coral Snake (Micrurus nigrocinctus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 292 places on iNaturalist.
