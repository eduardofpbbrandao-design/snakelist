About

Eastern Crowned Smooth Snake (Meizodon regularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 10 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
