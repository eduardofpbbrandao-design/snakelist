About

Painted Lancehead (Bothrops diporus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 255 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Argentina: Catamarca, Chaco, Corrientes, Córdoba, Entre Ríos, Formosa, Jujuy, La Pampa, La Rioja, Mendoza, Misiones, Neuquén, Río Negro, Salta, San Juan, San Luis, Santa Fe, Santiago del Estero
  • Bolivia: Chuquisaca, Santa Cruz
  • Brazil: Mato Grosso do Sul, Paraná, Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina, São Paulo
  • Paraguay: Alto Paraguay, Alto Paraná, Boquerón, Central, Cordillera, Itapúa, Paraguarí, Presidente Hayes
