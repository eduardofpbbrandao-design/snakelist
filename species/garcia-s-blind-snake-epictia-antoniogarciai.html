About

García's Blind Snake (Epictia antoniogarciai) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Peru. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
