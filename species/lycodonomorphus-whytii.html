About

Whyte's Water Snake (Lycodonomorphus whytii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Mozambique, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
