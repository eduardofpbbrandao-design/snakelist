About

Middle American Burrowing Snake (visoninum) (Adelphicos visoninum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
