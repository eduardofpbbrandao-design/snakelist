About

Central America Lizard Eater (Mastigodryas alternatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 80 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Costa Rica: Alajuela, Heredia, Limón, Puntarenas, San José
  • Honduras
  • Nicaragua: Managua
  • Panama: Chiriquí, Coclé, Colón, Panamá, Veraguas, comarca Ngäbe-Buglé
