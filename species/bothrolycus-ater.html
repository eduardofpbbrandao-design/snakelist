About

Günther's Black Snake (Bothrolycus ater) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
