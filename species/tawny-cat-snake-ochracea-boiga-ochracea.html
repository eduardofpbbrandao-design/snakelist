About

Tawny Cat Snake (ochracea) (Boiga ochracea) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
