Mexican Parrot Snake

Leptophis mexicanus

Mexican Parrot Snake (Leptophis mexicanus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and 2 more.
