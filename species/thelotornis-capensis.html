About

Twig Snake (Thelotornis capensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Malawi and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 92 places on iNaturalist.
