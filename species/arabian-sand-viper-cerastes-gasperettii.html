Where the name comes from

gasperettii: named after a person (the ending -ii means ‘of’ someone)

Arabian Sand Viper: ‘Arabian’ – after Arabia; ‘Viper’ – from the Latin vipera, ‘bearing live young’ - most vipers give birth instead of laying eggs.
