About

Nganson Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis ngansonensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 102 places on iNaturalist.
