About

Banded Racer (fasciolatus) (Lycodon fasciolatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 179 places on iNaturalist.
