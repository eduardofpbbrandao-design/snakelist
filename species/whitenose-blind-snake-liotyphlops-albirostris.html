About

Whitenose Blind Snake (Liotyphlops albirostris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 10 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
