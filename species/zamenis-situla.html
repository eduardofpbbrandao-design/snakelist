About

Leopard Snake (Zamenis situla) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Crimea, Italy, Malta. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 7 more. Common: recorded in 276 places on iNaturalist.
