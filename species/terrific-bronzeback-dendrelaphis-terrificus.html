About

Terrific Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis terrificus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 9 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
