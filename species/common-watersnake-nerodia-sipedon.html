About

Common Watersnake (Nerodia sipedon) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 7942 places on iNaturalist.
