About

Günther's Green Racer (Philodryas psammophidea) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 98 places on iNaturalist.
