About

Barbour's Tropical Racer (Mastigodryas bruesi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Grenada, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, secondary forest and 5 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
