About

Southeastern Scarlet Snake (Pseudoboa serrana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, coastal forest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
