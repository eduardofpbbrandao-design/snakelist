About

Striped House Snake (Boaedon lineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 15 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
