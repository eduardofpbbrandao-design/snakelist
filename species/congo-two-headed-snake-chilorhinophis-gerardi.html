About

Congo Two-headed Snake (Chilorhinophis gerardi) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
