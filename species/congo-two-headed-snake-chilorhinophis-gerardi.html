Congo Two-headed Snake

Chilorhinophis gerardi

Congo Two-headed Snake (Chilorhinophis gerardi) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia, Zimbabwe.
