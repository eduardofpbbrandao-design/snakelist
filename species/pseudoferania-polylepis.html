About

Macleay's Mud Snake (Pseudoferania polylepis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 47 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
