About

Negros Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis fuliginosus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, secondary forest and 5 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
