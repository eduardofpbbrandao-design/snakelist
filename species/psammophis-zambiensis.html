About

Zambian Whip Snake (Psammophis zambiensis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
