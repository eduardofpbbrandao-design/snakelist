About

Atractus pantostictus is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 17 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
