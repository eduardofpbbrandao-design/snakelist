About

Dominican Worm Snake (Antillotyphlops dominicanus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Dominica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, underground burrows and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
