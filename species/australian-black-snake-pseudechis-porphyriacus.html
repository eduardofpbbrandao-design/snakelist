About

Australian black snake (Pseudechis porphyriacus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, temperate rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 49 more. Common: recorded in 1303 places on iNaturalist.
