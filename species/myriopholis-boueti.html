About

Bouet's Worm Snake (Myriopholis boueti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Burkina Faso, Chad, Mali, Mauritania, Niger and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest and 15 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
