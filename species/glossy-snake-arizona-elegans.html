About

Glossy Snake (Arizona elegans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 582 places on iNaturalist.
