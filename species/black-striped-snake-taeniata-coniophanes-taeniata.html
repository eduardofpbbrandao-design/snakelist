About

Black-striped Snake (taeniata) (Coniophanes taeniata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
