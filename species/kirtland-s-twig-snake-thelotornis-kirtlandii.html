Kirtland's Twig Snake

Thelotornis kirtlandii

Kirtland's Twig Snake (Thelotornis kirtlandii) is a venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 21 more.
