About

Yellow-spotted Wolf Snake (Lycodon flavomaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 18 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
