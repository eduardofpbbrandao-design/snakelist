About

Barred Wolf Snake (Lycodon striatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, India, Iran, Nepal, Pakistan and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 119 places on iNaturalist.
