About

Mexican Moccasin (Agkistrodon bilineatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 34 more. Common: recorded in 192 places on iNaturalist.
