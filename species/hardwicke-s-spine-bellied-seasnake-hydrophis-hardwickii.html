Hardwicke's Spine-bellied Seasnake

Hydrophis hardwickii

Hardwicke's Spine-bellied Seasnake (Hydrophis hardwickii) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Iran, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand and United Arab Emirates.

    Where the name comes from

    Hydrophis: ‘water snake’, from the Greek roots hydr- ‘water’ + -ophis ‘snake’

    hardwickii: named after Thomas Hardwicke, a British army officer and naturalist in India

    Hardwicke's Spine-bellied Seasnake: ‘Hardwicke's’ – named after Thomas Hardwicke, a British army officer and naturalist in India; ‘Seasnake’ – it lives in the sea.
