About

Kinkelin Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinella kinkelini) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 3 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
