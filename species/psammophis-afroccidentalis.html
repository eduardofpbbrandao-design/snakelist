About

Kalahari Sand Snake (Psammophis afroccidentalis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Algeria, Benin, Burkina Faso, Chad, Gambia and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 25 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
