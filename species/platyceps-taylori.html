About

Taylor's Racer (Platyceps taylori) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Djibouti, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, dry woodland, scrub forest, thorn forest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
