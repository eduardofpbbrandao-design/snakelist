About

Bibron's Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops bibronii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 171 places on iNaturalist.
