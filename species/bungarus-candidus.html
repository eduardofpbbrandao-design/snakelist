About

Blue Krait (Bungarus candidus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Laos and 3 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 257 places on iNaturalist.
