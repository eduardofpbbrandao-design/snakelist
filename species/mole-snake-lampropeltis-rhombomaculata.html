About

Mole snake (Lampropeltis rhombomaculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 355 places on iNaturalist.
