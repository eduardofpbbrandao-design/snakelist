About

Chinese Bamboo Snake (Pseudoxenodon karlschmidti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
