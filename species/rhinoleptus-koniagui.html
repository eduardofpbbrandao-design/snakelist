About

Villiers' Blind Snake (Rhinoleptus koniagui) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Senegal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 17 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
