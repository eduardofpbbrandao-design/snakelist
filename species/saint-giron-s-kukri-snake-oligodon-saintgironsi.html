About

Saint Giron's Kukri Snake (Oligodon saintgironsi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 5 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
