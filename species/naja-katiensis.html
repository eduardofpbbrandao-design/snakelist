About

Mali Cobra (Naja katiensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 23 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
