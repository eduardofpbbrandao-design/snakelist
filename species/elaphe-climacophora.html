About

Japanese Ratsnake (Elaphe climacophora) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Japan, Southern Kuril Islands. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 477 places on iNaturalist.
