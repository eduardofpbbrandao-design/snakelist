About

Striped Lizard Eater (Mastigodryas dorsalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
