About

Copperhead (Agkistrodon contortrix) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 4807 places on iNaturalist.
