About

Coast Nightsnake (Hypsiglena ochrorhynchus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, shrubland, scrubland, chaparral and 14 more. Common: recorded in 307 places on iNaturalist.
