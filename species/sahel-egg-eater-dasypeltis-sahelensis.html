About

Sahel Egg Eater (Dasypeltis sahelensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Burkina Faso, Chad, Mali, Mauritania, Morocco and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 16 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
