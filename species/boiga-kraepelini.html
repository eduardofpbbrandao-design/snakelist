About

Kelung Cat Snake (Boiga kraepelini) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 364 places on iNaturalist.
