Nose-horned Viper

Vipera ammodytes

Nose-horned Viper (Vipera ammodytes) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Albania, Austria, Azerbaijan, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria and 11 more.
