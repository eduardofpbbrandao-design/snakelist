About

African Saw-scaled Viper (Echis ocellatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
