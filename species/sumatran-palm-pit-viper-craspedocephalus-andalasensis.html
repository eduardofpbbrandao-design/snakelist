About

Sumatran Palm Pit Viper (Craspedocephalus andalasensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 5 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
