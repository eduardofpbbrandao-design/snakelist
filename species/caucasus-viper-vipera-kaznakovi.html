About

Caucasus Viper (Vipera kaznakovi) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Georgia, Russia, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, mountain forest, grassland, temperate grassland and 6 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
