About

Olive Whip Snake (Psammophis mossambicus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 50 more. Common: recorded in 257 places on iNaturalist.
