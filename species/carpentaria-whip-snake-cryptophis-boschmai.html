About

Carpentaria Whip Snake (Cryptophis boschmai) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 64 places on iNaturalist.
