About

Fischer's Snail-eating Snake (Tropidodipsas fischeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Recorded in 32 places on iNaturalist.
