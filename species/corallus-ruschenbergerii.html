About

Dormilona (Corallus ruschenbergerii) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Panama, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 185 places on iNaturalist.
