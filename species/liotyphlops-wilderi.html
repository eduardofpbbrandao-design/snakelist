About

Wilder's Blind Snake (Liotyphlops wilderi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
