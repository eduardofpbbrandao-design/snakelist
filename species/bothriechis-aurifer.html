About

Yellow-blotched Palm Pit Viper (Bothriechis aurifer) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
