About

Zweifel's Snail-eating Snake (Tropidodipsas zweifeli) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
