About

Lined Olympic Snake (Psammophis lineatus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 24 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
