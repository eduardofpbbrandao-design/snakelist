About

Slevin's Tropical Ground Snake (Trimetopon slevini) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
