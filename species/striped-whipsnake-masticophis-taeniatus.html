About

Striped Whipsnake (Masticophis taeniatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 704 places on iNaturalist.
