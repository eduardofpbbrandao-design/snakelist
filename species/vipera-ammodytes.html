About

Nose-horned Viper (Vipera ammodytes) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Albania, Austria, Azerbaijan, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 417 places on iNaturalist.
