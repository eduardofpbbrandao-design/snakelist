About

Eastern Rainbow Boa (Epicrates crassus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
