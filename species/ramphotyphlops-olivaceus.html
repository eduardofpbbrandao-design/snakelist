About

Olive Blind Snake (Ramphotyphlops olivaceus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Indonesia, Philippines, Solomon Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
