About

Black-striped Rat Snake (Ptyas dhumnades) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 286 places on iNaturalist.
