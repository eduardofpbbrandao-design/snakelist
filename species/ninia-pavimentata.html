About

Northern Banded Coffee Snake (Ninia pavimentata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Honduras. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
