About

White-headed Beaked Snake (Rhinotyphlops leucocephalus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, scrub forest, thorn forest, semi-desert and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
