About

Striped Blind Snake (Ramphotyphlops lineatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
