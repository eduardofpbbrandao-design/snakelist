About

Common Krait (Bungarus caeruleus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, India, Myanmar, Nepal and 2 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, montane woodland and 17 more. Common: recorded in 365 places on iNaturalist.
