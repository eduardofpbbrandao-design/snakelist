About

Gore's Bronzeback (biloreatus) (Dendrelaphis biloreatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Myanmar, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
