About

Brown's Coral Snake (Micrurus browni) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 80 places on iNaturalist.
