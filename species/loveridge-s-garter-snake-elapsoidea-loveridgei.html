About

Loveridge's Garter Snake (Elapsoidea loveridgei) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
