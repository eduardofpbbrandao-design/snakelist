About

Northwestern Garter Snake (Thamnophis ordinoides) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 1523 places on iNaturalist.
