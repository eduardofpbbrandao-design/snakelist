Where the name comes from

elegans: ‘elegant’

Elegant Pitviper: ‘Elegant’ – its elegant look; ‘Pitviper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
