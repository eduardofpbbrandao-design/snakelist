About

Spotted Python (Antaresia maculosa) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 276 places on iNaturalist.
