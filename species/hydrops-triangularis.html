About

Triangle Water Snake (Hydrops triangularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 40 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
