About

Small-Scaled Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis microlepidota) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Ethiopia, Gambia, Kenya, Mali and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
