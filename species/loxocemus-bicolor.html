About

Mexican burrowing python (Loxocemus bicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Loxocemidae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 92 places on iNaturalist.
