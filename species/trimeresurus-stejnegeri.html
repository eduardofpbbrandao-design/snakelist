About

Chinese Green Tree Viper (Trimeresurus stejnegeri) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 600 places on iNaturalist.
