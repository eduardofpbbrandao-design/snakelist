About

Central Asian Cobra (Naja oxiana) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Afghanistan, India, Iran, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 16 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
