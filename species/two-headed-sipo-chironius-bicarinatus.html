Where the name comes from

bicarinatus: ‘two-keeled’, from Latin roots: bi- ‘two’ + -carinatus ‘keeled’

Two-headed Sipo: ‘Two-headed’ – for its two head; ‘Sipo’ – from the Portuguese cipó, a jungle vine.
