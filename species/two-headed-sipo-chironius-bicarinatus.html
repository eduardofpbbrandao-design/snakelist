About

Two-headed Sipo (Chironius bicarinatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 154 places on iNaturalist.
