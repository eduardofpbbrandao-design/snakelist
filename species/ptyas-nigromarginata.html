About

Green Rat Snake (nigromarginata) (Ptyas nigromarginata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 29 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
