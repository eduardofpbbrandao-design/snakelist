About

Western Worm Snake (Carphophis vermis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 300 places on iNaturalist.
