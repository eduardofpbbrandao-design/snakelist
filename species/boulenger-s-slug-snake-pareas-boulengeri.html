About

Boulenger's Slug Snake (Pareas boulengeri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.
