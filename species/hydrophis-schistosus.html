About

Common Seasnake (Hydrophis schistosus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, India, Indonesia, Iran, Malaysia and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Recorded in 56 places on iNaturalist.
