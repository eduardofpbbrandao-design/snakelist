About

Southern Smooth Snake (Coronella girondica) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in France, Gibraltar, Italy, Portugal, Spain. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 1189 places on iNaturalist.
