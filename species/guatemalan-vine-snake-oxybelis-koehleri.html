Guatemalan Vine Snake

Oxybelis koehleri

Guatemalan Vine Snake (Oxybelis koehleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and Nicaragua.
