About

Bolivian Coral Snake (serranus) (Micrurus serranus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bolivia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, semi-desert and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
