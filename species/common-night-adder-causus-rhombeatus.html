Common Night Adder

Causus rhombeatus

Common Night Adder (Causus rhombeatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 18 more.

Where the name comes from

rhombeatus: ‘diamond-patterned’

Common Night Adder: ‘Common’ – it is the commonest or best-known of its kind; ‘Adder’ – from the Old English nædre, ‘snake’ (‘a nadder’ became ‘an adder’).
