About

Common Night Adder (Causus rhombeatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 18 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 48 more. Common: recorded in 308 places on iNaturalist.
