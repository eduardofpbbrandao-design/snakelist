About

Ornate Wolf Snake (Lycophidion ornatum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
