About

Western Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira punctata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 10 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
