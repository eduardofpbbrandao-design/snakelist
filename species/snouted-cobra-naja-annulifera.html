Snouted Cobra

Naja annulifera

Snouted Cobra (Naja annulifera) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 3 more.

Where the name comes from

Naja: from the Sanskrit nāga, meaning snake[1]

annulifera: ‘ring-bearing’

Snouted Cobra: ‘Cobra’ – from the Portuguese cobra de capelo, ‘hooded snake’.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
