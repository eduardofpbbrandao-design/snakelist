About

Snouted Cobra (Naja annulifera) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 149 places on iNaturalist.
