About

Bombay Sea Snake (Hydrophis mamillaris) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
