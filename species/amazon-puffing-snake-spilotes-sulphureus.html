About

Amazon Puffing Snake (Spilotes sulphureus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, French Guiana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 162 places on iNaturalist.
