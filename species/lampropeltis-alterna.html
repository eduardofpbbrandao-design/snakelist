About

Gray-banded Kingsnake (Lampropeltis alterna) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, montane woodland, mountain forest, forest edges and 21 more. Common: recorded in 146 places on iNaturalist.
