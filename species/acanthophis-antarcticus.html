About

Common Death Adder (Acanthophis antarcticus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 190 places on iNaturalist.
