About

Banded Snake (Pseudoboodon lemniscatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eritrea, Ethiopia. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, dry woodland and 12 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
