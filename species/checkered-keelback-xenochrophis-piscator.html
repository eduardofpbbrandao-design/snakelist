About

Checkered Keelback (Xenochrophis piscator) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, China, India, Indonesia, Laos and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 28 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
