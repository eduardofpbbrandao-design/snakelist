About

California Mountain Kingsnake (Lampropeltis zonata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, forest edges, shrubland and 16 more. Common: recorded in 325 places on iNaturalist.
