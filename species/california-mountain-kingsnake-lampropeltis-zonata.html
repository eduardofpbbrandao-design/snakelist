California Mountain Kingsnake

Lampropeltis zonata

California Mountain Kingsnake (Lampropeltis zonata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico and United States. It is known to eat other snakes.

What the name means

Lampropeltis: from the Greek lampros (shiny) and peltē (small shield) - for the smooth, glossy scales[1]
