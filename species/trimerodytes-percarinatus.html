About

Chinese Keelback Water Snake (Trimerodytes percarinatus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Laos, Myanmar, Taiwan and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 253 places on iNaturalist.
