About

Wall's Sind Krait (Bungarus walli) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
