About

South Florida Mole Kingsnake (Lampropeltis occipitolineata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest, forest edges and 7 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
