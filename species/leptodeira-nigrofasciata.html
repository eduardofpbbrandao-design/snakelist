About

Black-banded Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira nigrofasciata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 127 places on iNaturalist.

  • El Salvador: La Libertad
