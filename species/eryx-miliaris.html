About

Dwarf Sand Boa (Eryx miliaris) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Afghanistan, China, Iran, Iraq, Kazakhstan and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, mountain forest, subalpine forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 155 places on iNaturalist.
