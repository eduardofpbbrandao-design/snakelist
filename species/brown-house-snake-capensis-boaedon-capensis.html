Brown House Snake (capensis)

Boaedon capensis

Brown House Snake (capensis) (Boaedon capensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Kenya, Lesotho, Malawi and 6 more.

Where the name comes from

capensis: ‘from the Cape (South Africa)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Brown House Snake (capensis): ‘Brown’ – its colour.
