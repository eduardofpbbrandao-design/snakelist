About

Brown House Snake (capensis) (Boaedon capensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Kenya, Lesotho, Malawi and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 42 more. Common: recorded in 748 places on iNaturalist.
