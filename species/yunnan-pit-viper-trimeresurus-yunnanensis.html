About

Yunnan Pit Viper (Trimeresurus yunnanensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
