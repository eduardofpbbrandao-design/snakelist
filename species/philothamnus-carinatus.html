About

Thirteen-scaled Green Snake (Philothamnus carinatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 15 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
