About

Lined Snake (Tropidoclonion lineatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 692 places on iNaturalist.
