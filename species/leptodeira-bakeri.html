About

Baker's Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira bakeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Aruba, Colombia, Curaçao, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 8 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
