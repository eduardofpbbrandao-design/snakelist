About

Alburzi Viper (Vipera eriwanensis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Iran, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as mountain forest, alpine meadow, alpine grassland, alpine shrubland and 1 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
