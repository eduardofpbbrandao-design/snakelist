About

Sand Boa (jaculus) (Eryx jaculus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Algeria, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Cyprus and 15 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 197 places on iNaturalist.
