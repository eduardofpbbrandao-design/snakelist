About

Lead Keelback (Rhabdophis plumbicolor) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in India, Myanmar, Pakistan, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Common: recorded in 103 places on iNaturalist.
