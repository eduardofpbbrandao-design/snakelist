About

Merker's Thread Snake (Leptotyphlops merkeri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Kenya, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland and 15 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
