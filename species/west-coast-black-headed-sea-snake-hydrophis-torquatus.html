About

West Coast Black-headed Sea Snake (Hydrophis torquatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
