About

Thickhead Ground Snake (Atractus crassicaudatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 7 more. Common: recorded in 81 places on iNaturalist.
