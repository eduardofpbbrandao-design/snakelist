About

Asiatic Water Snake (Trimerodytes aequifasciatus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, evergreen forest, broadleaf forest, bamboo forest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
