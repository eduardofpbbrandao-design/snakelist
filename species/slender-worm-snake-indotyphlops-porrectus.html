About

Slender Worm Snake (Indotyphlops porrectus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in India, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 13 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
