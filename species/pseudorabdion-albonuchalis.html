About

White-collared Reed Snake (Pseudorabdion albonuchalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
