About

Triangle Many-tooth Snake (Sibynophis triangularis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, indochina dry forest. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
