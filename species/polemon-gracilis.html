About

Graceful Snake-eater (Polemon gracilis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Cameroon. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
