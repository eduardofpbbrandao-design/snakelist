Where the name comes from

Phyllorhynchus: ‘leaf snout’, from the Greek roots phyllo- ‘leaf’ + -rhynchus ‘snout’

Spotted Leafnose Snake: ‘Spotted’ – its spots; ‘Leafnose Snake’ – it has a leaf-shaped scale on its snout.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
