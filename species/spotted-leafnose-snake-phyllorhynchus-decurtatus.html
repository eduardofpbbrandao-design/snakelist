About

Spotted Leafnose Snake (Phyllorhynchus decurtatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, pine forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 262 places on iNaturalist.
