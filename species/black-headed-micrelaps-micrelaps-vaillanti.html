About

Black-headed Micrelaps (Micrelaps vaillanti) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Chad, Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, coastal forest and 28 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
