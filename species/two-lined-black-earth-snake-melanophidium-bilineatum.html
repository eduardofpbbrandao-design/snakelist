Where the name comes from

bilineatum: ‘two-lined’, from Latin roots: bi- ‘two’ + -lineatum ‘lined’

Two-lined Black Earth Snake: ‘Two-lined’ – for its two lines; ‘Black’ – its colour; ‘Earth Snake’ – it burrows in the soil.
