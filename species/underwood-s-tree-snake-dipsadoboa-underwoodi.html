About

Underwood's Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa underwoodi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Ghana and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
