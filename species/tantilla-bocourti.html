About

Bocourt's Black-headed Snake (Tantilla bocourti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.
