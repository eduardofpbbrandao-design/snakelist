About

Brazilian Coral Snake (Micrurus decoratus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 12 more. Recorded in 17 places on iNaturalist.
