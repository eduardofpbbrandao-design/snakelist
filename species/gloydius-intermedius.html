About

Central Asian pitviper (Gloydius intermedius) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, east asian temperate forest. Common: recorded in 169 places on iNaturalist.
