About

Kramer's Pit Viper (Trimeresurus macrops) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 113 places on iNaturalist.
