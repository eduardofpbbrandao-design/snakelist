About

Spotted Water Snake (Tropidonophis dendrophiops) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 16 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
