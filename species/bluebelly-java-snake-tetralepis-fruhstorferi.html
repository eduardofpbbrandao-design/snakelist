About

Bluebelly Java Snake (Tetralepis fruhstorferi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 3 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
