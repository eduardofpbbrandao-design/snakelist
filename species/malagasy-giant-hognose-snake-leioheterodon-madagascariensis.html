Where the name comes from

madagascariensis: ‘from Madagascar’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Malagasy Giant Hognose Snake: ‘Malagasy’ – it lives in Madagascar; ‘Giant’ – it is very big; ‘Hognose Snake’ – its upturned snout looks like a pig's.
