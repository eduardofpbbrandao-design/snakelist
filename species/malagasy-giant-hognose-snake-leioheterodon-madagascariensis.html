About

Malagasy Giant Hognose Snake (Leioheterodon madagascariensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 86 places on iNaturalist.
