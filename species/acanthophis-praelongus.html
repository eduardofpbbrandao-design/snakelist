About

Northern Death Adder (Acanthophis praelongus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 60 places on iNaturalist.
