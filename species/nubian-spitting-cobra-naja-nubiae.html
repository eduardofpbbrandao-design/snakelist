Nubian Spitting Cobra

Naja nubiae

Nubian Spitting Cobra (Naja nubiae) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Chad, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Niger and Sudan.

Where the name comes from

Naja: from the Sanskrit nāga, meaning snake[1]

nubiae: ‘of Nubia’ – named after where it lives

Nubian Spitting Cobra: ‘Spitting Cobra’ – it can spit venom at the eyes of an attacker.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
