About

Nubian Spitting Cobra (Naja nubiae) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Chad, Djibouti, Egypt, Eritrea, Niger and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as gallery forest, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 21 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
