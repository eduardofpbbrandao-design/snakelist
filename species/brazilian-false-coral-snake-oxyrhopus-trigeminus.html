About

Brazilian False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus trigeminus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 35 more. Common: recorded in 274 places on iNaturalist.
