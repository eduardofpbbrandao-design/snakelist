About

Trinidad coralsnake (Micrurus diutius) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
