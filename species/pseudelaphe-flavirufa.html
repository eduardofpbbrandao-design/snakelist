About

Yellow-red Rat Snake (Pseudelaphe flavirufa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Recorded in 61 places on iNaturalist.
