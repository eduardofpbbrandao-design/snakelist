About

Ruthvens Kingsnake (Lampropeltis ruthveni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 14 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
