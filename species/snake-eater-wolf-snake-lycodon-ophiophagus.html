About

Snake-Eater Wolf Snake (Lycodon ophiophagus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Thailand. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
