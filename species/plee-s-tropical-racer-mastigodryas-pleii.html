About

Plee's Tropical Racer (Mastigodryas pleii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Panama, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 113 places on iNaturalist.
