About

Potyguara Coral Snake (Micrurus potyguara) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 6 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
