About

Two-striped Shovel-snout (Prosymna bivittata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Botswana, Namibia, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland, dry savanna and 14 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
