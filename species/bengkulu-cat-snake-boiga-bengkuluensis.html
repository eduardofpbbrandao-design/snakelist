About

Bengkulu Cat Snake (Boiga bengkuluensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.
