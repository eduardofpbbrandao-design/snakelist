About

Bismarck sharp-nosed blindsnake (Acutotyphlops subocularis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Ireland, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, temperate deciduous forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
