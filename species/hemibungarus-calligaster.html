About

Barred Coral Snake (calligaster) (Hemibungarus calligaster) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 6 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
