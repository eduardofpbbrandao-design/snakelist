About

Mlanje White-bellied Water-Snake (Lycodonomorphus mlanjensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 29 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
