About

Tropical Flat Snake (Siphlophis compressus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 232 places on iNaturalist.
