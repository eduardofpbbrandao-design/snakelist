About

Egg-eating Sea Snake (Emydocephalus annulatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, New Caledonia, Philippines, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 15 more. Recorded in 39 places on iNaturalist.
