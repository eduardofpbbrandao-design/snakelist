About

Diard's Blindsnake (Argyrophis diardii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Indonesia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 31 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
