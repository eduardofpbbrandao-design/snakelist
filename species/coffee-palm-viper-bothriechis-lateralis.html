About

Coffee Palm Viper (Bothriechis lateralis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 88 places on iNaturalist.
