About

Mock Cobra (Pseudoxenodon macrops) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 196 places on iNaturalist.
