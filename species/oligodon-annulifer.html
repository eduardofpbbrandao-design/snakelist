About

Ringed Kukri Snake (Oligodon annulifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
