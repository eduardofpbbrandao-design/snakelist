About

Colombian Earth Snake (brachycephalus) (Geophis brachycephalus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 43 places on iNaturalist.
