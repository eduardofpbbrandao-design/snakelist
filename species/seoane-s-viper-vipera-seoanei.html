About

Seoane's Viper (Vipera seoanei) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in France, Portugal, Spain. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 15 more. Common: recorded in 365 places on iNaturalist.
