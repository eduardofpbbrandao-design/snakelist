About

Bernier's Striped Snake (Dromicodryas bernieri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 78 places on iNaturalist.
