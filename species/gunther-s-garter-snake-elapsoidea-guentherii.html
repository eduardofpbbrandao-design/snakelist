About

Günther’s Garter Snake (Elapsoidea guentherii) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana, Malawi, Tanzania and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
