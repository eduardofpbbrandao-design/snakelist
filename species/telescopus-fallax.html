About

Cat Snake (Telescopus fallax) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Croatia and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 318 places on iNaturalist.
