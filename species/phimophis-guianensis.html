About

Troschel's Pampas Snake (Phimophis guianensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana, Panama and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
