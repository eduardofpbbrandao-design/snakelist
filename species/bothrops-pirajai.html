About

Piraja's Lancehead (Bothrops pirajai) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 19 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
