About

Earthworm Blind Snake (Typhlops lumbricalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Bahamas, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Haiti, Jamaica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
