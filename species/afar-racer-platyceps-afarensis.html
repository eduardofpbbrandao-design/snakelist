About

Afar racer (Platyceps afarensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Djibouti. It lives in habitats such as semi-desert, rocky hillsides, rocky outcrops, rock crevices. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
