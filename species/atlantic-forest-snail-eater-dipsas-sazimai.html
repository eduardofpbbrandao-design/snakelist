About

Atlantic Forest Snail-eater (Dipsas sazimai) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
