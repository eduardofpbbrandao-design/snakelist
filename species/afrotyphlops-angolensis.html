About

Angola blind snake (Afrotyphlops angolensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
