About

Baird's Patchnose Snake (Salvadora bairdi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 184 places on iNaturalist.
