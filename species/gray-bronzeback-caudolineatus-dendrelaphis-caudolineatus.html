About

Gray Bronzeback (caudolineatus) (Dendrelaphis caudolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 243 places on iNaturalist.
