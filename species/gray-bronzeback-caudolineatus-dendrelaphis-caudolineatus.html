Gray Bronzeback (caudolineatus)

Dendrelaphis caudolineatus

Gray Bronzeback (caudolineatus) (Dendrelaphis caudolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand.
