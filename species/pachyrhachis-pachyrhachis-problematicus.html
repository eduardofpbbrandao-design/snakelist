Pachyrhachis

Pachyrhachis problematicus

Pachyrhachis (Pachyrhachis problematicus) is a prehistoric snake of the family Simoliophiidae, known only from fossils. It died out in the Late Cretaceous (early Cenomanian), about 97 million years ago.
