About

Lataste's Viper (Vipera latastei) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Algeria, Morocco, Portugal, Spain, Tunisia. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, shrubland and 14 more. Common: recorded in 204 places on iNaturalist.
