About

Walnut Kukri Snake (Oligodon juglandifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 8 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
