About

Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) (Calliophis maculiceps) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. Recorded in 69 places on iNaturalist.
