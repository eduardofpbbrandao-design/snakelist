Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps)

Calliophis maculiceps

Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) (Calliophis maculiceps) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam. It is known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

Calliophis: ‘beautiful snake’, from the Greek roots calli- ‘beautiful’ + -ophis ‘snake’

maculiceps: ‘spotted head’, from Latin roots: maculi- ‘spotted’ + -ceps ‘head’

Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps): ‘Speckled’ – its speckles.
