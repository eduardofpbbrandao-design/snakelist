About

Red Black-headed Snake (Tantilla rubra) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
