About

Madrean Mountain Kingsnake (Lampropeltis knoblochi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest, thorn forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 105 places on iNaturalist.
