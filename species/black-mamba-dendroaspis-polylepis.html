About

Black Mamba (Dendroaspis polylepis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 20 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 55 more. Common: recorded in 272 places on iNaturalist.
