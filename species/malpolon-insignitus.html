About

Eastern Montpellier Snake (Malpolon insignitus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Croatia. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 10 more. Common: recorded in 487 places on iNaturalist.
