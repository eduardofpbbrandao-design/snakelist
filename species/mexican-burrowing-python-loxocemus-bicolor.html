Mexican Burrowing Python

Loxocemus bicolor

Mexican Burrowing Python (Loxocemus bicolor) is a non-venomous snake of the family Loxocemidae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and Nicaragua.
