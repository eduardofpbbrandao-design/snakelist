About

Slender Green Snake (Philothamnus heterolepidotus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic and 17 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
