About

Snouted Night Adder (Causus defilippii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya, Malawi and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.
