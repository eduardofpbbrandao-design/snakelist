About

Cat-eyed Snake (meridionalis) (Madagascarophis meridionalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 18 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
