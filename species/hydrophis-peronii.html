About

Spiny-headed Seasnake (Hydrophis peronii) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as coral reefs, seagrass beds, shallow coastal waters. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
