About

St. John's Keelback (Fowlea sanctijohannis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 14 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
