About

Eastern Sand Boa (Eryx vittatus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Tajikistan. It lives in habitats such as grassland, temperate grassland, steppe, sand dunes and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
