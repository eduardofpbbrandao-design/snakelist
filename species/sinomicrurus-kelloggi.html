About

Kellog's Coral Snake (Sinomicrurus kelloggi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in China, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.
