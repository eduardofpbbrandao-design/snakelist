About

Ground Snake (semiannulata) (Sonora semiannulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest and 52 more. Common: recorded in 382 places on iNaturalist.
