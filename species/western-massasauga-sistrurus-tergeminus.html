About

Western Massasauga (Sistrurus tergeminus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 568 places on iNaturalist.
