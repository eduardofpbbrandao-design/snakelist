Where the name comes from

Sistrurus: shares its root with the sistrum, an ancient Egyptian rattle, plus the Greek for tail - for the small rattle on the tail[1]

tergeminus: ‘threefold’

Western Massasauga: ‘Western’ – where it lives (in the west).

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
