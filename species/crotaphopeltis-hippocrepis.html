About

Ghana Herald Snake (Crotaphopeltis hippocrepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
