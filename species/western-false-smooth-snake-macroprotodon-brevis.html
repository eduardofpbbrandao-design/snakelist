Western False Smooth Snake

Macroprotodon brevis (Günther, 1862)

Western False Smooth Snake (Macroprotodon brevis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Gibraltar, Morocco, Portugal and Spain.
