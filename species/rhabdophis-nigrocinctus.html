About

Green Keelback (Rhabdophis nigrocinctus) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
