About

Redbelly Snake (Storeria occipitomaculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 5630 places on iNaturalist.
