About

Guinea Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops liberiensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
