About

Müller's Blind Snake (Argyrophis muelleri) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 30 places on iNaturalist.
