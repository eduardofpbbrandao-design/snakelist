About

Müller’s Sand Boa (Eryx muelleri) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad and 12 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 21 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
