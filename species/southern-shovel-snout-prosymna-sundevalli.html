About

Southern Shovel-snout (Prosymna sundevalli) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Botswana, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, dry woodland, dry savanna, shrubland and 16 more. Recorded in 61 places on iNaturalist.
