About

Banded Wolf Snake (Lycodon fasciatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Laos, Myanmar and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 81 places on iNaturalist.
