About

Great Plains Rat Snake (Pantherophis emoryi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 44 more. Common: recorded in 1466 places on iNaturalist.
