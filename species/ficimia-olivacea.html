About

Mexican Hook-nosed Snake (Ficimia olivacea) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
