Where the name comes from

Leptotyphlops: from the Greek leptos (thin) added to Typhlops (blind eye)[1]

Peter's Thread Snake: ‘Peter's’ – named after a person called Peter; ‘Thread Snake’ – it is as thin as a thread.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
