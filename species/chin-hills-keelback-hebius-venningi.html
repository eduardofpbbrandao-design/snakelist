About

Chin Hills Keelback (Hebius venningi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 19 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
