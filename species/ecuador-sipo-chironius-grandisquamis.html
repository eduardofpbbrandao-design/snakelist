About

Ecuador Sipo (Chironius grandisquamis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 107 places on iNaturalist.
