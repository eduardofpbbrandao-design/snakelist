About

Spotted Wolf Snake (Lycophidion albomaculatum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Gambia, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Senegal and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 11 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
