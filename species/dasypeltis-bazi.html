About

Egyptian Egg-eater (Dasypeltis bazi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Egypt. It lives in habitats such as desert, hot desert, abandoned buildings, sahara desert. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
