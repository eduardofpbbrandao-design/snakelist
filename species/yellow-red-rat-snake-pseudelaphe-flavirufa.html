Yellow-red Rat Snake

Pseudelaphe flavirufa

Yellow-red Rat Snake (Pseudelaphe flavirufa) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua.
