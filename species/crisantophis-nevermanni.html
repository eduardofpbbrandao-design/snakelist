About

Dunn's Road Guarder (Crisantophis nevermanni) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, central american dry forest. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
