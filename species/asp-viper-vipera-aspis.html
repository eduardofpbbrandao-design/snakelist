About

Asp Viper (Vipera aspis) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Andorra, France, Germany, Italy, San Marino and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 32 more. Common: recorded in 1557 places on iNaturalist.
