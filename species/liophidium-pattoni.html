About

Red-striped Smooth Snake (Liophidium pattoni) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
