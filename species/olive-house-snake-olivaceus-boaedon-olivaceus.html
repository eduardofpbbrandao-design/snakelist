Olive House Snake (olivaceus)

Boaedon olivaceus

Olive House Snake (olivaceus) (Boaedon olivaceus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 12 more.
