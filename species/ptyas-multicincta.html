About

Many-banded Green Snake (Ptyas multicincta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 78 places on iNaturalist.
