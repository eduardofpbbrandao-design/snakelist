About

Collared Reed Snake (leucogaster) (Calamaria leucogaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
