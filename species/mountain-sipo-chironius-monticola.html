About

Mountain Sipo (Chironius monticola) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 143 places on iNaturalist.
