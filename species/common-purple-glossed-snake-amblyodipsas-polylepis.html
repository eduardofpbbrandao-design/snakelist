About

Common Purple-glossed Snake (Amblyodipsas polylepis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Kenya and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 97 places on iNaturalist.
