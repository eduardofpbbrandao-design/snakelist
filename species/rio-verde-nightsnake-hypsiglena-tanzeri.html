About

Rio Verde Nightsnake (Hypsiglena tanzeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 20 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
