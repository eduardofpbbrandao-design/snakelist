About

Black-headed seasnake (Hydrophis atriceps) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
