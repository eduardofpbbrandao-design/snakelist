About

Mississippi Green Water Snake (Nerodia cyclopion) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 289 places on iNaturalist.
