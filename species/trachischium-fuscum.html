About

Blackbelly Worm-eating Snake (Trachischium fuscum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
