Where the name comes from

Hydrophis: ‘water snake’, from the Greek roots hydr- ‘water’ + -ophis ‘snake’

Arabian Gulf Sea Snake: ‘Arabian’ – after Arabia; ‘Sea Snake’ – it lives in the sea.
