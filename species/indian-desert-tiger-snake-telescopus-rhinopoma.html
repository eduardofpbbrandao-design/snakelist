Indian Desert Tiger Snake

Telescopus rhinopoma

Indian Desert Tiger Snake (Telescopus rhinopoma) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan, Turkmenistan.
