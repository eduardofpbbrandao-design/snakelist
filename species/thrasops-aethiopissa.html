About

Thrasops aethiopissa is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
