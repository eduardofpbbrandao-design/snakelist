About

Double-banded Coral Snake Mimic (Erythrolamprus bizona) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 176 places on iNaturalist.
