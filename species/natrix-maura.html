About

Viperine Snake (Natrix maura) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in France, Gibraltar, Italy, Portugal, Spain and 1 more. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Mallorca and Menorca), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 2475 places on iNaturalist.
