Green Bush Snake

Philothamnus nitidus

Green Bush Snake (Philothamnus nitidus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 7 more.
