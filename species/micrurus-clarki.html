About

Clark's Coral Snake (Micrurus clarki) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Panama. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
