About

Copper Parrot Snake (Leptophis cupreus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
