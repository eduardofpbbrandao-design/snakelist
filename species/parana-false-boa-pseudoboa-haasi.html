About

Paraná False Boa (Pseudoboa haasi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
