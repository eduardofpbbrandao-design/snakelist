About

Blackwater Mud Snake (Phytolopsis punctata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
