About

Common Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis schokari) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
