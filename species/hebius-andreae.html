About

Andrea's Keelback (Hebius andreae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, riverine forest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
