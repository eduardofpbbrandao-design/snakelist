About

Chucantí Centipede Snake (Tantilla berguidoi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Panama. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
