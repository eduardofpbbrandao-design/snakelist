About

Cerro Petlalcala Montane Pitviper (Cerrophidion petlalcalensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
