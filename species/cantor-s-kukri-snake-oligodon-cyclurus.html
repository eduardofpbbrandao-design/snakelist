About

Cantor's Kukri Snake (Oligodon cyclurus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, India, Laos and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
