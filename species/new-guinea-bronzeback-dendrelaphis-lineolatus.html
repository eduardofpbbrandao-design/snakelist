About

New Guinea Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis lineolatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guinea, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
