About

Common Mock Viper (Psammodynastes pulverulentus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudaspididae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 44 more. Common: recorded in 757 places on iNaturalist.
