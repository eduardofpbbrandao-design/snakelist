About

Bocage’s Brown House Snake (Boaedon bocagei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
