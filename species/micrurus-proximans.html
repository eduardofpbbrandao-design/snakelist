About

Nayarit Coral Snake (Micrurus proximans) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, central american dry forest. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
