About

Plain Seasnake (Hydrophis inornatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, China, Indonesia, Philippines, Sri Lanka and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 5 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
