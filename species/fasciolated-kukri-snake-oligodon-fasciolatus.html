Fasciolated Kukri Snake

Oligodon fasciolatus

Fasciolated Kukri Snake (Oligodon fasciolatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar and 2 more.
