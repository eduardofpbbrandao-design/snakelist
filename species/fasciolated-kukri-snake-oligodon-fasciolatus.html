About

Fasciolated Kukri Snake (Oligodon fasciolatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 259 places on iNaturalist.
