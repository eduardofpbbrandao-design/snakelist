About

Spotted Whip Snake (Hemorrhois ravergieri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, China, Georgia and 13 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.
