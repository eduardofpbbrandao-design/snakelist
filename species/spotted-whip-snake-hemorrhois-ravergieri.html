About

Spotted Whip Snake (Hemorrhois ravergieri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, China, Georgia and 13 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, woodland, open woodland, montane woodland and 29 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.
