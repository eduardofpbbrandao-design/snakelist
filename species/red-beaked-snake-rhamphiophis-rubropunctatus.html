Red Beaked Snake

Rhamphiophis rubropunctatus

Red Beaked Snake (Rhamphiophis rubropunctatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia, South Sudan, Tanzania and Uganda.

Where the name comes from

rubropunctatus: ‘red-dotted’, from Latin roots: rubro- ‘red’ + -punctatus ‘dotted’

Red Beaked Snake: ‘Red’ – its colour; ‘Beaked’ – its beak-like snout.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
