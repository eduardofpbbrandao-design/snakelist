About

False Smooth Snake (mauritanicus) (Macroprotodon mauritanicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Tunisia. It has also been introduced by people outside its native range (Mallorca and Menorca). Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
