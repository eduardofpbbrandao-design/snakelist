False Smooth Snake (mauritanicus)

Macroprotodon mauritanicus

False Smooth Snake (mauritanicus) (Macroprotodon mauritanicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria and Tunisia. People introduced it to Mallorca and Menorca in antiquity.
