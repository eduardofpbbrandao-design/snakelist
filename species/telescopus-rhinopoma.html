About

Indian Desert Tiger Snake (Telescopus rhinopoma) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan, Turkmenistan. It lives in habitats such as montane woodland, mountain forest, grassland, temperate grassland and 20 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
