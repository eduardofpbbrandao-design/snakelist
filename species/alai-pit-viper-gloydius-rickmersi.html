About

Alai Pit Viper (Gloydius rickmersi) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Kyrgyzstan. It lives in habitats such as grassland, temperate grassland, steppe, central asian steppe. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
