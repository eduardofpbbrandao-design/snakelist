About

Chihuahuan Nightsnake (Hypsiglena jani) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, temperate coniferous forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 588 places on iNaturalist.
