About

Southern Bahamas Boa (Chilabothrus chrysogaster) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Bahamas, Turks and Caicos Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, caves, abandoned buildings, caribbean dry forest. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
