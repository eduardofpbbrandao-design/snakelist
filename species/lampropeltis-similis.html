Extinct · fossil snake · late Miocene

Lampropeltis similis

Lampropeltis similis is a prehistoric snake of the family Colubridae, known only from fossils. It died out in the late Miocene, about 7 million years ago. It was known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

Lampropeltis: from the Greek lampros (shiny) and peltē (small shield) - for the smooth, glossy scales[1]

similis: ‘similar’
