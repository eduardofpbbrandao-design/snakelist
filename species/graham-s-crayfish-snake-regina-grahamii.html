Graham's Crayfish Snake

Regina grahamii Baird & Girard, 1853

Graham's Crayfish Snake is a harmless, semi-aquatic snake of the central United States that eats mainly crayfish.[1]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Edges of muddy marshes, oxbow lakes, rivers, streams and roadside ditches full of crayfish; it hides under debris and in crayfish burrows.[1]

Diet

Mostly crayfish, especially ones that have just moulted; also fish and amphibians.[1]

Reproduction

Gives birth to 10 to 15 live young, each about 20 cm long.[1]
