About

Graham's Crayfish Snake (Regina grahamii) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 368 places on iNaturalist.
