About

Philippine Dryophiops (Dryophiops philippina) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
