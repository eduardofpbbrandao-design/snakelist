About

Common sea krait (Laticauda laticaudata) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Japan, Myanmar and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 143 places on iNaturalist.
