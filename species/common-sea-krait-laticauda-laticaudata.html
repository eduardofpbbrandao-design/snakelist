Where the name comes from

laticaudata: ‘broad-tailed’, from Latin roots: lati- ‘broad’ + -caudata ‘tailed’

Common Sea Krait: ‘Common’ – it is the commonest or best-known of its kind; ‘Sea Krait’ – a krait-like snake that lives in the sea.
