About

Mojave Rattlesnake (Crotalus scutulatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 46 more. Common: recorded in 1068 places on iNaturalist.
