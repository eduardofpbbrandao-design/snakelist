About

Haas's Bronzeback Snake (Dendrelaphis haasi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 35 places on iNaturalist.
