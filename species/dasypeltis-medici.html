About

East African Egg Eater (Dasypeltis medici) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia, South Africa and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 11 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
