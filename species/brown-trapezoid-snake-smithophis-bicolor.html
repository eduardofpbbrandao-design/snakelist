About

Brown Trapezoid Snake (Smithophis bicolor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
