About

Pakaraima Snail-eater (Dipsas pakaraima) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guyana. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 4 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
