Herrmann's Water Snake

Where the name comes from

bicinctus: ‘two-banded’, from Latin roots: bi- ‘two’ + -cinctus ‘banded’

Herrmann's Water Snake: ‘Herrmann's’ – named after a person called Herrmann; ‘Water Snake’ – it lives in or near water.
