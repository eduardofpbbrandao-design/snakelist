About

Herrmann's Water Snake (Hydrodynastes bicinctus) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Suriname, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 38 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
