Herrmann's Water Snake

Hydrodynastes bicinctus

Herrmann's Water Snake (Hydrodynastes bicinctus) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Suriname, Venezuela.
