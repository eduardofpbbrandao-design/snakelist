About

Guo's Green Pit Viper (Trimeresurus guoi) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 22 more. Recorded in 46 places on iNaturalist.
