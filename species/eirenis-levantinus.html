About

Levantine Dwarf Snake (Eirenis levantinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cyprus, Israel, Lebanon, Syria, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, shrubland and 13 more. Recorded in 19 places on iNaturalist.
