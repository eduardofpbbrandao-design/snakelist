About

Bella Rat Snake (Archelaphe bella) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Myanmar, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
