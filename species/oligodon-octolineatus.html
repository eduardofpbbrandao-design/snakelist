About

Eight-striped Kukri Snake (Oligodon octolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brunei, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 87 places on iNaturalist.
