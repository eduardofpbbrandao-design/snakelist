Where the name comes from

Dendroaspis: from the Greek dendron (tree) and aspis (asp, an old word for a cobra or snake) - "tree asp", as most mambas live in trees[1]

jamesoni: named after a person called Jameson

Jameson's Mamba: ‘Jameson's’ – named after a person called Jameson; ‘Mamba’ – from imamba, its name in the Zulu language.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
