About

Blood Python (Python brongersmai) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Recorded in 44 places on iNaturalist.
