About

Tiger Cat Snake (Telescopus semiannulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 88 places on iNaturalist.
