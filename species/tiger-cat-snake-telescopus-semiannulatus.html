Tiger Cat Snake

Telescopus semiannulatus

Tiger Cat Snake (Telescopus semiannulatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini and 9 more.
