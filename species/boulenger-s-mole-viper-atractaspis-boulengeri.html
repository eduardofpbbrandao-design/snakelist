About

Boulenger's Mole Viper (Atractaspis boulengeri) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
