About

Madrean Narrowheaded Garter Snake (Thamnophis unilabialis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest, thorn forest and 25 more. Recorded in 19 places on iNaturalist.
