About

Lema's Ground Snake (Lygophis dilepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 25 more. Recorded in 50 places on iNaturalist.
