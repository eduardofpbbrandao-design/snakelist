About

Russell's Viper (Daboia russelii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Indonesia, Myanmar and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 455 places on iNaturalist.
