About

Western Dusky Rattlesnake (Crotalus triseriatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 114 places on iNaturalist.
