About

Twin-spotted Rattlesnake (Crotalus pricei) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 101 places on iNaturalist.
