About

Vietnamese Large-toothed Snake (Lycodon meridionalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as forest edges, abandoned buildings, roadsides. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
