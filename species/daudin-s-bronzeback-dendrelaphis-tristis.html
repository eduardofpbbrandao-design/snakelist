About

Daudin's Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis tristis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 393 places on iNaturalist.
