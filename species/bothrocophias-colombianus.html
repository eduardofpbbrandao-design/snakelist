About

Colombian Lancehead (Bothrocophias colombianus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, chocó rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
