About

Collared Dwarf Snake (Eirenis collaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bulgaria, Georgia, Iran and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 28 more. Recorded in 48 places on iNaturalist.
