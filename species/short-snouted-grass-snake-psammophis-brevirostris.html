About

Short-snouted Grass Snake (Psammophis brevirostris) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Namibia, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 199 places on iNaturalist.
