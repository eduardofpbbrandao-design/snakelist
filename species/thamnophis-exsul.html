About

Montane Garter Snake (Thamnophis exsul) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as scrub forest, thorn forest, forest edges, scrubland and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
