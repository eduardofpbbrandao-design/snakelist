About

Shropshire's Puffing Snake (Phrynonax shropshirei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Common: recorded in 82 places on iNaturalist.
