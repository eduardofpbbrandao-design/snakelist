Shropshire's Puffing Snake

Phrynonax shropshirei

Shropshire's Puffing Snake (Phrynonax shropshirei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama, Peru, Venezuela.
