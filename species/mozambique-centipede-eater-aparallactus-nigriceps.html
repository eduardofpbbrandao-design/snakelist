About

Mozambique Centipede Eater (Aparallactus nigriceps) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Mozambique. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter and 4 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
