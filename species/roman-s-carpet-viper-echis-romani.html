About

Roman's Carpet Viper (Echis romani) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Niger, Nigeria. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
