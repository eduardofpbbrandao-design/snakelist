About

Common File Snake (Limaformosa capensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 15 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Recorded in 47 places on iNaturalist.
