About

Diadem Snake (Spalerosophis diadema) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Gaza Strip, India, Iran, Iraq and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, evergreen forest and 22 more. Recorded in 73 places on iNaturalist.
