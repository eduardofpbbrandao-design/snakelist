About

Anderson’s Slug Snake (Pareas andersonii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
