About

Taiwan Stink Snake (Elaphe carinata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as fallen logs, reservoirs, irrigation canals, rice paddies and 8 more. Common: recorded in 375 places on iNaturalist.
