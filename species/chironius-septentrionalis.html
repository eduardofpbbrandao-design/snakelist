About

South American Sipo (septentrionalis) (Chironius septentrionalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
