About

Middle American Burrowing Snake (quadrivirgatum) (Adelphicos quadrivirgatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 54 places on iNaturalist.
