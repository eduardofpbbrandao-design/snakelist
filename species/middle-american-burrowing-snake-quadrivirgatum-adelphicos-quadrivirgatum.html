Middle American Burrowing Snake (quadrivirgatum)

Adelphicos quadrivirgatum

Middle American Burrowing Snake (quadrivirgatum) (Adelphicos quadrivirgatum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua.
