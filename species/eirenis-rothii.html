About

Roth's Dwarf Snake (Eirenis rothii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 13 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
