About

Zweifel's Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus zweifeli) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
