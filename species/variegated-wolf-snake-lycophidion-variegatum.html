About

Variegated Wolf Snake (Lycophidion variegatum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, South Africa, Zambia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 5 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
