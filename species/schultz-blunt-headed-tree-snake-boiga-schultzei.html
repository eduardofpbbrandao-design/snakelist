About

Schultz' Blunt-headed Tree Snake (Boiga schultzei) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, secondary forest and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
