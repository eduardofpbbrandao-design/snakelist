Twig Snake

Thelotornis capensis Smith, 1849

The Savanna Vine Snake, or Twig Snake, is a very thin, long-tailed tree snake of southern Africa. It is dangerously venomous even though its fangs are at the back of the mouth.[1]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Forest, savanna and shrubland.[1]

Behaviour

Active by day and spends much of its time climbing in bushes and trees.[1]

Bites: Its venom is delivered by large grooved teeth at the back of the upper jaw. The German snake scientist Robert Mertens died after being bitten by one.[1]

Reproduction

Lays 4 to 13 small, long eggs, each about 36 mm long and 16 mm wide.[1]

Did you know?

  • Its thin body and twig-like colours let it vanish among branches.[1]
