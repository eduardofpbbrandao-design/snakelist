Banded Racer (fasciolata)

Argyrogena fasciolata

Banded Racer (fasciolata) (Argyrogena fasciolata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka.
