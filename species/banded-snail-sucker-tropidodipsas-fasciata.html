About

Banded Snail Sucker (Tropidodipsas fasciata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
