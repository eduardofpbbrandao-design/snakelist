About

Cogger's sea snake (Hydrophis coggeri) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Fiji, New Caledonia. It lives in habitats such as coral reefs, shallow coastal waters, new caledonia forest. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
