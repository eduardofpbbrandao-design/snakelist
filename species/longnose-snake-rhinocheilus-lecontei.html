About

Longnose Snake (Rhinocheilus lecontei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 1557 places on iNaturalist.
