About

Largenose Earth Snake (Conopsis nasus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 233 places on iNaturalist.
