Hoffstetter's Worm Snake

Madatyphlops cariei

Hoffstetter's Worm Snake (Madatyphlops cariei) was a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae that lived in Mauritius. Extinct since the 1600s: known only from bones on Mauritius.
