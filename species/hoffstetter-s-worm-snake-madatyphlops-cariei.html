About

Hoffstetter's Worm Snake (Madatyphlops cariei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Mauritius. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
