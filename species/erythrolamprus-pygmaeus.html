About

Amazon Tropical Forest Snake (Erythrolamprus pygmaeus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Recorded in 22 places on iNaturalist.
