About

Venezuela Lancehead (Bothrops venezuelensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 6 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
