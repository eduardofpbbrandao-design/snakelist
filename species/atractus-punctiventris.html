About

Pointed Ground Snake (Atractus punctiventris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
