Red-headed Forest Racer

Dendrophidion rufiterminorum

Red-headed Forest Racer (Dendrophidion rufiterminorum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua.
