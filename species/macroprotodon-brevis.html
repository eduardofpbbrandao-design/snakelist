About

Western False Smooth Snake (Macroprotodon brevis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Gibraltar, Morocco. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, montane woodland, mountain forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 357 places on iNaturalist.
