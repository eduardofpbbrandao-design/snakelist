About

Western Forest Centipede-eater (Aparallactus modestus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 11 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
