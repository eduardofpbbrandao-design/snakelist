Where the name comes from

modestus: ‘modest, plain’

Western Forest Centipede-eater: ‘Western’ – where it lives (in the west); ‘Forest’ – it lives in forests; ‘Centipede-eater’ – it eats centipedes.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
