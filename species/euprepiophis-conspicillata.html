About

Japanese Woodsnake (Euprepiophis conspicillata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Japan, Russia, Southern Kuril Islands. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, bamboo forest and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
