About

Argentine Pampas Snake (Phimophis guerini) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 24 more. Recorded in 26 places on iNaturalist.
