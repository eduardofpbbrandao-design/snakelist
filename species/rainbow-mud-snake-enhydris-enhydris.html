About

Rainbow Mud Snake (Enhydris enhydris) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 199 places on iNaturalist.
