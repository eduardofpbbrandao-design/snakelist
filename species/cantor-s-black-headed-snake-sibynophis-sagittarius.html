Where the name comes from

Sibynophis: ‘spear snake’, from the Greek roots sibyn- ‘spear’ + -ophis ‘snake’

sagittarius: ‘archer’

Cantor’s Black-headed Snake: ‘Cantor’s’ – named after Theodore Cantor, a Danish doctor and zoologist in India; ‘Black-headed’ – for its black head.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
