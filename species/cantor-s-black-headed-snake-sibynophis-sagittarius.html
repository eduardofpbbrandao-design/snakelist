About

Cantor’s black-headed snake (Sibynophis sagittarius) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 21 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
