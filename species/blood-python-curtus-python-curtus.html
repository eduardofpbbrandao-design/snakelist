About

Blood Python (curtus) (Python curtus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
