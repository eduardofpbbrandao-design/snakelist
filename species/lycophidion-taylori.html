About

Taylor's Wolf Snake (Lycophidion taylori) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Central African Republic, Chad, Ethiopia, Senegal, Somalia and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
