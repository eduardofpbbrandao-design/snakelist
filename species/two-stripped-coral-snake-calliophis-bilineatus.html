About

Two-stripped Coral Snake (Calliophis bilineatus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
