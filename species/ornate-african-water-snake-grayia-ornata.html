About

Ornate African Water Snake (Grayia ornata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
