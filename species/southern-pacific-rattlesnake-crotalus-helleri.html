About

Southern Pacific Rattlesnake (Crotalus helleri) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, forest edges, shrubland, scrubland and 10 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
