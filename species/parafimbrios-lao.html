About

Ziggy Stardust Snake (Parafimbrios lao) is a non-venomous snake of the family Xenodermidae, found in China, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
