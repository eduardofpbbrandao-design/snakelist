About

Indian Cobra (Naja naja) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Nepal and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 594 places on iNaturalist.
