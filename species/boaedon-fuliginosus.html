About

Brown House Snake (fuliginosus) (Boaedon fuliginosus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 30 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 99 places on iNaturalist.
