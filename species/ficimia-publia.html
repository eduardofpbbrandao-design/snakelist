About

Blotched Hooknose Snake (Ficimia publia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Recorded in 73 places on iNaturalist.
