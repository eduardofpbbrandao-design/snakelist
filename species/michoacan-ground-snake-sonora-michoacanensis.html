About

Michoacán Ground Snake (Sonora michoacanensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 11 more. Recorded in 18 places on iNaturalist.
