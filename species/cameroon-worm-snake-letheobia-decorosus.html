About

Cameroon Worm Snake (Letheobia decorosus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
