About

Gerard's water snake (Gerarda prevostiana) is a non-venomous snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, India, Myanmar, Philippines, Sri Lanka and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 28 more. Recorded in 33 places on iNaturalist.
