About

Cameroon Snake-Eater (Polemon notatus) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
