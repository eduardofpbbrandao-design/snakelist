About

Ornate Green Snake (Philothamnus ornatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Botswana, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
