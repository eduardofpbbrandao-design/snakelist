Where the name comes from

Leptophis: ‘slender snake’, from the Greek roots lept- ‘slender’ + -ophis ‘snake’

Cope's Parrot Snake: ‘Cope's’ – named after Edward Drinker Cope, an American palaeontologist and herpetologist; ‘Parrot Snake’ – it is bright green, like a parrot.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
