About

Olive House Snake (inornatus) (Lycodonomorphus inornatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eswatini, South Africa, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as wetlands, marshes, freshwater marshes, swamps and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
