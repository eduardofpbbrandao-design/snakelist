Where the name comes from

forbesi: named after a person called Forbes

Forbes' Graceful Brown Snake: ‘Forbes'’ – named after a person called Forbes; ‘Graceful’ – its slim shape; ‘Brown’ – its colour.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
