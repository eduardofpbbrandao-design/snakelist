About

Horseshoe Whip Snake (Hemorrhois hippocrepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Gibraltar, Italy, Portugal, Spain. It is also invasive outside its native range (Ibiza, Formentera and Mallorca). It lives in habitats such as pine forest, woodland, open woodland, shrubland and 17 more. Common: recorded in 913 places on iNaturalist.
