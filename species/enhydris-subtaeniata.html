About

Mekong Mud Snake (Enhydris subtaeniata) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest, wetlands, marshes and 11 more. Recorded in 19 places on iNaturalist.
