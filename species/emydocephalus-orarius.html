About

Western Turtle-headed Sea Snake (Emydocephalus orarius) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, eucalyptus forest, woodland, dry woodland and 20 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
