About

Joger's Saw-scaled Viper (Echis jogeri) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Guinea, Mali, Senegal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
