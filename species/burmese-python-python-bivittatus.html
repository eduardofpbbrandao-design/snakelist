About

Burmese Python (Python bivittatus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 6 more. It has also been introduced by people outside its native range (Florida Everglades). Common: recorded in 435 places on iNaturalist.
