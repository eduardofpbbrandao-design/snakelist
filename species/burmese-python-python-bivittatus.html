About

Burmese Python (Python bivittatus) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 6 more. It is also invasive outside its native range (Florida Everglades). It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 38 more. Common: recorded in 435 places on iNaturalist.
