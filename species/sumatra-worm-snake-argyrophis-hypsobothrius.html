About

Sumatra Worm Snake (Argyrophis hypsobothrius) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
