About

Black-lined Green Snake (Hapsidophrys lineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
