About

Massasauga (Sistrurus catenatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 317 places on iNaturalist.
