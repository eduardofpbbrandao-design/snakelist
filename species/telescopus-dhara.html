About

Arabian Cat Snake (Telescopus dhara) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Israel, Oman, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Yemen. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 15 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
