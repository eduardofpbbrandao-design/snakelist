About

Cuming's Blind Snake (Ramphotyphlops cumingii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 12 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
