About

Baron's Green Racer (Philodryas baroni) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland, dry woodland and 8 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
