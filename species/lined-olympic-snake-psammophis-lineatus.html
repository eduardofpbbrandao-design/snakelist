Lined Olympic Snake

Psammophis lineatus

Lined Olympic Snake (Psammophis lineatus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 24 more.

Where the name comes from

Psammophis: ‘sand snake’, from the Greek roots psamm- ‘sand’ + -ophis ‘snake’

lineatus: ‘lined’

Lined Olympic Snake: ‘Lined’ – its lines.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
