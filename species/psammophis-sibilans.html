About

Striped Sand Snake (Psammophis sibilans) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Algeria, Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 28 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 45 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
