About

Moellendorff's Trinket Snake (Elaphe moellendorffi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 59 places on iNaturalist.
