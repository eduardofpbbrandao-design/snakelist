Where the name comes from

Hydrophis: ‘water snake’, from the Greek roots hydr- ‘water’ + -ophis ‘snake’

schistosus: ‘slate-grey’

Common Seasnake: ‘Common’ – it is the commonest or best-known of its kind; ‘Seasnake’ – it lives in the sea.
