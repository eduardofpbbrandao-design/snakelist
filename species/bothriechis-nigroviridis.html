About

Black-speckled Palm Pit Viper (Bothriechis nigroviridis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Costa Rica, Guatemala, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
