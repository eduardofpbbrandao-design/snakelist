About

Common Kingsnake (Lampropeltis getula) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 1370 places on iNaturalist.
