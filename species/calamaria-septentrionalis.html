About

Hong Kong Dwarf Snake (Calamaria septentrionalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 8 more. Common: recorded in 101 places on iNaturalist.
