About

Lichtenstein's Green Racer (Philodryas olfersii) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Guyana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 438 places on iNaturalist.
