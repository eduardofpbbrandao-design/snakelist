About

Everett's Kukri Snake (Oligodon everetti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
