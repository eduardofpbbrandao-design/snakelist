About

Radiated Ratsnake (Coelognathus radiatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 51 more. Common: recorded in 425 places on iNaturalist.
