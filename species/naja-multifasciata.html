About

Many-banded Snake (Naja multifasciata) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
