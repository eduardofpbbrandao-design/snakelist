About

Black-headed Cat Snake (Boiga nigriceps) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 112 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

Native range

  • Indonesia: Java, Kalimantan (Borneo), Sumatra
  • Malaysia: Johor, Kedah, Kelantan, Pahang, Sabah, Sarawak, Selangor
  • Thailand: Nakhon Si Thammarat, Narathiwat, Phatthalung, Songkhla, Surat Thani, Yala
