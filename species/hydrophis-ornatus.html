About

Ornate Sea Snake (Hydrophis ornatus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, India, Indonesia, Iran, Japan and 11 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 26 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
