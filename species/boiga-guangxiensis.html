About

Guangxi Cat Snake (Boiga guangxiensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 18 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
