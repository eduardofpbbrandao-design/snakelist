About

Black-headed Smooth Snake (Meizodon plumbiceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, scrub forest, thorn forest, semi-desert and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
