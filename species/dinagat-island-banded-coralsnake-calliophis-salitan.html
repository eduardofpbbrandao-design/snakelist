About

Dinagat Island Banded Coralsnake (Calliophis salitan) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
