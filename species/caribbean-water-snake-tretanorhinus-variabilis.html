About

Caribbean Water Snake (Tretanorhinus variabilis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cuba. It lives in habitats such as wetlands, marshes, freshwater marshes, swamps and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
