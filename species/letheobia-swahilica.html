About

Swahili Gracile Blind-snake (Letheobia swahilica) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Kenya, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
