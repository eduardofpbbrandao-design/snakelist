About

Ambre Brook Snake (Pseudoxyrhopus ambreensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, coastal forest, leaf litter, fallen logs and 6 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
