About

Pygmy Copperhead (Austrelaps labialis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as eucalyptus forest, woodland, dry woodland, shrubland and 8 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
