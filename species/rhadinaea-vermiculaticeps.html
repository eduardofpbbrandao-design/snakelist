About

Vermiculate Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinaea vermiculaticeps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Panama. It lives in habitats such as leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
