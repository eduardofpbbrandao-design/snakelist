About

Striped Vine Snake (Oxybelis vittatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 92 places on iNaturalist.
