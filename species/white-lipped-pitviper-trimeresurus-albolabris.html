White-lipped Pitviper

Trimeresurus albolabris Gray, 1842

The White-lipped Pit Viper is a green, venomous tree viper of South-east Asia, from Nepal to southern China and Indonesia.[1]

Who described it

First described by Gray in 1842.

Where the name comes from

albolabris: ‘white-lipped’, from Latin roots: albo- ‘white’ + -labris ‘lipped’

White-lipped Pitviper: ‘White-lipped’ – for its white lips; ‘Pitviper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
