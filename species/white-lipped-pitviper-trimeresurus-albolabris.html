About

White-lipped Pitviper (Trimeresurus albolabris) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 396 places on iNaturalist.
