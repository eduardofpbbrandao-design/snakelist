About

Braided Shadow Snake (Synophis plectovertebralis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, chocó rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
