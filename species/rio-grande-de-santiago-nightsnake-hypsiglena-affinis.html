About

Rio Grande de Santiago Nightsnake (Hypsiglena affinis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, montane woodland and 19 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
