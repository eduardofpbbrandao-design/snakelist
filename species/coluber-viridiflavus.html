About

Dark green snake (Coluber viridiflavus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in France, Italy, Spain. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 14 more. Common: recorded in 2751 places on iNaturalist.
