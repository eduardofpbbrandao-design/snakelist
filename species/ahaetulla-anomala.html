About

Brown Vine Snake (anomala) (Ahaetulla anomala) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 5 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
