About

North Philippine Temple Pitviper (Tropidolaemus subannulatus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Brunei, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Common: recorded in 336 places on iNaturalist.
