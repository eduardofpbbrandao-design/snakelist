About

Common Rough-sided Snake (Aspidura trachyprocta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, sri lanka rainforest. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
