About

Cape Thread Snake (conjunctus) (Leptotyphlops conjunctus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Eswatini, Lesotho, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest, shrubland and 12 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
