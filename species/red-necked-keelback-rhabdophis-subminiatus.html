About

Red-necked Keelback (Rhabdophis subminiatus) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 31 more. Recorded in 40 places on iNaturalist.
