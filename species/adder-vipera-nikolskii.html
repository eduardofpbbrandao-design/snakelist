About

Adder (Vipera nikolskii) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Moldova, Romania, Russia, Ukraine. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, mountain forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 112 places on iNaturalist.
