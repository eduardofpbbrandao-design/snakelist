About

Yellow-and-black Tree Snake (Lycodryas citrinus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Madagascar. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, madagascar dry deciduous forest, madagascar mangrove forest, madagascar limestone karst forest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
