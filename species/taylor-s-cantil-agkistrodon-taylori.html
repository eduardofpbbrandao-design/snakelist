About

Taylor's Cantil (Agkistrodon taylori) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
