About

Dorr's Racer (Bamanophis dorri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Ghana, Guinea, Mali and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
