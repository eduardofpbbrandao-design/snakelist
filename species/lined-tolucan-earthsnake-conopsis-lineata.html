About

Lined Tolucan Earthsnake (Conopsis lineata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 226 places on iNaturalist.
