About

Günther's Green Liophis (Erythrolamprus albertguentheri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, subtropical dry forest and 20 more. Recorded in 10 places on iNaturalist.
