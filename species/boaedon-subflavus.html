About

Yellow House Snake (Boaedon subflavus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, South Sudan. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
