About

Central Burrowing Snake (Apostolepis flavotorquata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 26 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
