About

Indo-Chinese Sand Snake (Psammophis indochinensis) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 15 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
