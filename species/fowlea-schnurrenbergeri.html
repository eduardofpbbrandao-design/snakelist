About

Bar-necked Keelback (Fowlea schnurrenbergeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
