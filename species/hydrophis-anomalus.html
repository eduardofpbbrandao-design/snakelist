About

Anomalous Sea Snake (Hydrophis anomalus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
