About

Haitian Ground Snake (Ialtris haetianus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Dominican Republic, Haiti. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest and 1 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
