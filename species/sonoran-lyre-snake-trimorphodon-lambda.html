About

Sonoran Lyre Snake (Trimorphodon lambda) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 284 places on iNaturalist.
