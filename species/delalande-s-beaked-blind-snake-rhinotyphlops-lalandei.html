Where the name comes from

lalandei: named after a person called Lalande

Delalande's Beaked Blind Snake: ‘Delalande's’ – named after a person called Delalande; ‘Beaked’ – its beak-like snout; ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
