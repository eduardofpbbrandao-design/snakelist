About

Delalande's Beaked Blind Snake (Rhinotyphlops lalandei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Mozambique, South Africa, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as subtropical dry forest, coastal forest, woodland, dry woodland and 18 more. Common: recorded in 187 places on iNaturalist.
