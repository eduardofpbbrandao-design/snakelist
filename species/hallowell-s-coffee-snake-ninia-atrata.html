About

Hallowell's Coffee Snake (Ninia atrata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Panama, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, mountain forest and 5 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.
