About

Black Milksnake (Lampropeltis micropholis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Mexico, Panama and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 22 more. Common: recorded in 216 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Costa Rica: Alajuela, Cartago, Guanacaste, Limón, Puntarenas, San José
  • Ecuador: Esmeraldas, Guayas, Imbabura, Loja, Los Ríos, Manabí, Pichincha, Santo Domingo de los Tsáchilas
  • Mexico
  • Panama: Chiriquí, Coclé, Panamá, comarca Ngäbe-Buglé
  • Venezuela: Tachira
