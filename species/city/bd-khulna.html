Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 5

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bamboo Pitviper Trimeresurus erythrurus, Black-banded Sea Snake Hydrophis nigrocinctus, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Russell's Viper Daboia russelii.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
