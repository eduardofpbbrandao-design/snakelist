Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 13Non-venomous: 27

11 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Indonesian Pit Viper Trimeresurus hageni, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Ornate Sea Snake Hydrophis ornatus, Red-headed Krait Bungarus flaviceps, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Shore Pit Viper Trimeresurus purpureomaculatus, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis, Yellow-lipped Sea Krait Laticauda colubrina.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
