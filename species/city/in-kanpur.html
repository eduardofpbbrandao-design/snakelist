All about Uttar Pradesh

These are the snakes of Uttar Pradesh, not of Kanpur itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 23 species recorded anywhere in Uttar Pradesh, the state or province Kanpur is in. Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 15

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Indian Cobra Naja naja, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Wall's Sind Krait Bungarus walli.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
