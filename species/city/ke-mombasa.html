All about Coast

These are the snakes of Coast, not of Mombasa itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 48 species recorded anywhere in Coast, the state or province Mombasa is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 20Non-venomous: 28

14 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Cobra Naja pallida, African Rock Python Python sebae, Ashe's Spitting Cobra Naja ashei, Black Garter Snake Elapsoidea nigra, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Brown Forest Cobra Naja subfulva, Eastern Vine Snake Thelotornis mossambicanus, Green Mamba Dendroaspis angusticeps, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Peters' Burrowing Asp Atractaspis fallax, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Snouted Night Adder Causus defilippii, Usambara Vine Snake Thelotornis usambaricus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
