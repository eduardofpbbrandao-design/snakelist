All about Orientale

These are the snakes of Orientale, not of Kisangani itself. Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7Non-venomous: 10

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Bush Viper Atheris squamigera, African Rock Python Python sebae, Gaboon Viper Bitis gabonica, Jameson's Mamba Dendroaspis jamesoni, Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) Bitis nasicornis, Ringed Water Cobra Naja annulata.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
