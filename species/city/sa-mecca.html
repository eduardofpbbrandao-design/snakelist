Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 4Non-venomous: 0

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Arabian Cobra Naja arabica, Arabian Small-Scaled Burrowing Asp Atractaspis andersonii, Palestine Saw-scaled Viper Echis coloratus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
