Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 4Non-venomous: 7

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brown Spotted Pitviper Protobothrops mucrosquamatus, Chinese Cobra Naja atra, Guangxi Coral Snake Sinomicrurus peinani.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
