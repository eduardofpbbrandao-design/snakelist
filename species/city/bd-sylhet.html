Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 18

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bamboo Pitviper Trimeresurus erythrurus, Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Bindee keelback Rhabdophis bindi, Burmese Python Python bivittatus, Greater Black Krait Bungarus niger, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Pope's Bamboo Pit Viper Trimeresurus popeiorum.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
