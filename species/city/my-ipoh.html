Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 23

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Clouded Pit Viper Trimeresurus nebularis, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Monocled Cobra Naja kaouthia, Red-headed Krait Bungarus flaviceps, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Shore Pit Viper Trimeresurus purpureomaculatus, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
