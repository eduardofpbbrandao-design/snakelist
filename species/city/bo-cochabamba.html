Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 10

6 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Aquatic Coral Snake Micrurus surinamensis, Bolivian Lancehead Bothrops sanctaecrucis, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Jonathan's Lancehead Bothrops jonathani, Mato Grosso Lancehead Bothrops mattogrossensis, Bothrops monsignifer.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
