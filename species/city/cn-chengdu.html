All about Sichuan

These are the snakes of Sichuan, not of Chengdu itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 47 species recorded anywhere in Sichuan, the state or province Chengdu is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 15Non-venomous: 32

14 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brown Spotted Pitviper Protobothrops mucrosquamatus, Chinese Green Tree Viper Trimeresurus stejnegeri, Chiwen Keelback Rhabdophis chiwen, Fea Viper Azemiops feae, Glacier pit viper Gloydius swild, Hubei Keelback Rhabdophis nuchalis, Jiuzhai pitviper Gloydius lateralis, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Mount Gongga Keelback Rhabdophis pentasupralabialis, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Strauch's Pitviper Gloydius strauchi, Szechwan Pit Viper Protobothrops xiangchengensis, Taiwan Mountain Pitviper Ovophis makazayazaya, Zoige pit viper Gloydius angusticeps.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
