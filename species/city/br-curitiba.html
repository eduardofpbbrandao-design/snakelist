Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 15Non-venomous: 59

12 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Brazilian Coral Snake Micrurus decoratus, Brazilian Lancehead Bothrops moojeni, Cerrado Lancehead (pauloensis) Bothrops pauloensis, Cotiara Bothrops cotiara, Jararaca Bothrops jararaca, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Jararacussu Bothrops jararacussu, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Southern Coral Snake Micrurus frontalis, Urutu Bothrops alternatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
