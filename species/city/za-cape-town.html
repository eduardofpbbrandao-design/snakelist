All about Western Cape

These are the snakes of Western Cape, not of Cape Town itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 37 species recorded anywhere in Western Cape, the state or province Cape Town is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16Non-venomous: 21

12 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Coral Shield Cobra Aspidelaps lubricus, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Many-horned Adder Bitis cornuta, Mountain Adder Bitis atropos, Namaqua Dwarf Adder Bitis schneideri, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Red Adder Bitis rubida, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus, Southern Adder Bitis armata.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
