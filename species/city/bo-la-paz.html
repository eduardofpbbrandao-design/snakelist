Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 7Non-venomous: 26

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Andean Lancehead Bothrocophias andianus, Bolivian Coral Snake (obscurus) Micrurus obscurus, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Fer-de-Lance Bothrops atrox, Bothrops monsignifer.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
