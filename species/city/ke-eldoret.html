Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 20Non-venomous: 23

16 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: African Cobra Naja pallida, African Rock Python Python sebae, Ashe's Spitting Cobra Naja ashei, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Black-necked Spitting Cobra Naja nigricollis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Brown Forest Cobra Naja subfulva, Egyptian Saw-scaled Viper Echis pyramidum, Green Night Adder Causus resimus, Kenya Horned Viper Bitis worthingtoni, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Loveridge's Garter Snake Elapsoidea loveridgei, Peters' Burrowing Asp Atractaspis fallax, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Rhinoceros Viper (nasicornis) Bitis nasicornis, Variable Burrowing Asp Atractaspis irregularis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
