Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 14

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Andean Lancehead Bothrocophias andianus, Annellated Coral Snake Micrurus annellatus, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Fer-de-Lance Bothrops atrox, Inca Forest Pit Viper (oligolepis) Bothrops oligolepis, Small-eyed Toadhead Bothrocophias microphthalmus, Speckled Forest Pit Viper Bothrops taeniatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
