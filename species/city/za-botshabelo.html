Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 13Non-venomous: 13

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Coral Shield Cobra Aspidelaps lubricus, Mountain Adder Bitis atropos, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
