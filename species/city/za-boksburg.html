All about Gauteng

These are the snakes of Gauteng, not of Boksburg itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 39 species recorded anywhere in Gauteng, the state or province Boksburg is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 16Non-venomous: 23

11 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Beaked Burrowing Asp Atractaspis duerdeni, Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
