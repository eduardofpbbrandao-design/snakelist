All about Sabah

These are the snakes of Sabah, not of Kota Kinabalu itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 72 species recorded anywhere in Sabah, the state or province Kota Kinabalu is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 18Non-venomous: 54

15 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Blue Coral Snake Calliophis bivirgatus, Blue-necked Keelback Rhabdophis rhodomelas, Borneo Pit Viper Craspedocephalus borneensis, Equatorial Spitting Cobra Naja sumatrana, Malcolm's Pitviper Trimeresurus malcolmi, Mount Kinabalu Pit Viper Garthius chaseni, Muruden Keelback Rhabdophis murudensis, North Philippine Temple Pitviper Tropidolaemus subannulatus, Red-bellied Keelback Rhabdophis conspicillatus, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Sabah Pit Viper Trimeresurus sabahi, Striped Coral Snake (intestinalis) Calliophis intestinalis, Striped Coral Snake (nigrotaeniatus) Calliophis nigrotaeniatus, Sumatra Pit Viper Trimeresurus sumatranus, Yellow-lipped Sea Krait Laticauda colubrina.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
