Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 11Non-venomous: 27

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Brazil's Lancehead Bothrops brazili, Brazilian coralsnake Micrurus brasiliensis, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Caatinga Coral Snake Micrurus ibiboboca, Caatinga Lancehead Bothrops erythromelas, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Fer-de-Lance Bothrops atrox, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Speckled Forest Pit Viper Bothrops taeniatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
