Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 15Non-venomous: 28

13 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Chinese Mountain Pit Viper Ovophis monticola, Green Keelback Rhabdophis nigrocinctus, Gumprecht's Pit Viper Trimeresurus gumprechti, Guo's Green Pit Viper Trimeresurus guoi, Indo-chinese Spitting Cobra Naja siamensis, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Kramer's Pit Viper Trimeresurus macrops, Malayan Pit Viper Calloselasma rhodostoma, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Omkoi Lance-headed Pit Viper Protobothrops kelomohy, Reticulated Python Malayopython reticulatus, Speckled Coral Snake (maculiceps) Calliophis maculiceps.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
