Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 44

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Brazilian Lancehead Bothrops moojeni, Caatinga Coral Snake Micrurus ibiboboca, Caatinga Lancehead Bothrops erythromelas, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Green Anaconda Eunectes murinus, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Lutz's Cerrado Lancehead Bothrops lutzi, South American Coral Snake Micrurus lemniscatus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
