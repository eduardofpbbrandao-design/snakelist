All about Rio Grande do Sul

These are the snakes of Rio Grande do Sul, not of Porto Alegre itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 91 species recorded anywhere in Rio Grande do Sul, the state or province Porto Alegre is in. Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 19Non-venomous: 72

14 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Cotiara Bothrops cotiara, Jararaca Bothrops jararaca, Jararaca Pintada Bothrops neuwiedi, Jararacussu Bothrops jararacussu, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Pampas Lancehead Bothrops pubescens, Silvia coralsnake Micrurus silviae, South American Coral Snake Micrurus lemniscatus, Southern Coral Snake Micrurus frontalis, Uruguayan coralsnake Micrurus altirostris, Urutu Bothrops alternatus, Yellow Anaconda Eunectes notaeus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
