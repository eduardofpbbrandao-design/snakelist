These are the snakes of KwaZulu-Natal, not of Durban itself. Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 26Non-venomous: 36

15 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Bibron's Burrowing Asp Atractaspis bibronii, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Brown Forest Cobra Naja subfulva, Common Night Adder Causus rhombeatus, Gaboon Viper Bitis gabonica, Green Mamba Dendroaspis angusticeps, Lesser African Python Python natalensis, Mountain Adder Bitis atropos, Mozambique Spitting Cobra Naja mossambica, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Ringhals Hemachatus haemachatus, Snouted Cobra Naja annulifera, Snouted Night Adder Causus defilippii, Twig Snake Thelotornis capensis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
