Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 17

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Caatinga Coral Snake Micrurus ibiboboca, Caatinga Lancehead Bothrops erythromelas, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Painted Coral Snake Micrurus corallinus, Potyguara Coral Snake Micrurus potyguara.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
