All about Yunnan

These are the snakes of Yunnan, not of Kunming itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 78 species recorded anywhere in Yunnan, the state or province Kunming is in (worked out from the city’s map position). Many of them may not live in or near the city.

Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 19Non-venomous: 59

17 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Banded Krait Bungarus fasciatus, Burmese Python Python bivittatus, Fea Viper Azemiops feae, Green Keelback Rhabdophis nigrocinctus, Gumprecht's Pit Viper Trimeresurus gumprechti, Guo's Green Pit Viper Trimeresurus guoi, King Cobra Ophiophagus hannah, Likiang pitviper Gloydius monticola, Many-banded Krait Bungarus multicinctus, Mount Gongga Keelback Rhabdophis pentasupralabialis, Nabang Pitviper Trimeresurus caudornatus, Northern Red-necked Keelback Rhabdophis helleri, Pope's Bamboo Pit Viper Trimeresurus popeiorum, Suzhen’s krait Bungarus suzhenae, Taiwan Mountain Pitviper Ovophis makazayazaya, Yunnan Pit Viper Trimeresurus yunnanensis, Zayuan mountain pitviper Ovophis zayuensis.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
