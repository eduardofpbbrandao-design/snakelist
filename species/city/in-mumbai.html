All about Maharashtra

These are the snakes of Maharashtra, not of Mumbai itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 57 species recorded anywhere in Maharashtra, the state or province Mumbai is in. Many of them may not live in or near the city.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
