Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 6Non-venomous: 15

5 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Argentinian Coral Snake Micrurus pyrrhocryptus, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus, Urutu Bothrops alternatus, Yellow Anaconda Eunectes notaeus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
