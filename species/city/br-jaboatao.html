Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 32

9 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Boa Constrictor Boa constrictor, Bushmaster Lachesis muta, Caatinga Coral Snake Micrurus ibiboboca, Caatinga Lancehead Bothrops erythromelas, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Green Jararaca Bothrops bilineatus, Lutz's Cerrado Lancehead Bothrops lutzi, Potyguara Coral Snake Micrurus potyguara, Whitetail Lancehead Bothrops leucurus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
