Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 8

4 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Argentinian Coral Snake Micrurus pyrrhocryptus, Cascabel Rattlesnake Crotalus durissus, Jonathan's Lancehead Bothrops jonathani, Painted Lancehead Bothrops diporus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
