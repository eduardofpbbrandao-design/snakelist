All about Rajasthan

These are the snakes of Rajasthan, not of Jaipur itself. SnakeList records snakes by state or province, not by city, so this page lists the 16 species recorded anywhere in Rajasthan, the state or province Jaipur is in. Many of them may not live in or near the city.
