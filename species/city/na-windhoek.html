Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 10Non-venomous: 4

7 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Anchieta's Cobra Naja anchietae, Black Mamba Dendroaspis polylepis, Boomslang Dispholidus typus, Cape Cobra Naja nivea, Horned Adder Bitis caudalis, Puff Adder Bitis arietans, Western Barred Spitting Cobra Naja nigricincta.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
