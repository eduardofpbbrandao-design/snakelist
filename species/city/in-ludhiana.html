Venomous and non-venomous

Venomous: 8Non-venomous: 5

3 are listed by SnakeList as dangerous to people: Nepal Pitviper Trimeresurus septentrionalis, Russell's Viper Daboia russelii, Sind Krait Bungarus sindanus.

The other venomous ones have mild venom: no serious bites to people are documented for them.
