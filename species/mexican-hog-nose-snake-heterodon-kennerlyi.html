About

Mexican Hog-nose Snake (Heterodon kennerlyi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 200 places on iNaturalist.
