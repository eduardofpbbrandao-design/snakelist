About

Rainbow Boa (Epicrates cenchria) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 353 places on iNaturalist.
