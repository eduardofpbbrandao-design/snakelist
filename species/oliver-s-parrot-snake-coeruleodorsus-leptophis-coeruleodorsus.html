About

Oliver's Parrot Snake (coeruleodorsus) (Leptophis coeruleodorsus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.
