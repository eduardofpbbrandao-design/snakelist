About

Oliver's Parrot Snake (coeruleodorsus) (Leptophis coeruleodorsus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Trinidad and Tobago, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, gallery forest and 13 more. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

Native range

  • Trinidad and Tobago: Couva-Tabaquite-Talparo, Diego Martin Regional Corporation, Mayaro-Rio Claro, Princes Town, Sangre Grande Regional Corporation, Siparia Regional Corporation, Tobago, Tobago (2), Tunapuna-Piarco Regional Corporation
  • Venezuela: Apure, Carabobo
