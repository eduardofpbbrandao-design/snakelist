About

Philippine Liopeltine Snake (Liopeltis philippina) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, philippine rainforest. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
