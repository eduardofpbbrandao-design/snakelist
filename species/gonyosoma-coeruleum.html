About

Blue-eyed Bush Rat Snake (Gonyosoma coeruleum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 45 places on iNaturalist.
