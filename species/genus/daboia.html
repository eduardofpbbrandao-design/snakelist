About the genus

A genus of the family Viperidae (vipers and pit vipers).

What the name means: Daboia – thought to come from a Hindi word, dabohiya, meaning "that lies hid" or "the lurker".[1]

Source
  1. Latin, local tongues and legends: study decodes the meanings of 1,047 Indian reptile names. Down To Earth.
