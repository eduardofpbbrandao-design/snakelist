About the genus

A genus of the family Elapidae (cobras, mambas, coral snakes, sea snakes and relatives).

What the name means: Dendroaspis – from the Greek dendron (tree) and aspis (asp, an old word for a cobra or snake) - "tree asp", as most mambas live in trees.[1]
