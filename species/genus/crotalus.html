About the genus

A genus of the family Viperidae (vipers and pit vipers).

What the name means: Crotalus – from the Greek krotalon, a rattle or castanet - after the rattle at the tip of the tail.[1]

About the genus Crotalus

  • Distribution: living species recorded in 17 countries (+2 territories), on 2 continents: North America, South America.
  • Family: Viperidae.
  • Countries and territories: Argentina, Aruba, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Canada, Colombia, Costa Rica, El Salvador, French Guiana, Guatemala, Guyana, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Suriname, United States, Venezuela.
