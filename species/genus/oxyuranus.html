About the genus

A genus of the family Elapidae (cobras, mambas, coral snakes, sea snakes and relatives).

What the name means: Oxyuranus – from the Greek oxys (sharp, needle-like) and ouranos (arch, vault of the sky) - for a needle-like point on the arch of the palate.[1]
