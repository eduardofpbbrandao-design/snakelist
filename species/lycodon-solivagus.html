About

Common Wolf Snake (solivagus) (Lycodon solivagus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
