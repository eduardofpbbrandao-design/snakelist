About

Black Racer (Hypsirhynchus ater) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Jamaica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
