About

Chinese Sea Snake (Laticauda semifasciata) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in China, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines, South Korea and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, temperate rainforest, temperate deciduous forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 116 places on iNaturalist.
