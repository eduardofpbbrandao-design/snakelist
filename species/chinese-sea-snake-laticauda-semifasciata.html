Chinese Sea Snake

Laticauda semifasciata

Chinese Sea Snake (Laticauda semifasciata) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in China, Indonesia, Japan, Philippines, South Korea and Taiwan.

Where the name comes from

semifasciata: ‘half-banded’, from Latin roots: semi- ‘half’ + -fasciata ‘banded’

Chinese Sea Snake: ‘Chinese’ – it lives in China; ‘Sea Snake’ – it lives in the sea.
