About

Montpellier snake (Malpolon monspessulanus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in France, Gibraltar, Monaco, Portugal, Spain. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 25 more. Common: recorded in 1629 places on iNaturalist.
