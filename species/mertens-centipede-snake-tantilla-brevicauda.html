About

Mertens' Centipede Snake (Tantilla brevicauda) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 8 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
