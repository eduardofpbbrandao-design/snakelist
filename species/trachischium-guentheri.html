About

Rosebelly Worm-eating Snake (Trachischium guentheri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Nepal. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest, montane woodland and 6 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
