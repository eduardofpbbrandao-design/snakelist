About

Dwarf Wolf Snake (Lycophidion nanum) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Mozambique, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, coastal scrub, east african coastal forest. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
