About

Newman's earth snake (Adelphicos newmanorum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as scrub forest, thorn forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 13 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
