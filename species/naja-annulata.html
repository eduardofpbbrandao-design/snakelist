About

Ringed Water Cobra (Naja annulata) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Angola, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 38 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
