About

Indo-Chinese Rat Snake (Ptyas korros) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 942 places on iNaturalist.
