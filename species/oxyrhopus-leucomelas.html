About

Werner's False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus leucomelas) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 21 places on iNaturalist.
