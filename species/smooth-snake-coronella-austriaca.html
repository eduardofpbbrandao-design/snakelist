About

Smooth Snake (Coronella austriaca) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Andorra, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Crimea and 21 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 4312 places on iNaturalist.
