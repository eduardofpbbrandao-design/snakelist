About

Bicoloured blind skink (Afrotyphlops nigrocandidus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
