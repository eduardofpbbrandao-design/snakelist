About

Reinhardt's Burrowing Snake (Apostolepis assimilis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 20 more. Recorded in 51 places on iNaturalist.
