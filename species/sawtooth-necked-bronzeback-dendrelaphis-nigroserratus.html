About

Sawtooth-necked Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis nigroserratus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Myanmar, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
