Eastern Montpellier Snake

Malpolon insignitus (Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire, 1827)

The Eastern Montpellier Snake is a large, mildly venomous, rear-fanged snake of the eastern Mediterranean, the Middle East and North Africa.[1]

Identification

Appearance

Often has narrow pale stripes along the body; unlike the western Montpellier Snake, males have no dark 'saddle'.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: From the eastern Adriatic coast through the Balkans, Greece, Turkey and Cyprus to the Caucasus, Iraq, Iran and Arabia, and across North Africa from Morocco to Egypt.[1,2]
