About

Doria's Green Snake (Ptyas doriae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 8 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
