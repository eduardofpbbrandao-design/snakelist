About

Mud Snake (caesurus) (Hydrops caesurus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, riverine forest and 22 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
