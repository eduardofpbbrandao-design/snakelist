About

Brown Water Snake (taxispilota) (Nerodia taxispilota) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 813 places on iNaturalist.
