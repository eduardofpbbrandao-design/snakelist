About

Gulf Coast Vine Snake (Oxybelis potosiensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 193 places on iNaturalist.
