About

Dahl's Whip Snake (Platyceps najadum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Croatia, Kosovo, Serbia. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, woodland and 14 more. Common: recorded in 320 places on iNaturalist.
