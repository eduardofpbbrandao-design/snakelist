About

Deharveng's Blind Snake (Cyclotyphlops deharvengi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
