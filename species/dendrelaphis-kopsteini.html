About

Kopstein's Bronzeback Snake (Dendrelaphis kopsteini) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 24 more. Recorded in 34 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Indonesia: Kalimantan (Borneo), Sumatra
  • Malaysia: Johor, Negeri Sembilan, Pahang, Sabah, Sarawak
  • Singapore: Central Singapore Community Development Council, South West Community Development Council
  • Thailand: Songkhla, Yala
