About

False Smooth Snake (mauritanicus) (Macroprotodon mauritanicus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Tunisia. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Mallorca and Menorca), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 6 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Algeria: Bordj Bou Arréridj, Béjaïa, Constantine, Tizi Ouzou
  • Tunisia: Bizerte, Jendouba, Manouba
