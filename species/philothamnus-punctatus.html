About

Spotted Green Snake (Philothamnus punctatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, coastal forest, gallery forest, woodland and 12 more. Recorded in 52 places on iNaturalist.
