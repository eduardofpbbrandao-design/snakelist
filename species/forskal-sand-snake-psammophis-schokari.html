About

Forskal Sand Snake (Psammophis schokari) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Afghanistan, Gaza Strip, India, Iran, Israel and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 250 places on iNaturalist.
