About

Ball Python (Python regius) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 13 more. Recorded in 71 places on iNaturalist.
