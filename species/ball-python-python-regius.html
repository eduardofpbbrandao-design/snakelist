Ball Python

Python regius

The Ball Python, or Royal Python, is the smallest of Africa's pythons: a calm, non-venomous constrictor from West and Central Africa. Its name comes from its habit of rolling into a tight ball, with its head tucked inside, when it feels threatened. It is one of the most traded pet reptiles in the world.[1,2]

Ecology

Habitat

Grasslands, savannas and sparsely wooded country.[3]

Behaviour

When stressed it curls into a ball, protecting its head in the middle of its coils, instead of biting.[1]

Conservation

NTReassessed in 2021 as Near Threatened on the IUCN Red List (previously Least Concern), because of a suspected decline of about 30% over three generations, mainly in West Africa.[1]

The main threat is the capture of wild snakes for the international pet trade, which a CITES study described as continuing and possibly increasing.[1,2]
