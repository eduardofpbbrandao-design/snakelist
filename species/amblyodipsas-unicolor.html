About

Dull Purple-glossed Snake (Amblyodipsas unicolor) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Central African Republic, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 16 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 14 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
