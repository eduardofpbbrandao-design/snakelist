Luzon Kukri Snake (Oligodon modestus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
