About

Trinidad Blind Snake (Typhlophis squamosus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Anomalepididae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
