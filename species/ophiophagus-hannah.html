About

King Cobra (Ophiophagus hannah) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India and 9 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 46 more. Common: recorded in 213 places on iNaturalist.
