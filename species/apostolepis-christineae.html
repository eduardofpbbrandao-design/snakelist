About

Christine's Blackhead (Apostolepis christineae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical dry forest, woodland and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
