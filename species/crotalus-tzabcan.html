About

Yucatan Neotropical Rattlesnake (Crotalus tzabcan) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 8 more. Common: recorded in 285 places on iNaturalist.
