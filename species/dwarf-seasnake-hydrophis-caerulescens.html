About

Dwarf Seasnake (Hydrophis caerulescens) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 8 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
