About

Clark's Forest Racer (Dendrophidion clarkii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 11 more. Recorded in 45 places on iNaturalist.
