About

Bailey's Blind Snake (Trilepida anthracina) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Ecuador. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
