Northeastern Hill Krait

Bungarus bungaroides

Northeastern Hill Krait (Bungarus bungaroides) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar, Nepal and Vietnam. It is known to eat other snakes.
