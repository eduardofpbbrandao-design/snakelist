About

Taiwan Mountain Pitviper (Ovophis makazayazaya) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in China, Laos, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 118 places on iNaturalist.
