About

Green treesnake (Dendrelaphis calligaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 79 places on iNaturalist.
