About

Western Green Mamba (Dendroaspis viridis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Benin, Central African Republic, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 19 more. Recorded in 31 places on iNaturalist.
