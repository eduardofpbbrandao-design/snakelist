About

Hoodless Cobra (Pseudohaje nigra) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Liberia, Nigeria and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 1 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
