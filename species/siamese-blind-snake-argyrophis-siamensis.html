About

Siamese Blind Snake (Argyrophis siamensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Cambodia, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
