About

Boulenger's Garter Snake (Elapsoidea boulengeri) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 21 more. Recorded in 46 places on iNaturalist.
