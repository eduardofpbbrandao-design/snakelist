About

Thick Graceful Brown Snake (Rhadinaea calligaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 10 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
