About

Schmidt's blind-snake (Afrotyphlops schmidti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, South Africa, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 13 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
