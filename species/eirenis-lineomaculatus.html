About

Striped Dwarf Snake (Eirenis lineomaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 13 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
