Coal Snake

Pseudoboa neuwiedii

Coal Snake (Pseudoboa neuwiedii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, French Guiana, Guyana and 5 more. It is known to eat other snakes.
