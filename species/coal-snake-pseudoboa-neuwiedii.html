About

Coal Snake (Pseudoboa neuwiedii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, French Guiana, Guyana and 5 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 184 places on iNaturalist.
