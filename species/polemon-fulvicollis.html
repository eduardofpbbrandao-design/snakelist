About

African Snake-eater (Polemon fulvicollis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Uganda. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 3 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
