About

European Adder (Vipera berus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Austria, Belarus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, temperate coniferous forest, boreal forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 6065 places on iNaturalist.
