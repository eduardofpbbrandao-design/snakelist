    Where the name comes from

    dorbignyi: named after Alcide d'Orbigny, a French naturalist who explored South America

    Bolivian Burrowing Snake: ‘Bolivian’ – it lives in Bolivia; ‘Burrowing Snake’ – it burrows underground.
