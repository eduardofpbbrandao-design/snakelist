About

Cross-barred Snake (Dipsadoboa aulica) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia, South Africa and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest, broadleaf forest and 13 more. Recorded in 58 places on iNaturalist.
