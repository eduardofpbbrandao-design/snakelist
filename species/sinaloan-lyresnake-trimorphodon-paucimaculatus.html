About

Sinaloan lyresnake (Trimorphodon paucimaculatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 5 more. Common: recorded in 87 places on iNaturalist.
