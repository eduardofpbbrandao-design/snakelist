Where the name comes from

marchi: named after a person called March

March's Palm Pit Viper: ‘March's’ – named after a person called March; ‘Palm’ – it lives in palm trees; ‘Pit Viper’ – it has a heat-sensing pit on each side of its head, between the eye and the nostril.
