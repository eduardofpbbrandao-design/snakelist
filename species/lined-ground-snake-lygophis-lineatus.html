About

Lined Ground Snake (Lygophis lineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 92 places on iNaturalist.
