Boulenger's Garter Snake

Elapsoidea boulengeri

Boulenger's Garter Snake (Elapsoidea boulengeri) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Botswana, Eswatini, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 4 more.
