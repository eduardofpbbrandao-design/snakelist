About

Sri Lanka Worm Snake (ceylonicus) (Gerrhopilus ceylonicus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Gerrhopilidae, found in Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, underground burrows and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
