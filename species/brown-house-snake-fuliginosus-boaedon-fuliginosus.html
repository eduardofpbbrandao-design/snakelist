About

Brown House Snake (fuliginosus) (Boaedon fuliginosus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 30 more. Common: recorded in 99 places on iNaturalist.
