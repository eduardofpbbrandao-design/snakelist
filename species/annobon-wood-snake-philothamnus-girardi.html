About

Annobon Wood Snake (Philothamnus girardi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Equatorial Guinea, Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
