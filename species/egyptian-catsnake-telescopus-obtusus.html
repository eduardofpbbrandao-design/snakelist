About

Egyptian Catsnake (Telescopus obtusus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Central African Republic, Chad, Egypt, Eritrea and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
