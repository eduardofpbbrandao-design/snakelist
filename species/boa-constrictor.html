About

Boa Constrictor (Boa constrictor) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 7 more. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Puerto Rico, Aruba), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 60 more. Common: recorded in 1289 places on iNaturalist.
