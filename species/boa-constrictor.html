About

Boa Constrictor (Boa constrictor) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 7 more. It has also been introduced by people outside its native range (Puerto Rico, Aruba). Common: recorded in 1289 places on iNaturalist.
