About

Boa Constrictor (Boa constrictor) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 7 more. It is also invasive outside its native range (Puerto Rico, Aruba). It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 60 more. Common: recorded in 1289 places on iNaturalist.
