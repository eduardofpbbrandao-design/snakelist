About

South American Sipo (foveatus) (Chironius foveatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, subtropical forest and 16 more. Recorded in 63 places on iNaturalist.
