About

Flat-tail Sea Snake (schistorhyncha) (Laticauda schistorhyncha) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
