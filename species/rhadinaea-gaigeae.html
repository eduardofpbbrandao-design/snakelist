About

Gaige's Pine Forest Snake (Rhadinaea gaigeae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, pine forest and 16 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
