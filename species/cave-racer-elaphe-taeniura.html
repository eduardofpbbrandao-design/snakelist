About

Cave Racer (Elaphe taeniura) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Indonesia, Japan and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 406 places on iNaturalist.
