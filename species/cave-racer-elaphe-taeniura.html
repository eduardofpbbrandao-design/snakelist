Cave Racer

Elaphe taeniura Cope, 1861

The Beauty Rat Snake (Cave Racer) is a long, slim, non-venomous rat snake of East and South-east Asia. Some live in caves.[1]

Distribution

Published distribution

Native: Much of China (not the north-east or west), Taiwan, Korea, the Ryukyu Islands, north-eastern India and Bhutan through Myanmar and Indochina to Malaysia, Sumatra and Borneo.[1,2]

Behaviour and ecology

Habitat

Rainforest and caves.[1]

Behaviour

A good climber that can move along cave walls; active at any time of day or night.[1]

Diet

Rodents - and in caves, roosting bats. It kills by squeezing.[1]

Reproduction

Lays eggs.[2]
