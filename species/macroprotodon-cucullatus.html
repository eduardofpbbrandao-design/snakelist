About

False Smooth Snake (cucullatus) (Macroprotodon cucullatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Israel, Italy and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 19 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
