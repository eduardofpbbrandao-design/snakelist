About

Central American Indigo Snake (Drymarchon melanurus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 8 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 39 more. Common: recorded in 749 places on iNaturalist.
