Central American Indigo Snake

Drymarchon melanurus

Central American Indigo Snake (Drymarchon melanurus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 8 more. It is known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

melanurus: ‘black-tailed’

Central American Indigo Snake: ‘Central American’ – after Central America; ‘Indigo Snake’ – its glossy blue-black colour.
