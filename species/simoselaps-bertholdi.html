About

Desert Banded Snake (Simoselaps bertholdi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, eucalyptus forest, woodland, dry woodland and 26 more. Common: recorded in 87 places on iNaturalist.
