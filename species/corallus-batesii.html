About

Bates' Tree Boa (Corallus batesii) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 80 places on iNaturalist.
