About

Red-headed Krait (Bungarus flaviceps) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. Recorded in 60 places on iNaturalist.
