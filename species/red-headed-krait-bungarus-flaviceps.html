Red-headed Krait

Bungarus flaviceps

Red-headed Krait (Bungarus flaviceps) is a venomous snake of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam. It is known to eat other snakes.
