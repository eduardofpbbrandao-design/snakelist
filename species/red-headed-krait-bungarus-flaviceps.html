About

Red-headed Krait (Bungarus flaviceps) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Thailand and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 19 more. Recorded in 60 places on iNaturalist.
