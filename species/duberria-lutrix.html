About

Common Slug Eater (Duberria lutrix) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudoxyrhophiidae, found in Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Kenya and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 352 places on iNaturalist.
