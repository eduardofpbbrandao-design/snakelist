About

Cross-banded Mountain Rattlesnake (Crotalus transversus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, pine forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 7 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
