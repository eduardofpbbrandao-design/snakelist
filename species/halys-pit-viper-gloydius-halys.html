About

Halys Pit Viper (Gloydius halys) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Afghanistan, China, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 184 places on iNaturalist.
