About

Red-naped Snake (Furina diadema) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, temperate rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 204 places on iNaturalist.
