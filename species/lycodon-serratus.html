About

Serrate-banded Wolf Snake (Lycodon serratus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
