About

Mexican Smallhead Rattlesnake (Crotalus intermedius) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 37 places on iNaturalist.
