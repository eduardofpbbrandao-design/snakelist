About

Banded Shovel-snout (Prosymna semifasciata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
