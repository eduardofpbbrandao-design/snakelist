About

Kaulback's Lance-headed Pitviper (Protobothrops kaulbacki) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 9 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
