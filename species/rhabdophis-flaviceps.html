About

Orange-lipped Keelback (Rhabdophis flaviceps) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 25 more. Recorded in 23 places on iNaturalist.
