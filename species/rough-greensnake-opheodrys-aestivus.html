About

Rough Greensnake (Opheodrys aestivus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 4871 places on iNaturalist.
