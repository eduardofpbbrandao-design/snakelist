About

Boie's Dwarf Snake (Calamaria virgulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
