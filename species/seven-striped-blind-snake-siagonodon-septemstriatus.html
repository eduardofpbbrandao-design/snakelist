Seven-striped Blind Snake (Siagonodon septemstriatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Guyana and Venezuela.

Where the name comes from

septemstriatus: ‘seven-striped’, from Latin roots: septem- ‘seven’ + -striatus ‘striped’

Seven-striped Blind Snake: ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
