About

Sabah Keelback (Hebius flavifrons) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 27 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
