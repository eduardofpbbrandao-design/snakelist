About

Malayan Pit Viper (Calloselasma rhodostoma) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Thailand and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 214 places on iNaturalist.
