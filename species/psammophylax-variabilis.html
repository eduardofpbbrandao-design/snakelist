About

Grey-bellied Grass Snake (Psammophylax variabilis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Botswana, Burundi, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ethiopia, Kenya and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 13 places on iNaturalist.
