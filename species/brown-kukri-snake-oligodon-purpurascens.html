About

Brown Kukri Snake (Oligodon purpurascens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Common: recorded in 92 places on iNaturalist.

Native range
