About

Saw-scaled Viper (Echis carinatus) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Afghanistan, India, Iran, Oman, Pakistan and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 37 more. Common: recorded in 254 places on iNaturalist.
