About

Yaqui Black-headed Snake (Tantilla yaquia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest and 16 more. Recorded in 53 places on iNaturalist.
