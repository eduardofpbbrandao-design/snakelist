About

Brown Rainbow Boa (Epicrates maurus) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica, French Guiana, Guyana and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 213 places on iNaturalist.
