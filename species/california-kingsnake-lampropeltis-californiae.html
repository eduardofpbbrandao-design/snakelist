About

California Kingsnake (Lampropeltis californiae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It has also been introduced outside its native range (Canary Islands), where it is invasive. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest and 33 more. Common: recorded in 1837 places on iNaturalist.
