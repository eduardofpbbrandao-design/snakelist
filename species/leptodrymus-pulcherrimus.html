About

Striped Lowland Snake (Leptodrymus pulcherrimus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 5 more. Recorded in 67 places on iNaturalist.
