About

Modigliani's Dwarf Reed Snake (Pseudorabdion modiglianii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
