Where the name comes from

Lycodon: from the Greek lykos (wolf) and odon (tooth) - for the long, fang-like front teeth[1]

Boehme's Wolf Snake: ‘Boehme's’ – named after a person called Boehme; ‘Wolf Snake’ – it has long fang-like front teeth, like a wolf.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
