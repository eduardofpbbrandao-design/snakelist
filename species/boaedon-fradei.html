About

Frade’s Brown House Snake (Boaedon fradei) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Kenya, Malawi and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 10 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
