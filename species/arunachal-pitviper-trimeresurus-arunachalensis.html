About

Arunachal Pitviper (Trimeresurus arunachalensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 10 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
