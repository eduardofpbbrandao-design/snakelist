About

Sinai Racer (Platyceps sinai) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Egypt, Israel, Jordan, Saudi Arabia. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 15 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
