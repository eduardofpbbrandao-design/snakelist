About

Forest Marsh Snake (Natriciteres sylvatica) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Malawi, Mozambique, South Africa, Tanzania, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 26 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
