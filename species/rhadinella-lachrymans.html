About

Tearful Pine-Oak Snake (Rhadinella lachrymans) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 7 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
