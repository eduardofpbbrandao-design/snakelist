About

Chinese Cobra (Naja atra) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 415 places on iNaturalist.
