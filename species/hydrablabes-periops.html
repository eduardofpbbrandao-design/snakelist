About

Olive Small-eyed Snake (Hydrablabes periops) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 26 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
