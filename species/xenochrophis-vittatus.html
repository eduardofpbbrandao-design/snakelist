About

Striped Keelback (Xenochrophis vittatus) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 21 more. Common: recorded in 88 places on iNaturalist.
