About

Big Ground Snake (Atractus major) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.

  • Ecuador: Morona-Santiago, Orellana, Pastaza, Tungurahua, Zamora Chinchipe
