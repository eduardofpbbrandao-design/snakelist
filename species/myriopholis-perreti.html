Cameroon Thread Snake (Myriopholis perreti) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Cameroon, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 7 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
