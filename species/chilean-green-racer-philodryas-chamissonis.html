Chilean Green Racer

Philodryas chamissonis (Wiegmann, 1835)

The Chilean Green Racer (Chilean Long-tailed Snake) is a rear-fanged, mildly venomous snake found only in Chile.[1]

Who described it

First described by Wiegmann in 1835. The brackets around the authority, (Wiegmann, 1835), mean it was first described in a different genus.

Where the name comes from

Chilean Green Racer: ‘Chilean’ – it lives in Chile; ‘Green’ – its colour; ‘Racer’ – a name for fast-moving snakes.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
