About

Painted Coral Snake (Micrurus corallinus) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Paraguay and 1 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 27 more. Common: recorded in 231 places on iNaturalist.
