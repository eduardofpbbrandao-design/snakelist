About

Greater Green Snake (Ptyas major) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 24 more. Common: recorded in 499 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Vietnam: Hà Giang, Lào Cai
