About

Kunua Blind Snake (Acutotyphlops kunuaensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Papua New Guinea, Solomon Islands. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
