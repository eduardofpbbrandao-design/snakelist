About

Dotted Dwarf Snake (Eirenis punctatolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Armenia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq, Turkey. It lives in habitats such as montane woodland, mountain forest, steppe, desert and 16 more. Recorded in 25 places on iNaturalist.
