About

Common Many-tooth Snake (Sibynophis collaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 75 places on iNaturalist.
