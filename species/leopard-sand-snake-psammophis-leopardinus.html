About

Leopard Sand Snake (Psammophis leopardinus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Namibia, Zambia. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, gallery forest, woodland, dry woodland and 19 more. Recorded in 19 places on iNaturalist.
