Yellow-Lipped Sea Krait

Laticauda saintgironsi

Yellow-Lipped Sea Krait (Laticauda saintgironsi) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, New Caledonia and New Zealand.

Where the name comes from

saintgironsi: named after Hubert Saint-Girons, a French herpetologist

Yellow-Lipped Sea Krait: ‘Yellow-Lipped’ – for its yellow lips; ‘Sea Krait’ – a krait-like snake that lives in the sea.
