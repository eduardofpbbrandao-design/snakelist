About

Yellow-Lipped Sea Krait (Laticauda saintgironsi) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India, New Caledonia, New Zealand. It lives in habitats such as coastal lagoons, beaches, rocky shores, coral reefs and 2 more. Recorded in 67 places on iNaturalist.
