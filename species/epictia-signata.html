About

South American Blind Snake (signata) (Epictia signata) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Colombia, Venezuela. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil, leaf litter and 2 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
