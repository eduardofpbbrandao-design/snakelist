About

Leopard Keelback (Helicops leopardinus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, French Guiana, Paraguay, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 51 more. Common: recorded in 122 places on iNaturalist.
