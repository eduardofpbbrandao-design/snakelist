About

Short-snouted Tree Snake (Dipsadoboa brevirostris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 2 more. Recorded in 3 places on iNaturalist.
