About

Splendid Cat-eyed Snake (Leptodeira splendida) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 22 more. Recorded in 49 places on iNaturalist.
