About

Ringneck Coffee Snake (Ninia diademata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Common: recorded in 114 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Belize: Toledo
  • Guatemala: Alta Verapaz, Huehuetenango, Retalhuleu, San Marcos
  • Honduras: Comayagua, Cortés, Santa Bárbara
  • Mexico: Chiapas, Quintana Roo, San Luis Potosí
