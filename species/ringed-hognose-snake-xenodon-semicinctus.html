About

Ringed Hognose Snake (Xenodon semicinctus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, mountain forest, grassland, temperate grassland and 6 more. Recorded in 41 places on iNaturalist.
