About

Gomes' Pampas Snake (Rodriguesophis iglesiasi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 17 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
