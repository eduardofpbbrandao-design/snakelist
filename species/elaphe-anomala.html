About

Korean Ratsnake (Elaphe anomala) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Mongolia, South Korea. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 18 more. Recorded in 74 places on iNaturalist.
