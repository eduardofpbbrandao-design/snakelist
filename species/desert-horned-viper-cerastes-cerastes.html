About

Desert Horned Viper (Cerastes cerastes) is a venomous viper of the family Viperidae, found in Algeria, Chad, Egypt, Israel, Jordan and 9 more. It lives in habitats such as woodland, dry woodland, open woodland, scrub forest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 172 places on iNaturalist.
