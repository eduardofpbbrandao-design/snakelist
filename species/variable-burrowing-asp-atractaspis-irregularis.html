Variable Burrowing Asp

Atractaspis irregularis

Variable Burrowing Asp (Atractaspis irregularis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Eritrea and 13 more.

Where the name comes from

irregularis: ‘irregular’

Variable Burrowing Asp: ‘Variable’ – its colours vary a lot; ‘Burrowing Asp’ – it burrows, and is venomous (asp is an old word for a venomous snake).
