About

False Ficimia (Pseudoficimia frontalis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 18 more. Recorded in 35 places on iNaturalist.
