About

Fitzinger's False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus fitzingeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as forest edges, desert, coastal desert, roadsides. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
