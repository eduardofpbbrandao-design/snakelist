About

Andean Lancehead (Bothrocophias andianus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Bolivia, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 18 more. Recorded in 15 places on iNaturalist.
