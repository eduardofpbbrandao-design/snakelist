Where the name comes from

gastrostictus: ‘with a spotted belly’, from Greek roots: gastro- ‘belly’ + -stictus ‘spotted’

Montane Treesnake: ‘Montane’ – it lives in mountains; ‘Treesnake’ – it lives in trees.
