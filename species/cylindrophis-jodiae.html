About

Jodi's Pipe-Snake (Cylindrophis jodiae) is a non-venomous snake of the family Cylindrophiidae, found in Cambodia, China, Thailand, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 9 more. Common: recorded in 132 places on iNaturalist.
