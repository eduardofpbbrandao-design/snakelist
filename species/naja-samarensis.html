About

Samar Cobra (Naja samarensis) is a venomous cobra of the family Elapidae, found in Philippines. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 13 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
