Amazon False Coral Snake

Oxyrhopus rhombifer

Amazon False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus rhombifer) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru and Uruguay.
