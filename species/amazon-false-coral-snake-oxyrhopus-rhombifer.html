About

Amazon False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus rhombifer) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 50 more. Common: recorded in 185 places on iNaturalist.
