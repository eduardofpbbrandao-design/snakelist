About

Black-tailed Rattlesnake (Crotalus molossus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 41 more. Common: recorded in 414 places on iNaturalist.
