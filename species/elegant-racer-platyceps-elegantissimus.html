About

Elegant Racer (Platyceps elegantissimus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Israel, Jordan, Saudi Arabia. It lives in habitats such as woodland, open woodland, shrubland, scrubland and 14 more. Recorded in 9 places on iNaturalist.
