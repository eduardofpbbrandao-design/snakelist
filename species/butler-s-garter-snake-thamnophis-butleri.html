About

Butler's Garter Snake (Thamnophis butleri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, boreal forest, broadleaf forest and 16 more. Common: recorded in 121 places on iNaturalist.
