About

Mangrove Snak (Erythrolamprus cobella) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, French Guiana, Guyana and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 33 places on iNaturalist.
