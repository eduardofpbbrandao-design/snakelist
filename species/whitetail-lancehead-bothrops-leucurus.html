About

Whitetail Lancehead (Bothrops leucurus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 23 more. Common: recorded in 76 places on iNaturalist.
