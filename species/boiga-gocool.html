About

Eastern Cat Snake (Boiga gocool) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 12 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

  • Bangladesh: Chattogram, Khulna, Sylhet
  • Bhutan: Samdrup Jongkhar
  • India: Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Sikkim, Tripura
