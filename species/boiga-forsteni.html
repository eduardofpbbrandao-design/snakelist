About

Forsten's Cat Snake (Boiga forsteni) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in India, Nepal, Sri Lanka. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 111 places on iNaturalist.

  • Sri Lanka: Anuradhapura, Galle, Hambantota, Matale, Matara, Polonnaruwa
