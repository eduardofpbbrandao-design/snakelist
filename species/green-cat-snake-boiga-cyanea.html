About

Green Cat Snake (Boiga cyanea) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Cambodia, China, India, Laos and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 168 places on iNaturalist.
