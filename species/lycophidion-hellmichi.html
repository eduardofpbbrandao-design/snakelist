About

Hellmich's Wolf Snake (Lycophidion hellmichi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Namibia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 1 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
