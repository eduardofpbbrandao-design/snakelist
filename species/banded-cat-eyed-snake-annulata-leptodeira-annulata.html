About

Banded Cat-eyed Snake (annulata) (Leptodeira annulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica and 14 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 59 more. Common: recorded in 277 places on iNaturalist.
