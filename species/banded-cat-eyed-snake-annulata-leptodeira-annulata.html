Banded Cat-eyed Snake (annulata)

Leptodeira annulata

Banded Cat-eyed Snake (annulata) (Leptodeira annulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Costa Rica and 14 more.
