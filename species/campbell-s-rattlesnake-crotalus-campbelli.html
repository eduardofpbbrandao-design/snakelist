About

Campbell's Rattlesnake (Crotalus campbelli) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, pine forest, forest edges and 2 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
