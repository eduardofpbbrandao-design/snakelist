About

Maynard's Longnose Sand Snake (Lytorhynchus maynardi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Afghanistan, Iran, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as sand dunes, underground burrows, loose soil, sandy soil. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
