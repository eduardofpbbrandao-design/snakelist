About

Cantor's Narrow-headed Sea Snake (Hydrophis cantoris) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 9 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
