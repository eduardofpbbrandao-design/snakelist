About

Green Trinket Snake (Gonyosoma prasinum) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, China, India, Malaysia, Myanmar. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 11 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
