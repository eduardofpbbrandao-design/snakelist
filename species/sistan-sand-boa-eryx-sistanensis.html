About

Sistan Sand Boa (Eryx sistanensis) is a non-venomous boa of the family Boidae, found in India, Iran, Pakistan. It lives in habitats such as tropical dry forest, subtropical dry forest, dry woodland, scrub forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
