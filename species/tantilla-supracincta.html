About

Banded Centipede Snake (Tantilla supracincta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 17 more. Recorded in 23 places on iNaturalist.
