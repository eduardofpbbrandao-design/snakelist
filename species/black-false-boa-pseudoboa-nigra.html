About

Black False Boa (Pseudoboa nigra) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Peru. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 32 more. Common: recorded in 209 places on iNaturalist.
