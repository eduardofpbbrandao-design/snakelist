About

Ruthven's Burrowing Snake (Apostolepis tenuis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
