About

Military Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus miliaris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 51 more. Common: recorded in 363 places on iNaturalist.
