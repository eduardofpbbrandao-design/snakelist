About

Jaeger's Ground Snake (Erythrolamprus jaegeri) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 21 more. Recorded in 57 places on iNaturalist.
