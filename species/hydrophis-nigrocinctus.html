About

Black-banded Sea Snake (Hydrophis nigrocinctus) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, India, Malaysia, Myanmar, Sri Lanka and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
