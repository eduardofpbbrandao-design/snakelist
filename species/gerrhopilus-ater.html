About

Black blindsnake (Gerrhopilus ater) is a non-venomous snake of the family Gerrhopilidae, found in Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
