About

Elegant Sand Racer (Psammophis elegans) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Gambia and 13 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 26 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
