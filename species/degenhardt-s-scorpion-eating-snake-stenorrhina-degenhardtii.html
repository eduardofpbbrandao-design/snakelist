About

Degenhardt's Scorpion-eating Snake (Stenorrhina degenhardtii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 7 more. Common: recorded in 184 places on iNaturalist.
