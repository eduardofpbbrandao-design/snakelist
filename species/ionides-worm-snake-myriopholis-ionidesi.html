About

Ionides' Worm Snake (Myriopholis ionidesi) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, coastal forest, woodland, open woodland and 11 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
