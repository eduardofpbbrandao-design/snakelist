About

Central American Jumping Pit Viper (Metlapilcoatlus mexicanus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Common: recorded in 104 places on iNaturalist.
