About

Formosa Slug Snake (Pareas formosensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in China, Laos, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 17 more. Common: recorded in 180 places on iNaturalist.
