About

MacLelland's Coral Snake (Sinomicrurus macclellandi) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Japan and 5 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 21 more. Recorded in 28 places on iNaturalist.
