About

Desert Nightsnake (Hypsiglena chlorophaea) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Canada, Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate rainforest, temperate coniferous forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 40 more. Common: recorded in 517 places on iNaturalist.
