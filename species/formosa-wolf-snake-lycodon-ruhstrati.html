About

Formosa Wolf Snake (Lycodon ruhstrati) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Japan, Taiwan, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest and 18 more. Common: recorded in 286 places on iNaturalist.
