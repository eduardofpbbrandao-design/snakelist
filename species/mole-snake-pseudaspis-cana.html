About

Mole Snake (Pseudaspis cana) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pseudaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Malawi, Mozambique, Namibia and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 31 more. Common: recorded in 456 places on iNaturalist.
