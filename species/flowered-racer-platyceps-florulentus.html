About

Flowered Racer (Platyceps florulentus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Egypt, Eritrea, Ethiopia, Kenya and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 33 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
