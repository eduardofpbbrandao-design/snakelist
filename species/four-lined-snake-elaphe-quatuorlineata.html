About

Four-lined Snake (Elaphe quatuorlineata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Albania, Bulgaria, Croatia, Greece, Italy and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 502 places on iNaturalist.
