About

Chamba Pitviper (Gloydius chambensis) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
