About

Collared Blind Snake (Habrophallos collaris) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Suriname. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 12 places on iNaturalist.
