About

Beautiful Sand Snake (Psammophis pulcher) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Ethiopia, Kenya, Somalia. It lives in habitats such as coastal forest, scrub forest, thorn forest, forest edges and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
