About

Collared Reed Snake (pavimentata) (Calamaria pavimentata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Indonesia and 7 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 116 places on iNaturalist.
