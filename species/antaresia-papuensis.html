About

Papuan Spotted Python (Antaresia papuensis) is a non-venomous python of the family Pythonidae, found in Australia, Indonesia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 25 more. Recorded in 6 places on iNaturalist.
