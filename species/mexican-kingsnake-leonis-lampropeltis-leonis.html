Mexican Kingsnake (leonis)

Lampropeltis leonis

Mexican Kingsnake (leonis) (Lampropeltis leonis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes.

Where the name comes from

Lampropeltis: from the Greek lampros (shiny) and peltē (small shield) - for the smooth, glossy scales[1]

Mexican Kingsnake (leonis): ‘Mexican’ – it lives in Mexico.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
