About

Mexican Kingsnake (leonis) (Lampropeltis leonis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as scrub forest, thorn forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 14 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.

Where it lives

Native range
