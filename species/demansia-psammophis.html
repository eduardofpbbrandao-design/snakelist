About

Yellow-faced Whipsnake (psammophis) (Demansia psammophis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, temperate rainforest and 30 more. Common: recorded in 514 places on iNaturalist.
