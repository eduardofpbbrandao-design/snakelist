About

Northern Red-necked Keelback (Rhabdophis helleri) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Bangladesh, Bhutan, China, India, Myanmar and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 268 places on iNaturalist.
