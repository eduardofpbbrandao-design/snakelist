About

Blackbelly Snake (Hydraethiops melanogaster) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon and 2 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
