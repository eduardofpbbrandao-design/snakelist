About

African Banded Snake (Chamaelycus fasciatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea and 8 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 14 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
