About

Senegalese Egg Eater (Dasypeltis latericia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guinea, Mali, Senegal. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
