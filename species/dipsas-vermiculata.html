About

Vermiculate Snail-eater (Dipsas vermiculata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 11 more. Recorded in 8 places on iNaturalist.
