About

Regal Coral Snake (Micrurus ancoralis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Colombia, Ecuador, Panama. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 7 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
