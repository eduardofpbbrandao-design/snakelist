About

Sumatran Dark-gray Ground Snake (Elapoidis sumatrana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
