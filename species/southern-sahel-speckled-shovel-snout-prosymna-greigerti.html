Where the name comes from

greigerti: named after a person called Greigert

Southern Sahel Speckled Shovel-snout: ‘Southern’ – where it lives (in the south); ‘Speckled’ – its speckles; ‘Shovel-snout’ – its snout is shaped like a shovel, for digging.
