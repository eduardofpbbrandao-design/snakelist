About

Savanna Lesser File Snake (Gonionotophis grantii) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Benin, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Ghana and 7 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 12 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
