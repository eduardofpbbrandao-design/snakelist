About

American Snail-eater (Dipsas articulata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Nicaragua, Panama. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Recorded in 39 places on iNaturalist.
