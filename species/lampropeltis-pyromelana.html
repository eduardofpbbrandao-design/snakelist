About

Sonoran Mountain Kingsnake (Lampropeltis pyromelana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as temperate coniferous forest, pine forest, montane woodland, mountain forest and 28 more. Common: recorded in 158 places on iNaturalist.
