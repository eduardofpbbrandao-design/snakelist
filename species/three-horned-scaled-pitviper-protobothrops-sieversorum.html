About

Three Horned-scaled Pitviper (Protobothrops sieversorum) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, indochina rainforest. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
