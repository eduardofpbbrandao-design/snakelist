About

Green Vine Snake (nasuta) (Ahaetulla nasuta) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bhutan, Cambodia, India, Malaysia, Myanmar and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 26 more. Common: recorded in 91 places on iNaturalist.
