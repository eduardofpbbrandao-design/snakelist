Diamondback Night Stalker

Toxicodryas adamanteus

Diamondback Night Stalker (Toxicodryas adamanteus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo.
