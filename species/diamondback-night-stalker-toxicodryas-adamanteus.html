About

Diamondback Night Stalker (Toxicodryas adamanteus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Republic of the Congo. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 6 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
