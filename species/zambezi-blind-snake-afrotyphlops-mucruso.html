Zambezi Blind Snake

Afrotyphlops mucruso

Zambezi Blind Snake (Afrotyphlops mucruso) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Botswana, Mozambique, South Africa, Zambia and Zimbabwe.

    Where the name comes from

    Zambezi Blind Snake: ‘Blind Snake’ – its eyes are tiny and covered by scales, so it can hardly see.
