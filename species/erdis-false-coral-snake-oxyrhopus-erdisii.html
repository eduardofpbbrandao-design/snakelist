About

Erdis' False Coral Snake (Oxyrhopus erdisii) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 8 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
