About

Raddi's Lizard-eating Snake (Elapomorphus quinquelineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Recorded in 42 places on iNaturalist.
