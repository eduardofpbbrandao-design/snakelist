About

Ijima's sea snake (Emydocephalus ijimae) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in China, Japan, Taiwan. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, subtropical forest, broadleaf forest and 8 more. Recorded in 35 places on iNaturalist.
