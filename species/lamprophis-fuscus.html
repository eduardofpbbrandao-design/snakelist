About

Yellow-bellied House Snake (Lamprophis fuscus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Eswatini, Lesotho, Mozambique, South Africa. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, coastal forest, shrubland and 9 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
