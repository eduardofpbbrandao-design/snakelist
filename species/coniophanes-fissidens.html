About

Yellowbelly Snake (Coniophanes fissidens) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 171 places on iNaturalist.
