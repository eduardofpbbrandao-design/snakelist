About

Mexican Black-headed Snake (Tantilla atriceps) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as subtropical forest, broadleaf forest, pine forest, scrub forest and 18 more. Recorded in 16 places on iNaturalist.
