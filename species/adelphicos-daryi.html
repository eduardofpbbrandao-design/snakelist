About

Dary's Burrowing Snake (Adelphicos daryi) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Guatemala. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
