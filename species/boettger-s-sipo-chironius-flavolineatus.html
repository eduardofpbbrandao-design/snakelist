About

Boettger's Sipo (Chironius flavolineatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Bolivia, Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical dry forest and 43 more. Common: recorded in 82 places on iNaturalist.
