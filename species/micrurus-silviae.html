About

Silvia coralsnake (Micrurus silviae) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, evergreen forest and 5 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
