About

Malabar Pit Viper (Craspedocephalus malabaricus) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in India. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 4 more. Common: recorded in 136 places on iNaturalist.
