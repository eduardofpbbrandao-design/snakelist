About

Common Egg Eater (Dasypeltis scabra) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Burundi and 28 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 58 more. Common: recorded in 513 places on iNaturalist.
