About

Barred Slug-eating Snake (Pareas nuchalis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Pareidae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 12 more. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
