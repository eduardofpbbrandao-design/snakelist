About

Central Sipo (maculoventris) (Chironius maculoventris) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, subtropical forest, subtropical dry forest and 15 more. Recorded in 29 places on iNaturalist.
