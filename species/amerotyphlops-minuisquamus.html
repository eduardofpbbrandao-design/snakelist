About

Basin Worm Snake (Amerotyphlops minuisquamus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Brazil, Colombia, Guyana, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 10 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
