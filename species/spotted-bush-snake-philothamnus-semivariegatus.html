About

Spotted Bush Snake (Philothamnus semivariegatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 28 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 63 more. Common: recorded in 582 places on iNaturalist.
