Spotted Bush Snake

Philothamnus semivariegatus

Spotted Bush Snake (Philothamnus semivariegatus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Angola, Benin, Botswana, Burkina Faso, Cameroon and 28 more.
