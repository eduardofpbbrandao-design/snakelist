About

Thamnodynastes phoenix is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 24 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
