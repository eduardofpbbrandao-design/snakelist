About

Southern Gracile Blind-snake (Afrotyphlops obtusus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Typhlopidae, found in Malawi, Mozambique, Zimbabwe. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 19 more. Recorded in 7 places on iNaturalist.
