Green Keelback

Rhabdophis nigrocinctus

Green Keelback (Rhabdophis nigrocinctus) is a venomous water snake of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam.

Where the name comes from

nigrocinctus: ‘black-banded’, from Latin roots: nigro- ‘black’ + -cinctus ‘banded’

Green Keelback: ‘Green’ – its colour; ‘Keelback’ – its scales have a ridge (a keel) along the middle.

Worked out from the Latin and Greek roots and the words in the name; parts SnakeList can’t explain are left out.
