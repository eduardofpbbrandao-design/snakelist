About

Elongate Quill-snouted Snake (Xenocalamus mechowii) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Angola, Botswana, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Namibia and 3 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 22 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
