About

Eastern Vine Snake (Thelotornis mossambicanus) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Kenya, Malawi, Mozambique, Somalia, Tanzania and 2 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, tropical seasonal forest and 19 more. Recorded in 68 places on iNaturalist.
