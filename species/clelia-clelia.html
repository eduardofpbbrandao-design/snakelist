About

Mussurana (clelia) (Clelia clelia) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Grenada, Guatemala and 4 more. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 192 places on iNaturalist.
