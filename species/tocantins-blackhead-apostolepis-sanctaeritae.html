About

Tocantins Blackhead (Apostolepis sanctaeritae) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Brazil. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 19 more. Recorded in 20 places on iNaturalist.
