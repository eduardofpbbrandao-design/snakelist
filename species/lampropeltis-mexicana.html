About

Mexican Kingsnake (Lampropeltis mexicana) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico. It is known to eat other snakes. It lives in habitats such as tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest, tropical dry forest and 30 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
