About

Schott's Whipsnake (Masticophis schotti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Mexico, United States. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 29 more. Common: recorded in 268 places on iNaturalist.
