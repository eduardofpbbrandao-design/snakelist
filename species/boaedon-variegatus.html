About

Dark-bellied Housesnake (Boaedon variegatus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Lamprophiidae, found in Angola, Ghana, Sierra Leone. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, evergreen forest and 9 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
