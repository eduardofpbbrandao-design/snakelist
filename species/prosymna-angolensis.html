About

Angola Shovel-snout (Prosymna angolensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Prosymnidae, found in Angola, Botswana, Malawi, Namibia, Zambia and 1 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical seasonal forest, woodland, dry woodland, open woodland and 16 more. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
