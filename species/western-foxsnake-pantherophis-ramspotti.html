About

Western Foxsnake (Pantherophis ramspotti) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in United States. It lives in habitats such as temperate deciduous forest, temperate mixed forest, broadleaf forest, forest edges and 9 more. Common: recorded in 411 places on iNaturalist.
