About

King's Seasnake (Hydrophis kingii) is a venomous sea snake of the family Elapidae, found in Australia, Papua New Guinea. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical seasonal forest and 30 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
