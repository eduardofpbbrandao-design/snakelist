About

Jonathan's Lancehead (Bothrops jonathani) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Argentina, Bolivia. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 16 more. Recorded in 11 places on iNaturalist.
