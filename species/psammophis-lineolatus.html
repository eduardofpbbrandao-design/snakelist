About

Steppe Ribbon Racer (Psammophis lineolatus) is a venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, China, Iran, Kazakhstan and 6 more. It lives in habitats such as forest edges, grassland, temperate grassland, steppe and 10 more. Common: recorded in 93 places on iNaturalist.
