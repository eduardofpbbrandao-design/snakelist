About

Cloud Forest Parrot Snake (Leptophis modestus) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 14 places on iNaturalist.
