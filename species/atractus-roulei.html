About

Roule's Ground Snake (Atractus roulei) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Ecuador, Peru. It lives in habitats such as underground burrows, loose soil, leaf litter, fallen logs. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
