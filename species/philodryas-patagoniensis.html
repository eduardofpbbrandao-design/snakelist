About

Patagonia Green Racer (Philodryas patagoniensis) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical dry forest, tropical seasonal forest and 45 more. Common: recorded in 652 places on iNaturalist.
