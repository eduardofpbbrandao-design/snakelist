About

Vietnam Water Snake (Hebius chapaensis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in China, Laos, Vietnam. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest and 24 more. Recorded in 4 places on iNaturalist.
