About

Boie's Mud Snake (Hypsiscopus plumbea) is a non-venomous water snake of the family Homalopsidae, found in Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Laos and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 41 more. Rare: no confirmed iNaturalist sightings.
