About

Keeled Rat Snake (Ptyas carinata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia and 5 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 36 more. Common: recorded in 94 places on iNaturalist.
