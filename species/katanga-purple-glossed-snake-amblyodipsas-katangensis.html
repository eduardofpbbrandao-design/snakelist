About

Katanga Purple-glossed Snake (Amblyodipsas katangensis) is a venomous snake of the family Atractaspididae, found in Democratic Republic of the Congo, Malawi, Tanzania, Zambia. Rare: recorded in only 2 places on iNaturalist.
