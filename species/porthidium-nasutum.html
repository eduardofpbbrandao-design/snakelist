About

Hognosed Pit Viper (Porthidium nasutum) is a venomous pit viper of the family Viperidae, found in Belize, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Guatemala and 4 more. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 15 more. Common: recorded in 167 places on iNaturalist.
