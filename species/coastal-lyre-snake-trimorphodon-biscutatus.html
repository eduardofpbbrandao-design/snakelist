Coastal Lyre Snake

Trimorphodon biscutatus

Coastal Lyre Snake (Trimorphodon biscutatus) is a mildly venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Costa Rica, Guatemala, Honduras, Mexico, Nicaragua and United States.

Where the name comes from

biscutatus: ‘two-shielded’, from Latin roots: bi- ‘two’ + -scutatus ‘shielded’

Coastal Lyre Snake: ‘Coastal’ – it lives near the coast; ‘Lyre Snake’ – it has a lyre-shaped mark on its head.
