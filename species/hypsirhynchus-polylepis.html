About

Jamaican Long-tailed Groundsnake (Hypsirhynchus polylepis) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Jamaica. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, evergreen forest, caribbean moist forest. Recorded in 5 places on iNaturalist.
