About

Spotted Whipsnake (Ahaetulla fasciolata) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 13 more. Recorded in 38 places on iNaturalist.
