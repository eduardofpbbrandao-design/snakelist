About

Pink Snake (Cryptophis incredibilis) is a venomous elapid of the family Elapidae, found in Australia. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical seasonal forest, subtropical forest and 19 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
