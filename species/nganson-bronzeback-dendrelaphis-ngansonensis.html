Nganson Bronzeback

Dendrelaphis ngansonensis

Nganson Bronzeback (Dendrelaphis ngansonensis) is a non-venomous snake of the family Colubridae, found in Cambodia, China, Laos, Thailand and Vietnam.

Where the name comes from

ngansonensis: ‘from Ngân Sơn (Vietnam)’ (the ending -ensis means ‘from’ a place)

Nganson Bronzeback: ‘Bronzeback’ – it has a bronze-coloured back.
