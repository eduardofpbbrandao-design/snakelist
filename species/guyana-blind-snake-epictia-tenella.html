Guyana Blind Snake

Epictia tenella

Guyana Blind Snake (Epictia tenella) is a non-venomous snake of the family Leptotyphlopidae, found in Brazil, French Guiana, Guyana, Suriname, Trinidad and Tobago and Venezuela.
