About

Peru Slender Snake (Tachymenis peruviana) is a venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Argentina, Bolivia, Chile, Peru. It lives in habitats such as tropical moist forest, tropical montane forest, montane rainforest, cloud forest and 12 more. Recorded in 71 places on iNaturalist.
