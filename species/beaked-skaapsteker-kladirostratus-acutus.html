About

Beaked Skaapsteker (Kladirostratus acutus) is a non-venomous snake of the family Psammophiidae, found in Angola, Benin, Burundi, Cameroon, Democratic Republic of the Congo and 8 more. Rare: recorded in only 1 place on iNaturalist.
