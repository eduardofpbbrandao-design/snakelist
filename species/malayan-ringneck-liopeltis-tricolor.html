About

Malayan Ringneck (Liopeltis tricolor) is a non-venomous colubrid of the family Colubridae, found in Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand. It lives in habitats such as tropical rainforest, tropical moist forest, tropical lowland forest, tropical montane forest and 20 more. Recorded in 30 places on iNaturalist.
